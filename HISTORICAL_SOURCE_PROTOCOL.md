## 1. Research and confirm the previous games online

1. Resolve the exact sport, competition, season/stage, participants and aliases, endpoint and information horizon. Start with the actual league/organizer/federation's results, original match sheets/scorecards and both sides' dated results. Open underlying pages or PDFs; snippets and AI summaries locate sources but do not establish facts.
2. For each team or player, seek the latest 20 eligible completed competitive games before the horizon. List each game's date, opponent, venue/home-away where applicable, native event ID or stable surrogate, final status, score and endpoint, source URL and available/retrieved times. Derive L5, L10, L15 and L20 from this one deduplicated list, newest first. They overlap and count once. Also research season-to-date and the separate exact-competition population needed for baseline/model fitting; 20 team games do not constitute league training coverage.
3. Seek the responsible field owner first. If unavailable or incomplete, open permitted structured providers, team archives, accredited local reports and original-language federation/tournament material. SOURCES is a starting map, not a closed allowlist. Search aliases, local script, date, round and opponent for obscure coverage. Never substitute another gender, age, format or better-known league.
4. Verify every material historical field against a second opened source under RESEARCH_EXHAUSTIVENESS_PROTOCOL, including uncontested official records; missing fields require additional routes. Ambiguous identity, incomplete coverage, conflicts and suspicious defaults require further resolving evidence. Trace collector lineage; mirrors of one feed are one collector. An authoritative record is the primary observation, not a waiver of the corroborating-source search. Missing corroboration remains an open requirement; do not claim independent confirmation from copied feeds. Preserve conflicting values and resolve with exact-game field-owner evidence; never average scores. State independence UNKNOWN or unavailable where that is the evidence.
5. Keep true n for every window. If only 8 eligible games exist, L5 has n=5 and L10/L15/L20 each n=8; do not invent games. Distinguish a genuinely short season/career from incomplete collection. State excluded games/reasons, date coverage, source failures, unresolved fields, impact and next search. Only actually exhausted applicable searches justify HISTORICAL_DATA_UNAVAILABLE, INCOMPLETE or CONFLICTING. One unavailable metric does not suppress the rest.
6. Apply [form recipe R2](ARCHIVE_USE_GUIDE.md#recipe-r2-form-windows) and CARD_AND_LOG_TEMPLATES §3b to sourced rows. Preserve cross-season continuity and existing numerical policy; do not insert unsourced adjustments or retired league numbers.

## 2. Sport-specific history and confirmed participants

Every row requires actual-competition online history and both sides' exact-game player research under [ROSTER_AVAILABILITY_PROTOCOL](ROSTER_AVAILABILITY_PROTOCOL.md). Capture names, selected role/status, source and time separately from medical/disciplinary availability. A roster, probable starter, projection or postgame participation flag is not pregame confirmation. Recheck the actual release window and immediately before issue, with the existing information horizon and truthful late-source/issue labels.

| Sport | History/endpoint checks | Players to seek and confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Basketball | Box scores, regulation/OT and period length; scored/conceded/margin | Starting five, active game roster, bench, injuries and late scratches |
| Cricket | Same format/innings role; runs/wickets/legal balls, all-out/chase/shortened/declaration/method | Playing XI, toss when published, keeper/captain, substitute/impact-player categories; batting/bowling roles only when evidenced |
| Baseball | Native ID/doubleheader number; regulation/extras/ties and final convention | Confirmed pitcher, batting order/fielding lineup, bench/bullpen availability and scratches; probable remains probable |
| Soccer | Regulation versus extra time/penalties, awarded/abandoned games; sourced half/corner fields | Starting XI, goalkeeper, substitutes, suspensions and late replacements |
| Ice hockey | Regulation/OT/shootout and official score convention | Starting goalie, selected skaters/lines, bench, scratches and late changes |
| Tennis | Singles/doubles, surface, best-of/tiebreak; retirements/walkovers separate | Exact players/pair, draw/order of play, withdrawals/replacements and reported fitness; no invented bench |
| Australian rules | Actual national/state/local competition; goals/behinds/points, duration and finals/draws | Final selected side, substitutes/interchange, emergencies versus activated players and late changes |
| Rugby league | Actual league; regulation/golden point/finals; no union substitution | Starting side, interchange, reserves versus activated players, eligibility and withdrawals |

| Field hockey / other | Actual federation/format; shootout versus match score | Exact participants, starters and replacements where applicable, withdrawals and absences |

## 3. Source custody, missingness and time

For accepted event/field values retain URL, native/local identity, existing canonical references and aliases, exact value or faithful extract, source publication/update time when known, first defensible available_at, retrieval time with offset, collector lineage (UNKNOWN if unknown), endpoint/final status and capture identity/hash. Raw captures/helpers remain outside the repository; permitted market-free extracts/normalized CSV stay in the named mini/primary evidence scope. Classify OBSERVED, DERIVED, MISSING and CONFLICTING by field. Reconcile reschedules, aliases and doubleheaders. No guessed dates, times, period scores, OT flags, zero attendance or healthy-player assumptions.

Event date, availability and retrieval differ. Today's final score can support a future fit, but not an earlier historical refit without known-at evidence. Unknown historical publication uses the first defensible observed time, never a guessed timestamp. Later lineup pages and postgame gamebooks cannot prove pregame selection. Admit only horizon-compatible evidence under TIMING_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL; exclude target live/final outcomes and later corrections from pre-start features, and record exposure. No odds, tips, betting previews, fantasy or market inputs enter normalized research.

Match the exact implemented sport/competition and supported endpoint/format. Require RESEARCH_INPUT_REVIEWED, HTTPS sporting evidence, its hash and horizon-compatible available_at. The legacy field `archive_disposition=MISSING` denotes the retired store; explain retirement in reason. INCOMPLETE/INCORRECT remain accepted compatibility values. No borrowed major-league constants or unsupported engines. Non-cricket overs_cap is blank. HISTORY source_kind is explicit WEB_RECOVERED or WEB_CORRECTED for new web inputs; legacy ARCHIVE_REVIEWED stays readable for old receipts only.

Freeze metadata/history/evidence/config snapshots and verify hashes. Discovery over the removed store is not a prerequisite. Models/reports derived from it remain historical, without automatic admission to new forecasts. Missing/failed/unsupported models retain complete research and the stable local event key under RESEARCH_RETENTION_PROTOCOL, using RESEARCH_ONLY_NO_MODEL without invented probabilities or receipts.
