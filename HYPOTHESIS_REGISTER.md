## How a hypothesis is tested without code

1. **Pre-register in this file** before the cohort starts: the hypothesis, the baseline, the candidate method, the cohort rule, the number of rows and weeks, the effect size and the acceptance rule. Do not edit it afterwards; add a dated line.
2. **Record the method on each card** (the card's distribution object and adjustments table). The cohort is every card that meets the cohort rule after the registration date.
3. **Score by hand** at the retrospective (prompt 6): for each row, Brier = (p - outcome)^2; the candidate's mean minus the baseline's mean; group by ISO week; take the mean and standard error across weeks; the 95% interval is the mean plus or minus 1.96 standard errors.
4. **Accept** only if the interval's upper bound is below 0 for Brier and log loss, the calibration slope's interval contains 1, and at least 8 week-blocks are in the sample.
5. **Report honestly** if the cohort is infeasible. Rows and weeks below are computed at 20 cards a week, power 0.8 and one-sided alpha 0.025; "not feasible" means more than 26 weeks.
