## 1. Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CANDIDATE | Observed in one or more cases; no prospective evidence yet |
| TESTING | A prospective process check is active; forecast-weight promotion additionally requires the complete v2 test manifest |
| PROMOTED_PROCESS | Required because it protects identity, data quality, coherence, or leakage; not a claim of predictive lift |
| PROMOTED_FORECAST | Prospective chronological evidence supports a forecast-weight change |
| RETIRED | Tested and removed or narrowed |
| SUPERSEDED | Replaced because it was incorrect, contradictory, or redundant |

A five-case total is an exploratory checkpoint, not statistical validation. No test below currently qualifies as a promoted forecast-weight rule.

## 5. Update procedure

1. A retrospective proposes a candidate with a stable ID; it does not change weights immediately.
2. Before the next eligible case, complete the v2 test manifest: population, independent unit, frozen champion/challenger, exact delta, primary proper score, guardrails, review horizon, uncertainty, promotion rule, and falsification criterion.
3. Mark eligibility and the forecast-time procedure before the result is known.
4. After settlement, update the count and score difference without selecting only favourable cases.
5. Promote, narrow, or retire at a scheduled review. A process-compliance count cannot promote a numerical weight. Record the method version and effective date.
6. Emergency corrections for identity, contract, arithmetic, source transformation, settlement, or leakage bypass forecast-weight testing because they repair validity rather than fit outcomes.
