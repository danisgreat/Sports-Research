## 1. What actually generates the primary rows

For each new game, complete independent sporting source/availability/contract research and declare the candidate recipe before pricing. Run the [primary runtime](ml/primary.py) against source-reviewed, hash-custodied, horizon-safe normalized history. It fits two competition-specific team-rating models with different predeclared decay windows (`RATINGS_FAST`, half-life 90 days; `RATINGS_SLOW`, 365 days) and a league-only comparator (`LEAGUE_BASELINE`). **PRIMARY_HEDGE_MIX_V2** combines the three complete score grids into ONE primary distribution, their exponentially weighted mixture; primary p_card, W/P/L, expected score and original ranks come from that mixed grid. A mixture of joint score grids is itself a joint score grid, so winner, handicap, total and push masses stay coherent. The baseline remains a separate same-row comparator. This is **MODEL_ASSISTED_PRIMARY_V2 / EXPERIMENTAL_PRIMARY_NOT_CERTIFIED**, not manual-only analysis or a qualified production model.

The learning step is the RL integration, in its full-information form (exponential weights / Hedge): actions are forecasting experts, not stakes or bets, and every expert's loss is observed after every final, so nothing is selected at random and no importance weighting is needed. Weight of expert *i* ∝ exp(−η·(L_i − min L)), where L_i is its summed loss on common questions: 0.5·[(P(home win) − y)² + (P(over the league line) − y)²], with the over-line term only for cricket. The league line is the median total of the admitted training games. L comes from two sources, both known by the information horizon (§2): (a) a warm start: weekly rolling refits over the last 30 game-weeks of admitted history, however far back an off-season puts them; and (b) settled earlier primary bundles of the same competition/endpoint, whose three frozen expert grids are re-scored from the evidence final. The context is policy | competition | endpoint; expert losses on common questions are comparable across recipes, coverage and timing. η comes from the competition's row in the [policy replay](research/model_shadow/PRIMARY_POLICY_REPLAY/RESULTS.md), tuned on earlier seasons only. Any competition without a row uses η = 2.0, receipt-labelled `DEFAULT_NO_REPLAY_ROW`. The runtime is deterministic; no seed exists to search. [Algorithm reference](https://doi.org/10.1006/jcss.1997.1504).

**Why v2 replaced v1 (PRIMARY_EXP3_V1).** v1 drew a single expert at random with a delayed Exp3 bandit and published that expert's grid. That is a bandit solution to a problem that is not a bandit: all experts' losses are observable. Brier and log loss are convex, so the weighted mixture's score is never worse than the expected score of a random draw with the same weights (Jensen). On the runtime's own experts, a 41,752-game, 28-competition walk-forward replay found pooled home-win Brier HEDGE_MIX − EXP3_SELECT = −0.0081 [−0.0087, −0.0074]. It was below zero in 23 competitions; the other five intervals spanned zero, and none were above. Exp3 still priced 15–34% of cards from the team-blind baseline; the mixture gave that expert about 0 weight. Against the best single expert chosen in hindsight, the mixture was level everywhere except one DATA_HOLD league (Greece, +0.0007). **Limitation:** with the replay-tuned η up to 50 and hundreds of warm games, weights are often close to one-hot (follow-the-leader). The gain is mostly from never drawing a worse expert, not from diversification. This is historical development evidence, not prospective skill.

## 2. Information horizon, runtime and input contract

**There is no separate cutoff.** The information horizon is the game's scheduled (expected) start as recorded in the game log for that event (the mini/card's Scheduled start). Research of lineups, confirmed starters, bench, injuries, suspensions, team news, weather and any other sporting information continues up to that time, and anything published by then is admissible. The horizon moves earlier only when facts force it: the forecast was issued earlier (`issue_at`; nothing published after issue can have been used), or play verifiably began earlier than scheduled (`actual_start`). The runtime takes the earliest of the three, records which one set it (`horizon_source`), and refuses a request that carries a `cutoff` column. If the official start is rescheduled before play, the game log's updated scheduled start, with its source, is the horizon; a delay announced after the scheduled start does not extend it. Information first published after the horizon (a late lineup change, a live score) never enters the primary rows; record it separately under [timing rules](TIMING_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md).

Use the local checked-out version matching the card's authority receipt:

```text
python -m ml primary predict --request REQUEST.csv --history HISTORY.csv --contracts CONTRACTS.csv --out research/primary_predictions/EVENT_KEY
python -m ml primary predict --request REQUEST.csv --history HISTORY.csv --contracts CONTRACTS.csv --feedback EFFECTIVE_FEEDBACK.csv --out research/primary_predictions/NEXT_EVENT_KEY
python -m ml primary settle --bundle research/primary_predictions/EVENT_KEY --home-score HOME --away-score AWAY --final-at ISO_TIME --available-at ISO_TIME --evidence SOURCE_CAPTURE --evidence-sha256 SHA256 --out NEW_FEEDBACK_ROW.csv
```

Paths are explicit. A new immutable event/revision folder is required; reruns cannot overwrite issued bundles. Raw sporting captures stay outside the repository; admitted normalized market-free CSV and Markdown output may live only in the named primary folder or the named mini evidence scope. No canonical log/ID is written by the runtime. A remote/read-only agent without execution access must obtain a real bundle, or retain the research as NO_FORECAST / ML_NOT_RUN; never invent a model run, weights, probabilities or a learning update. An explicitly requested manual comparison stays separate, not silently substituted as the default primary.

REQUEST.csv has one row, columns `event_id,competition,home,away,scheduled_start,issue_at,actual_start,endpoint,outcome_exposure,candidate_recipe`, optional `venue`. `scheduled_start` is copied from the game log, with its zone. Competition is the exact discovered archive path. All times have explicit zones. `outcome_exposure=NONE_RECORDED` is an operator declaration, not independently audited blindness. `actual_start` may be blank; timing then stays unverified. Issue is a declared timestamp; generated_at is recorded independently. A local timestamp/receipt cannot prove external pre-start publication. Never backdate an actual card to the scheduled start.

HISTORY.csv columns: `event_id,competition,endpoint,date,final_at,available_at,status,admission,source_url,evidence_path,evidence_sha256,season,home,away,hs,as_,reg_hs,reg_as,decision`; optional `stage`. One terminal event per row, no target or duplicate IDs. `status=FINAL`, `admission=RESEARCH_INPUT_REVIEWED` requires an actual human/source-field review; it is not an automatic owner-certification label. Every referenced capture must exist and match its measured SHA. Date <= final <= available <= information horizon. Date-only/current-day uncertainty is handled conservatively: training games on the horizon's UTC date are excluded. Exact score endpoints/participants/source field meanings must be reviewed. Source hash comparison proves custody, not truth; retrospective archive dates alone are insufficient. No arbitrary extra/market columns accepted.

Cricket additionally requires `bat,bowl,venue,runs,legal_balls,wickets`, with full scheduled legal balls or all-out. Home/away coordinates represent batting/bowling teams and the full first-innings endpoint; settle as `(innings_runs,0)`. Never fit a full-innings model to shortened innings or derive a powerplay/second-innings/match-winner forecast from it.

CONTRACTS.csv columns: `contract_key,family,side,line,endpoint,definition,action`. Families `winner`, `handicap`, `total`; side HOME/AWAY/DRAW or OVER/UNDER. Winner action is explicitly THREE_WAY_DRAW_LOSES or DRAW_PUSH; other families require STANDARD_COMPLETED. Definition FROZEN_FRAMEWORK_CONVENTION is conditional sporting action, not verified operator terms. Record all supplied/derived candidate dispositions; semantic duplicates fail. Values above the selection ceiling stay diagnostic, never clipped. Existing family/gate/precision/unsupported-line checks still apply; the operator checks the complete candidate universe and prints the gate and joint, not a fabricated PASS. The runtime enforces the baseball +1.5 Rank1 floor but does not invent unavailable period models.

## 3. Scope, availability and actual use

Supported engines: fitted joint-normal team ratings for supported points sports, Dixon-Coles for soccer regulation, count ratings for supported hockey/baseball endpoints, limited-overs cricket full first innings. The process is generic: every discovered competition folder in every sport, not only the major leagues, runs through the same admission, fitting, mixture and feedback steps under its own exact endpoint/tie configuration. Regulation scoring is fitted before adding extras once. The raw input must supply reviewed regulation/final fields. No generic NBA/NHL/MLB substitution for another league. Config DATA_HOLD warnings, unsupported engines, unknown participants, bad fit and wrong endpoint block numerical primary output. Tennis, field hockey, multi-day cricket, powerplays/player/period rows have no matching primary runtime here; research is retained with the exact NOT_IMPLEMENTED/NO_FORECAST reason, never falsely called ML-integrated. New adapters require their own tests and source contract.

For cricket, request home=batting side and away=bowling side; training home=bat, away=bowl, hs=runs, as_=0 (auxiliary), with integer legal balls/wickets within format limits. Cricket form means separately count batting runs and first-innings runs conceded; no chase score is inferred from the auxiliary zero. Both-side L5/L10/L15/L20 tables are generated with true n; shorter windows stay short. Ratings learn from prior team identity/scores/time decay. Current player availability, injuries, tactical changes and weather are mandatory sporting evidence, gathered up to the scheduled start, but are **not fitted features of this primary runtime**. Record their coverage and model limitation; do not claim the model quantified a lineup effect, or hand-edit p_card to imply it did. Material unsupported lineup/format regimes must be retained as qualitative/NO_FORECAST or trigger a separately versioned supported scenario model. Do not count correlated form windows repeatedly or append subjective model adjustments silently.

The game-log scheduled start determines admissible evidence, not a compulsory countdown. [Timing rules](TIMING_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md) still permit research and issue after start with truthful input/issue separation; observed live/final outcomes block this full-event unexposed primary lane. Late-issued clean-input research is separate from genuinely pre-start-published prospective models. Old independently frozen shadow comparisons retain their own stricter timing requirements; no model independence is claimed for the new assisted primary cards.

## 4. Immutable model and feedback receipts

Bundle: FORECAST.md, PREDICTIONS.csv, GRIDS.csv (all three expert score distributions), FORMS.csv, HISTORY.csv, CONTRACTS.csv, REQUEST.csv, FEEDBACK_INPUT.csv, MODEL_CONFIG.csv and RECEIPT.csv. The receipt retains event/scope/endpoint, `selected_arm=HEDGE_MIX`, mixture weights, the cumulative expert losses and η that produced them (η source and replay-table SHA-256), warm-start game count, feedback event count, league line, information horizon and its source, scheduled start, context, actual source-code-tree/input/output hashes, repository HEAD, library versions, exact discovered League config snapshot, fixed expert hyperparameter recipe, generated time and declared issue, primary status and expected score moments. Verification recomputes the weights from the recorded losses and η, re-mixes the saved expert grids, and checks frozen W/P/L, p_decisive and baseline_p against them; any difference rejects the bundle. Use PREDICTIONS directly as the A4/A5 numerical source; do not copy arbitrary old development predictions into a new game.

Settlement still researches official terminal fields and completes original R1-R12 first. Then `primary settle` verifies the exact original bundle and scores all three frozen experts on the common questions (`loss_RATINGS_FAST`, `loss_RATINGS_SLOW`, `loss_LEAGUE_BASELINE`). It records the issued top-two Brier as a diagnostic (`NOT_COMPUTED` when every primary row pushed; no invented zero), and writes a new-only feedback row with the final/source evidence hash. A feedback row is admitted only if re-scoring the frozen grids from its evidence final reproduces every recorded loss. Source custody is not source-truth verification. Reconcile each canonical effective settlement revision and operator/definition before admitting a feedback row; preserve prior rows in the audit package and build a new effective-feedback inventory. Duplicate event feedback/branch conflicts block replay, never last-row-wins. Never update on pending events, target-event labels, feedback published after the horizon, or future outcomes.

Keep historical manual, MODEL_ASSISTED_PRIMARY_V2, model-shadow and staking/decision-lab cohorts separate. Only issued primary top-two rows count in their own T2 cohort; the experts are comparators, not additional wins. Monitor same-row baseline-relative Brier/log loss, event/week dependence, calibration, actual starts, source coverage and field/endpoint errors. New training or policy versions create new receipts/context versions; preserve old issued forecasts. No practical-effect or sample-size acceptance is invented, and no production/performance certification follows from passing tests or the historical replay.

## 5. Separation from the market decision lab

The older `ml/decision/` staking/CLV experiment remains outside primary predictions. No odds, stake amounts, returns, fantasy, market moves, CLV or decision-lab feedback enter the primary runtime, its expert weights, features, ranks or learning. The mixture learns only from sporting prediction errors. Owner authorisation changes the primary ML reading/execution gate; it does not remove source integrity, known-at checks, immutable originals or this market firewall.

**Primary pricing precedence:** this named runtime supersedes the former mandatory manual low/base/high mixture and hand form-centre adjustments for new primary cards. Its mixed complete grid is the only primary probability source. Manual scenarios remain explicitly requested comparisons or qualitative sensitivity; they are not an unimplemented fitted availability model. Unsupported material mechanisms block numerical primary issuance.
