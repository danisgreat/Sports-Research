## Daily sports-only research (optional, by hand)

> Read CURRENT_STATE, METHOD, CURRENT_RULES and SOURCES. For the day's fixtures in the leagues the user names, list each fixture with its official time and the state the source shows (scheduled, live, final), record every source failure with the time and reason, and note lineups and starters as they are announced. Do not issue a P-number, do not build a card, and do not claim a live pilot. Report source freshness and which fixtures have no usable source.

## Reconcile a mini against the Combined Log

> Read CURRENT_STATE, GAME_LOG_STATUS_CURRENT and the active Combined Log header. For every entry in the mini, find its event key and ID in the logs. Exact existing event, ID and card lines keep their canonical ID and consume none; changed content becomes a dated addendum under the original ID. Report conflicts and collisions without renumbering. Delete a redundant working mini only after confirming every card, addendum and settlement is in the log (compare the pick tables cell by cell) and the user has agreed.
