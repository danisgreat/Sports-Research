# AFL Historical Players, Rosters & Champions Registry (1900–2025)

An authoritative season-by-season chronicle of participating clubs, premiership teams, Grand Finalists, Brownlow Medalists, Coleman/Leading Goalkickers, and marquee players across 126 years of Australian rules football history.

- **Era:** World War I Curtailment. Due to the Great War, only 4 clubs competed in 1916 (Carlton, Collingwood, Fitzroy, Richmond) and 6 in 1917. Fitzroy won the 1916 premiership despite finishing last on the regular-season ladder.
- **Premiers:** Fitzroy
- **Notable Players:** Dick Lee (Collingwood), Percy Sheehan (Fitzroy), Cliff Rankin (Geelong).
