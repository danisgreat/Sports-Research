# AFLW — Historical Players, Rosters & Champions Register (1900–2025)

An authoritative, comprehensive season-by-season chronicle of the AFL Women's (AFLW) competition from foundation to the present day, detailing participating clubs, expansion milestones, premiership teams, Grand Final results, winning coaches and captains, AFLW Best & Fairest medalists, leading goalkickers, Grand Final Best on Ground medalists, and match officiating appointments.

---

## Historical Timeline & Status (1900–2025)

- **Pre-Founding Era (1900 to 2016):**
  - **Status:** `NOT_YET_FOUNDED`
  - **Context:** The AFL Women's competition had not yet been formally established. Prior to 2017, women's Australian rules football was contested at state and community levels (such as the VWFL in Victoria and WAWFL in Western Australia), alongside exhibition games organized by the AFL between Melbourne and the Western Bulldogs (2013–2016). No official national league, franchise contracts, or sanctioned premiership records existed prior to the launch of AFLW in February 2017.

- **Active Championship Era (2017 to Present):**
  - **2017–2018 (Foundation 8-Club Era):** Adelaide Crows, Brisbane Lions, Carlton, Collingwood, Fremantle, GWS GIANTS, Melbourne, Western Bulldogs.
  - **2019 (10-Club Era):** Geelong Cats and North Melbourne admitted to the competition.
  - **2020–2022 S6 (14-Club Era):** Gold Coast SUNS, Richmond, St Kilda, and West Coast Eagles admitted.
  - **2022 S7–Present (Full 18-Club AFL National Era):** Essendon, Hawthorn, Port Adelaide, and Sydney Swans admitted, completing national representation across all 18 AFL clubs.

- **Era:** Foundation 8-Club Era

- **Grand Final Result:** Adelaide Crows 4.11 (35) def. Brisbane Lions 4.5 (29) (Winning Margin: 6 points)
- **Match Date & Venue:** 2017-03-25 | Metricon Stadium (Carrara), Gold Coast
- **Official Attendance:** 15,610 spectators
- **Grand Final Best on Ground Medalist:** Erin Phillips (Adelaide Crows)

- **Leading Goalkicker:** Darcy Vescio (Carlton, 14 goals)

- **Key Marquee Players & Pioneers:** Erin Phillips, Chelsea Randall, Darcy Vescio, Daisy Pearce, Tayla Harris, Emma Kearney, Karen Paxman, Ellie Blackburn, Sabrina Frederick.
