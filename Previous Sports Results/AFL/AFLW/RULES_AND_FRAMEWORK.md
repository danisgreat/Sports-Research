# AFLW premiership — rules and competition procedures

**Scope:** women's AFL competition. **Checked:** 30 September 2026. AFLW is a distinct competition; do not import men's match duration, player count, interchange, ladder/finals format or statistical baselines. Historical yearly results require that season's version.

## Complete controlling documents and precedence

1. [AFLW Competition Rules, issued 11 June 2025, 40-page PDF](https://resources.afl.com.au/afl/document/2025/08/21/549bb3bb-0466-4b69-982e-38d4f03ca3aa/AFLW-Competition-Rules-effective-11-June-2025-1.pdf), the version linked from the [AFL policies index](https://www.afl.com.au/policies) at this check. Sections 1–2 define the competition; 3–7 lists, signing, draft and replacement players; 8–10 contracts, access and compliance; 11–13 AFLW playing and match-day variations, personnel, reporting and review; 14–18 apparel, licensing, media, soft cap and precedence. Appendix B is the Final Eight and additional-time procedure. The document points to separate CBA, medical, match-day and commercial instruments. Follow any later AFL-issued variation for the relevant season.
2. [2026 Laws of Australian Football](https://resources.afl.com.au/afl/document/2026/02/13/8676d880-481a-4211-a479-305f138ce8b6/Laws-of-Australian-Football-Final-13-February-2026-.pdf) and [AFL Regulations](https://resources.afl.com.au/afl/document/2026/02/13/54c158af-15e9-483b-a195-62a0f4e33b11/AFL-Regulations-Final-11-February-2026-.pdf) apply where AFLW competition rules do not vary them. Do not overwrite a specific AFLW rule with the men's regulation.
3. [Four 2026 AFLW law and interpretation changes](https://www.afl.com.au/aflw/news/1479703/changes-introduced-for-2026-nab-aflw-season): rucks may not cross the centre-circle line to engage before contest; kick-in time aligns to eight seconds; a shrug in a tackle is prior opportunity; a player in the five-metre protected area must stand. AFLW already used last-disposal out of bounds and did not wait for a nominated ruck. This AFL-issued 2026 notice supplements the linked 2025 PDF.

## Playing conditions

- The published AFLW competition rules specify 16 on the playing surface and five interchange players (s. 11.1), a size 4 football and a 60-interchange cap (s. 11.2). Check the original clauses for exceptions, team sheets and cap accounting.
- Match time is stated in s. 11.1 as 68 scheduled minutes: four quarters with 15 minutes plus time-on for goals or major injuries, and a further two minutes per quarter with broader time-on for stoppages. Quarter intervals are six, fourteen and six minutes. This is materially different from men's 20-minute quarters.
- Five players start in each 50-metre arc under the 5-6-5 arrangement, subject to s. 11.1's full conditions. AFLW's last kick or handball out of bounds between the 50-metre arcs gives the opponent a free kick; other out-of-bounds restarts and the boundary throw-in position vary by zone. See s. 11.1 and the Laws for deliberate/out-on-the-full and other exceptions.
- Goal = six points; behind = one. Home-and-away draws stand. For marks, disposals, tackles, free kicks, advantage, scoring reviews, equipment and conduct, consult the Laws as modified by AFLW rules. Do not assume a men's rule update was adopted without an AFLW notice.

## Ladder, finals, lists and match administration

AFLW uses a single ladder. The published s. 2.1 orders clubs by premiership points, percentage, result between tied clubs if they played, percentage in those games, then coin toss. The top eight qualify for the Final Eight under s. 2.2 and Appendix B. The bracket ends with the two preliminary-final winners in the Grand Final; the Appendix defines the designated home club. Check the current season fixture for exact number of home-and-away games rather than carrying an old round count forward.

A tied AFLW final goes to a 6-minute-30-second rest, two periods of three minutes plus time-on with a change of ends, and further pairs until there is a winner (Appendix B). Appendix B also specifies who may access the field and the timing procedure. AFLW team-list announcements, late changes, match-day access, runners and interchange are in s. 11–12. List size, primary/rookie places, draft, trade, inactive and top-up eligibility are in s. 3–7; use those clauses instead of assuming every listed player is eligible for a given match.

For historical results, verify season and official match ID, fixture/venue, final status, goals and behinds, and any additional-time or interrupted-match ruling in AFL match centre. Record the rule version used and keep AFLW separate from men's AFL.

**Annual audit:** check the policies index, AFLW competition rules, 2026-plus notices, fixture, CBA and AFLW tribunal/medical documents before each AFLW season. The previously stated fixed July reminder and 10–12-round range are not rules.
