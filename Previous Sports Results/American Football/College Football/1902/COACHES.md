- **Sport:** American Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Michigan
- **Winning Head Coach:** Fielding Yost
- **Championship Opponent:** Stanford
- **Opposing Head Coach:** C.M. Fickert
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Inaugural Tournament East-West Game (Rose Bowl)
- **Game Result:** Michigan 49 def. Stanford 0
- **Venue:** Tournament Park (Pasadena, CA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1902-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
Yost's Point-a-Minute offense overwhelmed Stanford with Neil Snow scoring five rushing touchdowns behind precision wedge blocking. The season reached its climax in the Inaugural Tournament East-West Game (Rose Bowl), where Fielding Yost's Michigan squad secured national championship honors over C.M. Fickert's Stanford.
