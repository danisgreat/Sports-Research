## Tactical & Season Overview
First season following President Theodore Roosevelt's safety conference, legalizing the forward pass and increasing first down distance to 10 yards. The season reached its climax in the Big Three Championship Decider, where Bill Roper's Princeton squad secured national championship honors over Foster Rockwell's Yale.
