- **Sport:** American Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** LSU
- **Winning Head Coach:** Edgar Wingard
- **Championship Opponent:** Arkansas
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Hugo Bezdek
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Southern Intercollegiate Championship Decider
- **Game Result:** LSU 36 def. Arkansas 4
- **Venue:** State Fairgrounds (Little Rock, AR)
- **Official Match Date:** 1908-11-26

## Tactical & Season Overview
Edgar Wingard deployed Doc Fenton's revolutionary triple-threat playmaking (passing, rushing, placekicking) across the South. The season reached its climax in the Southern Intercollegiate Championship Decider, where Edgar Wingard's LSU squad secured national championship honors over Hugo Bezdek's Arkansas.
