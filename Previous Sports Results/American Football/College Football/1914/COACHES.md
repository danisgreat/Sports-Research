## Tactical & Season Overview
Charles Daly incorporated Elmer Oliphant's bruising downhill ball-carrying behind an unyielding cadet defensive front. The season reached its climax in the Army-Navy Game (National Decider), where Charles Daly's Army squad secured national championship honors over Douglas Howard's Navy.
