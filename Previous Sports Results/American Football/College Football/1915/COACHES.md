## Tactical & Season Overview
Al Sharpe implemented a disciplined power-shift system led by All-American quarterback Charles Barrett. The season reached its climax in the Thanksgiving Championship Decider, where Al Sharpe's Cornell squad secured national championship honors over George Brooke's Penn.
