- **Sport:** American Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Pittsburgh
- **Winning Head Coach:** Pop Warner
- **Championship Opponent:** Penn State
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Dick Harlow
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Thanksgiving Decider
- **Game Result:** Pittsburgh 31 def. Penn State 0
- **Venue:** Forbes Field (Pittsburgh, PA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1916-11-30

## Tactical & Season Overview
Pop Warner introduced his revolutionary Single-Wing offense, confusing opponents with unbalanced lines and direct snaps. The season reached its climax in the Thanksgiving Decider, where Pop Warner's Pittsburgh squad secured national championship honors over Dick Harlow's Penn State.
