## Tactical & Season Overview
Heisman's famous 'Jump Shift' offense baffled opponents, shifting backs immediately before the snap to create perimeter mismatches. The season reached its climax in the Thanksgiving Championship Decider, where John Heisman's Georgia Tech squad secured national championship honors over Mike Donahue's Auburn.
