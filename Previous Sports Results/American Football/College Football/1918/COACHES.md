## Tactical & Season Overview
Yost adapted his gameplan for wartime student Army training corps rosters, relying on rugged defense and field position punting. The season reached its climax in the War Era Decider, where Fielding Yost's Michigan squad secured national championship honors over John Wilce's Ohio State.
