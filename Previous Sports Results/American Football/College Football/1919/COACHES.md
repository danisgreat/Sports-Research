## Tactical & Season Overview
Bob Fisher's Crimson relied on Eddie Casey's open-field agility and stellar goal-line stand defense to hold off Oregon. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where Bob Fisher's Harvard squad secured national championship honors over Shy Huntington's Oregon.
