# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1924 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1924

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Notre Dame
- **Winning Head Coach:** Knute Rockne
- **Championship Opponent:** Stanford
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Pop Warner
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Rose Bowl Game
- **Game Result:** Notre Dame 27 def. Stanford 10
- **Venue:** Rose Bowl (Pasadena, CA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1925-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
Rockne's legendary Notre Dame Box offense baffled Stanford with rhythm shifts, misdirection, and Elmer Layden's two interception return TDs. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where Knute Rockne's Notre Dame squad secured national championship honors over Pop Warner's Stanford.
