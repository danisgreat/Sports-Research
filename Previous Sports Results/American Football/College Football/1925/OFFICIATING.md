# College Football Officiating & Referee Corps — 1925 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1925

## Championship Officiating Assignment
- **Referee (Crew Chief):** Herb Dana
- **Officiating Panel / Governance:** PCC / Southern Conference Neutral Crew
- **Championship Decider / Matchup:** Alabama vs. Washington (Alabama 20 def. Washington 19)
- **Venue:** Rose Bowl (Pasadena, CA)

## Officiating Governance & Mechanics
Herb Dana presided over this epic Rose Bowl, widely heralded as the turning point for Southern college football. Championship and postseason assignments adhered to strict neutral-conference guidelines, ensuring officiating crews had no institutional affiliation with the participating universities.
