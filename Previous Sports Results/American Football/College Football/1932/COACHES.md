- **Sport:** American Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** USC
- **Winning Head Coach:** Howard Jones
- **Championship Opponent:** Pittsburgh
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Jock Sutherland
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Rose Bowl Game
- **Game Result:** USC 35 def. Pittsburgh 0
- **Venue:** Rose Bowl (Pasadena, CA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1933-01-02

## Tactical & Season Overview
Cotton Warburton dissected Pitt with dazzling runs and touchdown passes in one of the most lopsided early Rose Bowls. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where Howard Jones's USC squad secured national championship honors over Jock Sutherland's Pittsburgh.
