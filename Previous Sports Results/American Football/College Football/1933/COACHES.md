## Tactical & Season Overview
Harry Kipke's 'punt, pass, and prayer' strategy relied on Chuck Bernard and future President Gerald Ford to anchor an impenetrable defense. The season reached its climax in the Big Ten Decider, where Harry Kipke's Michigan squad secured national championship honors over Dick Hanley's Northwestern.
