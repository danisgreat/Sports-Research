## Tactical & Season Overview
Bierman's Single-Wing powerhouse featured relentless blocking and the dynamic dual-threat running of Pug Lund. The season reached its climax in the Big Ten Decider, where Bernie Bierman's Minnesota squad secured national championship honors over Clark Shaughnessy's Chicago.
