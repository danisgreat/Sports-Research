- **Sport:** American Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Minnesota
- **Winning Head Coach:** Bernie Bierman
- **Championship Opponent:** Wisconsin
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Harry Stuhldreher
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Inaugural AP Poll Championship Decider
- **Game Result:** Minnesota 24 def. Wisconsin 0
- **Venue:** Camp Randall Stadium (Madison, WI)
- **Official Match Date:** 1936-11-21

## Tactical & Season Overview
Bernie Bierman's third consecutive national title team featured All-American tackle Ed Widseth and guard Bud Wilkinson. The season reached its climax in the Inaugural AP Poll Championship Decider, where Bernie Bierman's Minnesota squad secured national championship honors over Harry Stuhldreher's Wisconsin.
