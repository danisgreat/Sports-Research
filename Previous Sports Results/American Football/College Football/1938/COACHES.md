## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership

- **Winning Head Coach:** Dutch Meyer
- **Championship Opponent:** Carnegie Tech

- **Championship Decider / Game:** Sugar Bowl

- **Venue:** Tulane Stadium (New Orleans, LA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1939-01-02

## Tactical & Season Overview
Dutch Meyer revolutionized collegiate offense by spreading the field and letting Davey O'Brien throw 20+ passes per game. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl, where Dutch Meyer's TCU squad secured national championship honors over Bill Kern's Carnegie Tech.
