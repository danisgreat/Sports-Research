## Tactical & Season Overview
Bernie Bierman's Golden Gopher machine dominated both lines of scrimmage behind All-American tackle Dick Wildung. The season reached its climax in the Big Ten Decider, where Bernie Bierman's Minnesota squad secured national championship honors over Harry Stuhldreher's Wisconsin.
