# College Football Officiating & Referee Corps — 1941 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1941

## Championship Officiating Assignment
- **Referee (Crew Chief):** Tom Hamilton
- **Officiating Panel / Governance:** Big Ten Officials Panel
- **Championship Decider / Matchup:** Minnesota vs. Wisconsin (Minnesota 41 def. Wisconsin 6)
- **Venue:** Memorial Stadium (Minneapolis, MN)

## Officiating Governance & Mechanics
Big Ten panel. Bruce Smith was awarded the Heisman Trophy days before the attack on Pearl Harbor. Championship and postseason assignments adhered to strict neutral-conference guidelines, ensuring officiating crews had no institutional affiliation with the participating universities.
