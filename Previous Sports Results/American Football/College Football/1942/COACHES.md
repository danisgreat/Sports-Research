## Tactical & Season Overview
Paul Brown brought modern football innovations including play-calling messenger guards, rigorous film study, and classroom playbook tests. The season reached its climax in the The Game (AP Decider), where Paul Brown's Ohio State squad secured national championship honors over Fritz Crisler's Michigan.
