# College Football Officiating & Referee Corps — 1943 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1943

## Championship Officiating Assignment
- **Referee (Crew Chief):** Cliff Montgomery
- **Officiating Panel / Governance:** NCAA Intersectional Panel
- **Championship Decider / Matchup:** Notre Dame vs. Iowa Pre-Flight (Notre Dame 14 def. Iowa Pre-Flight 13)

## Officiating Governance & Mechanics
Cliff Montgomery refereed before 45,000 fans at Notre Dame Stadium. Championship and postseason assignments adhered to strict neutral-conference guidelines, ensuring officiating crews had no institutional affiliation with the participating universities.
