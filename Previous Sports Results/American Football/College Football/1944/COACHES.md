## Tactical & Season Overview
Earl 'Red' Blaik perfected the complementary backfield: Blanchard smashing between the tackles and Davis sweeping around end. The season reached its climax in the Army-Navy Game (AP No. 1 vs No. 2), where Earl Blaik's Army squad secured national championship honors over Billick Whelchel's Navy.
