## Tactical & Season Overview
A defensive masterpiece: Leahy designed a specialized five-man front that completely neutralized Army's perimeter sweep game. The season reached its climax in the The Game of the Century (No. 1 vs No. 2), where Frank Leahy's Notre Dame squad secured national championship honors over Earl Blaik's Army.
