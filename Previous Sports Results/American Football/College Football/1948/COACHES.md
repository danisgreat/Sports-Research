# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1948 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1948

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Michigan
- **Winning Head Coach:** Bennie Oosterbaan
- **Championship Opponent:** Ohio State
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Wes Fesler
- **Championship Decider / Game:** The Game (AP Decider)
- **Game Result:** Michigan 13 def. Ohio State 3
- **Venue:** Ohio Stadium (Columbus, OH)
- **Official Match Date:** 1948-11-20

## Tactical & Season Overview
Michigan utilized Fritz Crisler's revolutionary two-platoon system to out-condition opponents in the second half. The season reached its climax in the The Game (AP Decider), where Bennie Oosterbaan's Michigan squad secured national championship honors over Wes Fesler's Ohio State.
