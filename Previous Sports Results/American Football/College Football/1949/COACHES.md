## Tactical & Season Overview
Wilkinson's Split-T offense tore open LSU's interior with Darrell Royal's option reads and Leon Heath's explosive fullback traps. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl, where Bud Wilkinson's Oklahoma squad secured national championship honors over Gaynell Tinsley's LSU.
