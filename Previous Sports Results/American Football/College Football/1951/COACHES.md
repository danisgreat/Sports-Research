## Tactical & Season Overview
Neyland's Single-Wing relied on punishing gang-tackling defense, ball security, and coffin-corner punting. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl, where Robert Neyland's Tennessee squad secured national championship honors over Jim Tatum's Maryland.
