## Tactical & Season Overview
Biggie Munn's 'Multiple Offense' shifted formations constantly to create blocking angles for Don McAuliffe. The season reached its climax in the Championship Decider, where Biggie Munn's Michigan State squad secured national championship honors over Lisle Blackbourn's Marquette.
