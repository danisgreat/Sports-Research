# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1954 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1954

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Ohio State
- **Winning Head Coach:** Woody Hayes
- **Championship Opponent:** USC
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Jess Hill
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Rose Bowl Game
- **Game Result:** Ohio State 20 def. USC 7
- **Venue:** Rose Bowl (Pasadena, CA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1955-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
Hayes established his trademark 'three yards and a cloud of dust' philosophy, running Hopalong Cassady behind future Hall of Fame guard Jim Parker. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where Woody Hayes's Ohio State squad secured national championship honors over Jess Hill's USC.
