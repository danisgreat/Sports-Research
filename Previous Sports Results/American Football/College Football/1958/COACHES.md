- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** LSU
- **Winning Head Coach:** Paul Dietzel
- **Championship Opponent:** Clemson
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Frank Howard
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Sugar Bowl
- **Game Result:** LSU 7 def. Clemson 0
- **Venue:** Tulane Stadium (New Orleans, LA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1959-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
Paul Dietzel pioneered the three-platoon system ('White Team' starters, 'Go Team' offense, and the ferocious 'Chinese Bandits' defense). The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl, where Paul Dietzel's LSU squad secured national championship honors over Frank Howard's Clemson.
