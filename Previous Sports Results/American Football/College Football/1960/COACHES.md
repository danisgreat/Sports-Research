# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1960 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1960

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Minnesota
- **Winning Head Coach:** Murray Warmath
- **Championship Opponent:** Washington
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Jim Owens
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Rose Bowl Game
- **Game Result:** Washington 17 def. Minnesota 7
- **Venue:** Rose Bowl (Pasadena, CA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1961-01-02

## Tactical & Season Overview
Murray Warmath engineered a stunning worst-to-first turnaround led by groundbreaking African-American All-American quarterback Sandy Stephens. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where Murray Warmath's Minnesota squad secured national championship honors over Jim Owens's Washington.
