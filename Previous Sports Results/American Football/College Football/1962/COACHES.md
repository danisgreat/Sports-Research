## Tactical & Season Overview
John McKay's I-Formation offense scored 42 points before Ron Vander Kelen's heroic aerial comeback set a bowl passing record. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where John McKay's USC squad secured national championship honors over Milt Bruhn's Wisconsin.
