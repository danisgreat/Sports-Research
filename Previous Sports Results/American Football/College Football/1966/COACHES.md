# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1966 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1966

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Notre Dame
- **Winning Head Coach:** Ara Parseghian
- **Championship Opponent:** Michigan State
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Duffy Daugherty
- **Championship Decider / Game:** The Game of the Century (No. 1 vs No. 2)
- **Game Result:** Notre Dame 10 tied Michigan State 10
- **Venue:** Spartan Stadium (East Lansing, MI)
- **Official Match Date:** 1966-11-19

## Tactical & Season Overview
Parseghian took enormous criticism for running out the clock, but preserved his battered team's undefeated record and consensus No. 1 ranking. The season reached its climax in the The Game of the Century (No. 1 vs No. 2), where Ara Parseghian's Notre Dame squad secured national championship honors over Duffy Daugherty's Michigan State.
