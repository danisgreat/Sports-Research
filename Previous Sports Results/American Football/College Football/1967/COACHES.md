# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1967 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1967

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** USC
- **Winning Head Coach:** John McKay
- **Championship Opponent:** Indiana
- **Opposing Head Coach:** John Pont
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Rose Bowl Game
- **Game Result:** USC 14 def. Indiana 3
- **Venue:** Rose Bowl (Pasadena, CA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1968-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
McKay's devastating Student Body Right running play led by Outland Trophy winner Ron Yary proved unstoppable throughout the postseason. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where John McKay's USC squad secured national championship honors over John Pont's Indiana.
