## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership

- **Championship Opponent:** Notre Dame

- **Championship Decider / Game:** Cotton Bowl Classic

- **Venue:** Cotton Bowl (Dallas, TX)
- **Official Match Date:** 1970-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
Darrell Royal's legendary Wishbone triple-option offense, developed with assistant Emory Bellard, revolutionized college football. The season reached its climax in the Cotton Bowl Classic, where Darrell Royal's Texas squad secured national championship honors over Ara Parseghian's Notre Dame.
