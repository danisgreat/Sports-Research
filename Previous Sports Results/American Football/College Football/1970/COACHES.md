- **Sport:** American Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership

- **Winning Head Coach:** Bob Devaney
- **Championship Opponent:** LSU
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Charles McClendon
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Orange Bowl

## Tactical & Season Overview
Devaney's physical I-Formation offense blended with Johnny Rodgers' game-breaking punt returns and speed sweeps. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl, where Bob Devaney's Nebraska squad secured national championship honors over Charles McClendon's LSU.
