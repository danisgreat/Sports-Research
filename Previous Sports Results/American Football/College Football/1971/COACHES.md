# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1971 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1971

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Nebraska
- **Winning Head Coach:** Bob Devaney
- **Championship Opponent:** Alabama
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Bear Bryant
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Orange Bowl (No. 1 vs No. 2)
- **Game Result:** Nebraska 38 def. Alabama 6
- **Venue:** Orange Bowl (Miami, FL)
- **Official Match Date:** 1972-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
Nebraska's 'Blackshirts' defense anchored by Outland/Lombardi winner Rich Glover completely shut down Alabama's newly installed Wishbone. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl (No. 1 vs No. 2), where Bob Devaney's Nebraska squad secured national championship honors over Bear Bryant's Alabama.
