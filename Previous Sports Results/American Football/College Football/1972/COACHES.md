## Tactical & Season Overview
McKay's 1972 Trojans are widely considered one of college football's greatest teams, never trailing in the fourth quarter all season. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl Game, where John McKay's USC squad secured national championship honors over Woody Hayes's Ohio State.
