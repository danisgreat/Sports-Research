## Tactical & Season Overview
Oklahoma's Wishbone shattered NCAA rushing records behind Joe Washington and offensive line dominance. USC won UPI title. The season reached its climax in the Bedlam Series (AP Decider), where Barry Switzer's Oklahoma squad secured national championship honors over Jim Stanley's Oklahoma State.
