# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1975 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1975

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Oklahoma
- **Winning Head Coach:** Barry Switzer
- **Championship Opponent:** Michigan
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Bo Schembechler
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Orange Bowl
- **Game Result:** Oklahoma 14 def. Michigan 6
- **Venue:** Orange Bowl (Miami, FL)
- **Official Match Date:** 1976-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
The Selmon brothers (Lee Roy and Dewey) dominated the line of scrimmage, stifling Michigan's vaunted power-running attack. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl, where Barry Switzer's Oklahoma squad secured national championship honors over Bo Schembechler's Michigan.
