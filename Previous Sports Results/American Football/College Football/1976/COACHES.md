- **Sport:** American Football

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Pittsburgh
- **Winning Head Coach:** Johnny Majors
- **Championship Opponent:** Georgia
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Vince Dooley
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Sugar Bowl
- **Game Result:** Pittsburgh 27 def. Georgia 3
- **Venue:** Louisiana Superdome (New Orleans, LA)
- **Official Match Date:** 1977-01-01

## Tactical & Season Overview
Johnny Majors utilized Tony Dorsett on outside zone sweeps behind pulling guards, setting an all-time NCAA single-season rushing record. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl, where Johnny Majors's Pittsburgh squad secured national championship honors over Vince Dooley's Georgia.
