# College Football Officiating & Referee Corps — 1976 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1976

## Championship Officiating Assignment
- **Referee (Crew Chief):** Charles Moffett
- **Officiating Panel / Governance:** SEC / Eastern Independent Neutral Crew
- **Championship Decider / Matchup:** Pittsburgh vs. Georgia (Pittsburgh 27 def. Georgia 3)
- **Venue:** Louisiana Superdome (New Orleans, LA)

## Officiating Governance & Mechanics
Inaugural Sugar Bowl played in the brand-new Louisiana Superdome. Championship and postseason assignments adhered to strict neutral-conference guidelines, ensuring officiating crews had no institutional affiliation with the participating universities.
