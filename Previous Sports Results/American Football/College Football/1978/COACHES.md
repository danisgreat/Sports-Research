Bear Bryant's defense executed two consecutive stops inside the 1-yard line in one of the most iconic defensive stands in football history. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl (No. 1 vs No. 2), where Bear Bryant's Alabama squad secured national championship honors over Joe Paterno's Penn State.
