# College Football Officiating & Referee Corps — 1978 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1978

## Championship Officiating Assignment
- **Referee (Crew Chief):** Tom Dooley
- **Officiating Panel / Governance:** Pac-10 Neutral Intersectional Crew
- **Championship Decider / Matchup:** Alabama vs. Penn State (Alabama 14 def. Penn State 7)
- **Venue:** Louisiana Superdome (New Orleans, LA)

## Officiating Governance & Mechanics
Tom Dooley and his neutral crew made the decisive spot on the 4th-and-goal tackle. Championship and postseason assignments adhered to strict neutral-conference guidelines, ensuring officiating crews had no institutional affiliation with the participating universities.
