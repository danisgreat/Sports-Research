## Tactical & Season Overview
Vince Dooley leaned on Herschel Walker's historic 36 carries and defensive coordinator Erk Russell's 'Junkyard Dawgs'. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl, where Vince Dooley's Georgia squad secured national championship honors over Dan Devine's Notre Dame.
