- **Championship Opponent:** Nebraska

- **Venue:** Orange Bowl (Miami, FL)

## Tactical & Season Overview
Freshman Bernie Kosar threw for 300 yards in Schnellenberger's pro-style offense; Tom Osborne famously went for the win instead of a national title-clinching tie. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl, where Howard Schnellenberger's Miami (FL) squad secured national championship honors over Tom Osborne's Nebraska.
