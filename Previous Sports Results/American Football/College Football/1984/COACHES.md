## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** BYU
- **Winning Head Coach:** LaVell Edwards
- **Championship Opponent:** Michigan

- **Championship Decider / Game:** Holiday Bowl
- **Game Result:** BYU 24 def. Michigan 17
- **Venue:** Jack Murphy Stadium (San Diego, CA)

## Tactical & Season Overview
LaVell Edwards proved that a WAC passing offense could win a national championship, leading the country in passing yards. The season reached its climax in the Holiday Bowl, where LaVell Edwards's BYU squad secured national championship honors over Bo Schembechler's Michigan.
