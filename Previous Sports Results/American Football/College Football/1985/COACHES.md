- **Venue:** Orange Bowl (Miami, FL)

## Tactical & Season Overview
Switzer revived the Wishbone mid-season after Troy Aikman's injury, turning Holieway into the only true freshman QB to start and win a national championship. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl (No. 1 vs No. 3), where Barry Switzer's Oklahoma squad secured national championship honors over Joe Paterno's Penn State.
