# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1988 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1988

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Notre Dame
- **Winning Head Coach:** Lou Holtz
- **Championship Opponent:** West Virginia
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Don Nehlen
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Fiesta Bowl (No. 1 vs No. 3)
- **Game Result:** Notre Dame 34 def. West Virginia 21
- **Venue:** Sun Devil Stadium (Tempe, AZ)
- **Official Match Date:** 1989-01-02

## Tactical & Season Overview
Holtz deployed an explosive option offense and swarming defense that knocked WVU quarterback Major Harris out of rhythm early. The season reached its climax in the Fiesta Bowl (No. 1 vs No. 3), where Lou Holtz's Notre Dame squad secured national championship honors over Don Nehlen's West Virginia.
