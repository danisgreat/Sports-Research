## Tactical & Season Overview
Miami's defense suffocated Nebraska's option, allowing only 82 rushing yards and recording the only bowl shutout of Tom Osborne's career. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl, where Dennis Erickson's Miami (FL) squad secured national championship honors over Tom Osborne's Nebraska.
