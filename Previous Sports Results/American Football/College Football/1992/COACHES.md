## Tactical & Season Overview
Gene Stallings and defensive coordinator Bill Oliver devised an aggressive 11-man blitz and disguised coverage scheme that rattled Heisman winner Gino Torretta into 3 INTs. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl (Bowl Coalition National Championship), where Gene Stallings's Alabama squad secured national championship honors over Dennis Erickson's Miami (FL).
