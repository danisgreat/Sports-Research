## Tactical & Season Overview
Heisman winner Charlie Ward operated Bowden's 'Fast Break' shotgun offense against Nebraska's formidable 4-3 defense. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl (Bowl Coalition National Championship), where Bobby Bowden's Florida State squad secured national championship honors over Tom Osborne's Nebraska.
