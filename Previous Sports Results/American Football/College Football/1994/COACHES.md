Osborne's powerful I-Formation option wore down Miami's smaller defensive front in the humid Miami night. The season reached its climax in the Orange Bowl (Bowl Coalition National Championship), where Tom Osborne's Nebraska squad secured national championship honors over Dennis Erickson's Miami (FL).
