## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership

- **Winning Head Coach:** Tom Osborne
- **Championship Opponent:** Florida
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Steve Spurrier

- **Game Result:** Nebraska 62 def. Florida 24
- **Venue:** Sun Devil Stadium (Tempe, AZ)
- **Official Match Date:** 1996-01-02

## Tactical & Season Overview
Widely ranked among the greatest single-game performances in history, Nebraska generated 524 rushing yards and sacked Danny Wuerffel seven times. The season reached its climax in the Fiesta Bowl (Bowl Alliance National Championship), where Tom Osborne's Nebraska squad secured national championship honors over Steve Spurrier's Florida.
