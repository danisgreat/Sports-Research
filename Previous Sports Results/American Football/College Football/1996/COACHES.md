## Tactical & Season Overview
Spurrier used shotgun formations exclusively, allowing Danny Wuerffel to throw for 306 yards and 3 TDs while Ike Hilliard caught 3 scores. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl (Bowl Alliance National Championship), where Steve Spurrier's Florida squad secured national championship honors over Bobby Bowden's Florida State.
