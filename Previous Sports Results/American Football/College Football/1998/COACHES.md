## Tactical & Season Overview
Phillip Fulmer's Volunteers captured the post-Peyton Manning era crown through stifling defense and deep play-action passing. The season reached its climax in the Fiesta Bowl (Inaugural BCS National Championship Game), where Phillip Fulmer's Tennessee squad secured national championship honors over Bobby Bowden's Florida State.
