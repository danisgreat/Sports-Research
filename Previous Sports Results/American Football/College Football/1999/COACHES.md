# College Football Coaching Staff & Leadership — 1999 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 1999

## Championship Matchup & Coaching Leadership
- **National Champions:** Florida State
- **Winning Head Coach:** Bobby Bowden
- **Championship Opponent:** Virginia Tech
- **Opposing Head Coach:** Frank Beamer
- **Championship Decider / Game:** Sugar Bowl (BCS National Championship Game)
- **Game Result:** Florida State 46 def. Virginia Tech 29
- **Venue:** Louisiana Superdome (New Orleans, LA)
- **Official Match Date:** 2000-01-04

## Tactical & Season Overview
Bobby Bowden became the first coach in history to lead a team from preseason No. 1 through an entire season to the national title without losing the top ranking. The season reached its climax in the Sugar Bowl (BCS National Championship Game), where Bobby Bowden's Florida State squad secured national championship honors over Frank Beamer's Virginia Tech.
