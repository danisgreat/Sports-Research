## Tactical & Season Overview
Jim Tressel's 'Tresselball' relied on field position, bruising running by Maurice Clarett, and clutch short-yardage conversions by Craig Krenzel. The season reached its climax in the Fiesta Bowl (BCS National Championship Game), where Jim Tressel's Ohio State squad secured national championship honors over Larry Coker's Miami (FL).
