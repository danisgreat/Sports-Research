# College Football Officiating & Referee Corps — 2006 Season

- **Sport:** American Football
- **Competition:** College Football
- **Season:** 2006

## Championship Officiating Assignment
- **Referee (Crew Chief):** Jack Folliard
- **Officiating Panel / Governance:** Pac-10 Neutral Intersectional Crew
- **Championship Decider / Matchup:** Florida vs. Ohio State (Florida 41 def. Ohio State 14)

## Officiating Governance & Mechanics
Inaugural standalone BCS title game refereed by Jack Folliard. Championship and postseason assignments adhered to strict neutral-conference guidelines, ensuring officiating crews had no institutional affiliation with the participating universities.
