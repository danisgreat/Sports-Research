## Tactical & Season Overview
Florida State rallied from an 18-point deficit, sparked by Kermit Whitfield's 100-yard kickoff return, to end the SEC's seven-year title streak. The season reached its climax in the Rose Bowl (Final BCS National Championship Game), where Jimbo Fisher's Florida State squad secured national championship honors over Gus Malzahn's Auburn.
