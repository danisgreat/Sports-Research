# IFAF World Championship Coaching Staff & Team Hierarchy — 1999 Tournament

- **Sport:** American Football (International)
- **Competition:** IFAF World Championship of American Football
- **Edition:** 1999 World Championship
- **Participating National Teams (6 Nations):** Japan, Mexico, Sweden, Italy, Australia, Finland

## Participating National Head Coaches
| National Team | Senior Head Coach & Staff | Tournament Placement |
|---|---|---|
| Japan | Toshiaki Abe (Head Coach) / Kiyoyuki Mori (OC) | Champions (Gold Medal) |
| Mexico | Jacinto Licea (Head Coach) | Runners-up (Silver Medal) |
| Sweden | Jan Jenmert (Head Coach) | 3rd Place (Bronze Medal) |
| Italy | Mike Wyatt (Head Coach) | 4th Place |
| Australia | Jim Wagstaff (Head Coach) | 5th Place |
| Finland | Tomas Hagman (Head Coach) | 6th Place |

## Championship Decider
- **World Champions (Gold Medal):** Japan
- **Runners-up (Silver Medal):** Mexico
- **Gold Medal Game Score:** Japan def. Mexico (6-0 (OT))
- **Tournament MVP:** Takashi Ogata (WR, Japan)
- **Host Venue:** Velodromo Paolo Borsellino (Palermo, Italy)

- **Sources:** International Federation of American Football (IFAF); USA Football; JAFA.
