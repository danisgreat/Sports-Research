# IFAF World Championship Coaching Staff & Team Hierarchy — 2007 Tournament

- **Sport:** American Football (International)
- **Competition:** IFAF World Championship of American Football
- **Edition:** 2007 World Championship
- **Participating National Teams (6 Nations):** United States, Japan, Germany, Sweden, South Korea, France

## Participating National Head Coaches
| National Team | Senior Head Coach & Staff | Tournament Placement |
|---|---|---|
| United States | John Mackovic (Head Coach) | Champions (Gold Medal) |
| Japan | Toshiaki Abe (Head Coach) / Kiyoyuki Mori (OC) | Runners-up (Silver Medal) |
| Germany | Peter Springwald (Head Coach) | 3rd Place (Bronze Medal) |
| Sweden | BD Kennedy (Head Coach) | 4th Place |
| South Korea | Baek Seong-il (Sun-Il Baek) (Head Coach) | 5th Place |
| France | Larry Legault (Head Coach) | 6th Place |

## Championship Decider
- **World Champions (Gold Medal):** United States
- **Runners-up (Silver Medal):** Japan
- **Gold Medal Game Score:** United States def. Japan (23-20 (2OT))
- **Tournament MVP:** Craig Coffin (K/P, USA)
- **Host Venue:** Todoroki Athletics Stadium (Kawasaki, Japan)

- **Sources:** International Federation of American Football (IFAF); USA Football; JAFA.
