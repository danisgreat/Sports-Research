# NFL — Historical Players, Rosters & Champions Register (1900–2025)

An authoritative, definitive season-by-season chronicle of the National Football League (NFL) and its predecessor, the American Professional Football Association (APFA), spanning from 1900 to 2025. This chronicle details official league eras, participating franchises, league champions and runners-up with head coaching leadership, championship game and Super Bowl deciders, official league MVPs (Joe F. Carr Trophy, UPI, and Associated Press MVP), Super Bowl MVPs, authentic active marquee roster players extracted from verified league datasets, and senior officiating appointments.

---

## Historical Timeline & Status (1900–2025)

- **Pre-Founding Era (1900 to 1919):**
  - **Status:** `NOT_YET_FOUNDED`
  - **Context:** The National Football League did not exist prior to 1920. Professional gridiron was contested across regional circuits (the Ohio League, Western Pennsylvania circuits, and New York Pro Football League). Early powerhouse clubs included the Canton Bulldogs (featuring Jim Thorpe), Massillon Tigers, Latrobe Athletic Association, and Frankford Athletic Association, but no unified national championship league operated.

- **Founding & APFA Era (1920 to 1921):**
  - Established as the American Professional Football Association (APFA) on August 20 and September 17, 1920 in Canton, Ohio. Fourteen foundation franchises took the field in 1920, with Akron Pros awarded the inaugural Brunswick-Balke-Collender Cup.

- **Pre-Playoff NFL Era (1922 to 1931):**
  - Reorganized and renamed the National Football League in 1922. League champions were determined strictly by regular-season winning percentage (ties excluded). Green Bay Packers achieved the league's first three-peat (1929–1931) under Curly Lambeau.

- **Inaugural Championship Game & Two-Division Era (1932 to 1965):**
  - Following the 1932 indoor playoff game at Chicago Stadium, the NFL divided into Eastern and Western Divisions in 1933 and instituted an annual NFL Championship Game. The era established the annual NFL Draft (1936), the Joe F. Carr Trophy MVP (1938–1946), the AAFC merger (1950), and the official Associated Press Most Valuable Player award (1957).

- **Super Bowl & Modern Merger Era (1966 to 2025):**
  - Initiated with the AFL-NFL merger agreement and Super Bowl I in January 1967. Full unification occurred in 1970 with the formation of the American Football Conference (AFC) and National Football Conference (NFC). The league expanded from 26 franchises in 1970 to 32 franchises across eight divisions in 2002, culminating in 17-game international regular-season campaigns through Super Bowl LIX (2025).
