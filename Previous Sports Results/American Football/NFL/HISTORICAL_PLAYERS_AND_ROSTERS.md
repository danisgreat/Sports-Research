- **League Status:** INACTIVE (Pre-NFL Era).

### 1920 Season
- **Inaugural Season (APFA):** The American Professional Football Association (APFA) was organized on August 20 and September 17, 1920 in Canton, Ohio. 14 teams competed.
- **Champion:** Akron Pros (awarded the Brunswick-Balke-Collender Cup with an undefeated 8-0-3 record).
- **Key Figures:** Jim Thorpe (APFA President / Canton player), George Halas (Decatur Staleys, later Chicago Bears).

### 1921 Season
- **Era:** Early APFA / NFL League (renamed National Football League in 1922). No playoff system; champion determined by regular-season winning percentage.
- **Franchises & Legends:** Green Bay Packers (entered 1921 under Curly Lambeau), Chicago Bears (George Halas, Red Grange), New York Giants (founded 1925 by Tim Mara).
