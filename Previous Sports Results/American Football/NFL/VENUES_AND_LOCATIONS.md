# NFL Stadiums & Venue Profiles — Historical & Modern

Comprehensive reference of all 30 modern NFL stadiums and historic venues from 1920 through 2025.

---

## 1. Modern NFL Stadium Profiles

### Lambeau Field — Green Bay, Wisconsin
- **Franchise:** Green Bay Packers
- **Opened:** 1957 (Oldest continuously operating NFL stadium)
- **Capacity:** 81,441
- **Surface:** SIS Grass (Kentucky bluegrass reinforced with synthetic fibers)
- **Roof:** Open-air
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - The "Frozen Tundra": Late-season and playoff temperatures routinely fall below 0°F (-18°C), causing football leather to harden and dramatically reducing kicking distances.
  - Substantial home-field edge against warm-weather and dome opponents.

### Arrowhead Stadium (GEHA Field) — Kansas City, Missouri
- **Franchise:** Kansas City Chiefs
- **Opened:** 1972
- **Capacity:** 76,416
- **Surface:** Latitude 36 Bermuda Grass
- **Roof:** Open-air
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - Guinness World Record for loudest outdoor stadium crowd noise (142.2 dB). Causes severe pre-snap communication breakdowns and offensive false-start penalties.
  - Swirling Midwestern winds and freezing winter turf in January playoffs.

### Highmark Stadium — Orchard Park (Buffalo), New York
- **Franchise:** Buffalo Bills
- **Opened:** 1973
- **Capacity:** 71,608
- **Surface:** A-Turf Titan 50
- **Roof:** Open-air
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - Extreme lake-effect snowstorms from Lake Erie; swirling wind funnel through the stadium bowl exceeding 30-40 mph, severely depressing passing efficiency and field goal attempts.

### Soldier Field — Chicago, Illinois
- **Franchise:** Chicago Bears
- **Opened:** 1924 (rebuilt 2003)
- **Capacity:** 61,500
- **Surface:** Bermuda Grass
- **Roof:** Open-air
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - Lakefront winds off Lake Michigan ("The Windy City"); heavy, soft sod conditions late in the season.

### AT&T Stadium — Arlington, Texas
- **Franchise:** Dallas Cowboys
- **Opened:** 2009
- **Capacity:** 80,000 (expandable to 105,000)
- **Surface:** Hellas Matrix Turf
- **Roof:** Retractable roof
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - East-West architectural orientation causes intense low-angle glare through west end-zone windows during late-afternoon Autumn kickoffs. Fast, climate-controlled passing conditions.

### SoFi Stadium — Inglewood, California
- **Franchises:** Los Angeles Rams, Los Angeles Chargers
- **Opened:** 2020
- **Capacity:** 70,240
- **Surface:** Matrix Turf with organic infill
- **Roof:** Translucent fixed canopy with open sides allowing ocean breezes.

### MetLife Stadium — East Rutherford, New Jersey
- **Franchises:** New York Giants, New York Jets
- **Opened:** 2010
- **Capacity:** 82,500
- **Surface:** FieldTurf Core
- **Roof:** Open-air
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - Meadowlands swamp wind patterns; unpredictable cross-winds in fourth quarters.

### Lumen Field — Seattle, Washington
- **Franchise:** Seattle Seahawks
- **Opened:** 2002
- **Capacity:** 68,740
- **Surface:** FieldTurf Revolution 360
- **Roof:** Open-air with cantilevered roof covers
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - Parabolic roof structure reflects crowd noise directly onto the playing surface; the "12th Man" generates persistent false starts. Constant Pacific Northwest rain.

### Empower Field at Mile High — Denver, Colorado
- **Franchise:** Denver Broncos
- **Opened:** 2001
- **Capacity:** 76,125
- **Elevation:** 5,280 feet (1,609 m) above sea level
- **Observations & Environmental Factors:**
  - High altitude decreases air density by ~17%, allowing field goals to travel 5-8 yards farther than at sea level. Visiting teams suffer aerobic fatigue in the 2nd half.

## 2. Historic NFL Stadiums

- **Polo Grounds (New York):** Horseshoe shape with 279-ft foul lines and 483-ft center field; home to Giants and Jets.
- **Wrigley Field & Comiskey Park (Chicago):** Early home of Chicago Bears (1921–1970) and Chicago Cardinals.
- **Cleveland Municipal Stadium (Ohio):** The "Mistake by the Lake" on Lake Erie; legendary dawg pound and gale-force winter winds.
- **Veterans Stadium (Philadelphia):** Notorious concrete-hard Neave-Turf surface, 700-level fan rowdiness.
- **Three Rivers Stadium (Pittsburgh), Riverfront Stadium (Cincinnati), Astrodome (Houston), Kingdome (Seattle), Pontiac Silverdome (Detroit), Giants Stadium (East Rutherford), Orange Bowl (Miami), RFK Stadium (Washington).**
