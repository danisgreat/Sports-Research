# Super Bowl — Historical Players, Rosters & Champions Register (1900–2025)

An authoritative, comprehensive season-by-season chronicle of every Super Bowl from Super Bowl I (1967) to Super Bowl LIX (2025), detailing championship franchises, winning and losing head coaches, game MVPs, crew chief referees, venues, and official attendances.

---

## Historical Timeline & Status (1900–2025)

- **Pre-Founding Era (1900 to 1966):**
  - **Status:** `NOT_YET_FOUNDED`
  - **Context:** The Super Bowl was established in 1967 following the merger agreement between the National Football League (NFL) and the American Football League (AFL). The first four editions (1967–1970) were played as the AFL-NFL World Championship Game before the leagues fully unified into the modern NFL in 1970. No sanctioned Super Bowl games existed prior to January 15, 1967 (Super Bowl I).

- **Active Championship Era (1967 to 2025):**
  - 59 Super Bowl editions staged (Super Bowl I through Super Bowl LIX).
