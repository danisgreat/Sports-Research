# UFL — Historical Players, Rosters & Champions Register (1900–2025)

An authoritative chronicle of the United Football League (UFL), detailing franchise rosters, head coaching staffs, coordinators, championship outcomes, MVPs, and league awards.

---

## Historical Timeline & Status (1900–2025)

- **Pre-Founding Era (1900 to 2023):**
  - **Status:** `NOT_YET_FOUNDED`
  - **Context:** The modern United Football League (UFL) was established in late 2023 through the formal merger of the United States Football League (USFL) and the XFL, commencing play in the spring of 2024. No unified UFL existed prior to the 2024 inaugural season.

- **Active League Era (2024 to 2025):**
  - 8-team spring football circuit featuring the USFL Conference and XFL Conference.
