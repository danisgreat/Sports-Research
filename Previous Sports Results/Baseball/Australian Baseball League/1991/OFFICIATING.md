# Australian Baseball League Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1991 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **Competition:** Australian Baseball League
- **Season:** 1991
- **Governing Body:** Baseball Australia / ABL Operations

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** ABL Umpiring Panel (Home Plate, First, Second, Third Base).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1991 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Baseball Australia / ABL Operations.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
