# CPBL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1994 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **Competition:** CPBL
- **Season:** 1994
- **Governing Body:** Chinese Professional Baseball League Commission (Taiwan)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** CPBL Umpiring Crew.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1994 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Chinese Professional Baseball League Commission (Taiwan).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
