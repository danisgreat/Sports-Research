# KBO Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1988 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **Competition:** KBO
- **Season:** 1988
- **Governing Body:** Korea Baseball Organization (KBO)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** KBO Umpire Committee.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1988 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Korea Baseball Organization (KBO).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
