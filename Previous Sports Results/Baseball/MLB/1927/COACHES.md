# MLB Managers & Coaching Staffs — 1927 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **League:** Major League Baseball (MLB)
- **Season:** 1927

## Managerial Overview
- **Field Managers:** Senior managers for all American and National League clubs directing bullpen usage, pinch-hitting, and defensive positioning.
- **Championship Manager:** The manager leading the pennant/World Series champion in 1927.
- **Coaching Staff:** Pitching Coach, Hitting Coach, Bench Coach, First Base Coach, Third Base Coach, and Bullpen Coach.
- **Tactical Developments:** In-game pitching changes, platoon splits, defensive shifts, and modern analytics integration.
