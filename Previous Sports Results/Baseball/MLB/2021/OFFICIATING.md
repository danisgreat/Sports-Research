# MLB Umpiring Crew & Officiating Registry — 2021 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **League:** Major League Baseball (MLB)
- **Season:** 2021

## Umpiring Framework
- **Crew Allocation:** 4-man regular season crews (Home Plate, First Base, Second Base, Third Base; expanding to 6 umpires including Left Field and Right Field for postseason).
- **World Series Crew Chief:** Veteran senior umpire leading the World Series officiating crew.
- **Technology & Review:** MLB Replay Operations Center in Chelsea, NY with Manager Challenge System.
- **Pitch Clock & Timing:** Traditional pace of play.
