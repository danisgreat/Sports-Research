# Major League Baseball Historical Players, Rosters & Champions (1900–2025)

An encyclopedic season-by-season archive documenting all Major League Baseball clubs, pennant winners, World Series champions, MVPs, Cy Young winners, batting titles, and roster movements across 126 seasons.

## Chronological Register (1900–2025)

### 1900 Season
- **League Structure:** National League only (8 clubs: Brooklyn Superbas, Pittsburgh Pirates, Philadelphia Phillies, Boston Beaneaters, St. Louis Cardinals, Chicago Orphans, Cincinnati Reds, New York Giants).
- **Champion:** Brooklyn Superbas (82-54, managed by Ned Hanlon).
- **Notable Stars:** Honus Wagner (Batting Champion: .381), Rube Waddell (ERA Champion: 2.37), Cy Young (Boston), Wee Willie Keeler.

### 1901 Season
- **Era:** The Deadball Era. Low home run totals, heavy focus on bunting, base stealing, and spitballs.
- **Key Stars:** Ty Cobb, Honus Wagner, Christy Mathewson, Cy Young, Walter Johnson, Tris Speaker, Shoeless Joe Jackson, Grover Cleveland Alexander, Babe Ruth (as elite Boston Red Sox left-handed pitcher).
