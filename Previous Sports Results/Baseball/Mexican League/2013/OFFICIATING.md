# Mexican League Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2013 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **Competition:** Mexican League
- **Season:** 2013
- **Governing Body:** Liga Mexicana de Beisbol (LMB)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** LMB Umpire Crew and Rules Committee.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2013 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Liga Mexicana de Beisbol (LMB).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
