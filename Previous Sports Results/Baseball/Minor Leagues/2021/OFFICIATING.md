# Minor Leagues Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2021 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **Competition:** Minor Leagues
- **Season:** 2021
- **Governing Body:** Minor League Baseball (MiLB) / MLB Player Development

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** MiLB Umpire Development Staff and Field Umpires.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2021 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Minor League Baseball (MiLB) / MLB Player Development.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
