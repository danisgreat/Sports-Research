# NPB Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2007 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **Competition:** NPB
- **Season:** 2007
- **Governing Body:** Nippon Professional Baseball Organization (NPB)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** NPB Umpire Department.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2007 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Nippon Professional Baseball Organization (NPB).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
