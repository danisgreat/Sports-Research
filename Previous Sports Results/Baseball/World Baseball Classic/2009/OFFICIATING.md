# World Baseball Classic Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2009 Season

- **Sport:** Baseball
- **Competition:** World Baseball Classic
- **Season:** 2009
- **Governing Body:** World Baseball Classic Inc. (MLB / MLBPA / WBSC)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** WBC Umpiring Crew (MLB & International Umpires).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2009 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by World Baseball Classic Inc. (MLB / MLBPA / WBSC).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
