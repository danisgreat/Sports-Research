## Historical Timeline & Status (1975–2025)

- **1975 to 1998 (A-Liga / 1. Bundesliga under ÖBV):**
  Direct governance by the Österreichischer Basketballverband (ÖBV). Dominated by UBM Milde Sorte Wien (6 titles in 7 years), followed by BK Klosterneuburg's legendary 8-in-a-row dynasty (1983–1990), and UKJ Süba St. Pölten (6 titles in the 1990s).

- **1998 to 2019 (Admiral Basketball Bundesliga - ABL / ÖBL):**
  Independent professional league era. Dominated by Kapfenberg Bulls (7 titles), Swans Gmunden (4 titles), Oberwart Gunners, WBC Wels, BSC Fürstenfeld Panthers, BC Vienna, and UBC Güssing Knights. Transitioned from halves to four 10-minute quarters in 2000–01.

- **2019 to 2026 (Basketball Superliga - win2day BSL):**
  Reintegrated under Basketball Austria. 2019–20 season halted in March 2020 by COVID-19 after 22 rounds with no champion awarded. Swans Gmunden (2021, 2023), BC Vienna (2022), and Gunners Oberwart (2024) claimed titles in the modern 3-phase championship format.
