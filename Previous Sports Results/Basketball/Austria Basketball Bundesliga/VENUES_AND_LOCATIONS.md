# Austria Basketball Bundesliga — Venues & Locations

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition / League:** Austria Bundesliga Basketball (Basketball Superliga [BSL] / Admiral Basketball Bundesliga [ABL] / A-Liga)
- **Governing Body:** Basketball Austria (ÖBV - Österreichischer Basketballverband)

---

## Historical Venues & Arena Profiles

1. **FZZ Happyland (Freizeitzentrum Happyland)** — Klosterneuburg, Lower Austria
   - Home of: BK IMMOunited Dukes (Klosterneuburg)
   - Capacity: 1,200 spectators
   - Historic Fortress of the 10-time Austrian champions.

2. **Volksbank Arena Gmunden** — Gmunden, Upper Austria
   - Home of: Swans Gmunden
   - Capacity: 2,200 spectators
   - Famous lakeside arena on the Traunsee; host to numerous Supercups and Finals.

3. **Sporthalle Walfersam** — Kapfenberg, Styria
   - Home of: Kapfenberg Bulls (ece bulls)
   - Capacity: 1,000 spectators
   - Iconic home of the Bulls' 7 modern national titles.

4. **Sporthalle Oberwart** — Oberwart, Burgenland
   - Home of: Unger Steel Gunners Oberwart
   - Capacity: 2,500 spectators
   - One of the largest dedicated basketball halls in Austria with a renowned passionate fanbase.

5. **Lions Dome** — Traiskirchen, Lower Austria
   - Home of: Arkadia Traiskirchen Lions (formerly UKJ Möllersdorf)
   - Capacity: 1,200 spectators

6. **Raiffeisen Arena Wels** — Wels, Upper Austria
   - Home of: Raiffeisen Flyers Wels (formerly WBC Kraftwerk Wels)
   - Capacity: 1,700 spectators

7. **Hallmann Dome / Wiener Stadthalle B** — Vienna (Wien)
   - Home of: BC Vienna (formerly BC Zepter / BC Hallmann / BC GGMT Vienna)
   - Capacity: 1,500 spectators

8. **Raiffeisen Sportpark Graz** — Graz, Styria
   - Home of: UBSC Raiffeisen Graz
   - Capacity: 3,000 spectators
   - Modern premier multi-sport venue in Styria.

9. **bet-at-home Arena / Sportzentrum NÖ** — St. Pölten, Lower Austria
   - Home of: SKN St. Pölten Basketball (formerly UKJ Süba St. Pölten)
   - Capacity: 1,000 spectators

10. **Sportzentrum Eisenstadt** — Eisenstadt, Burgenland
    - Home of: BBC Nord Dragonz Eisenstadt
    - Capacity: 800 spectators

11. **Wolves Dome** — Vienna (Wien)
    - Home of: Vienna Timberwolves
    - Capacity: 800 spectators

12. **Stadthalle Fürstenfeld** — Fürstenfeld, Styria
    - Home of: Coldamaris Fürstenfeld Panthers
    - Capacity: 1,200 spectators
