# Japanese Professional Basketball — Historical Players, Legends & Award Roll (1975–2026)

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition / League:** B.League (B1, B2), NBL, bj-league, JBL, Japan Basketball League (日本リーグ), & Emperor's Cup (天皇杯)
- **Governing Body:** Japan Professional Basketball League (B.LEAGUE) & Japan Basketball Association (JBA)

## 1. Legendary Figures & All-Time Japanese Basketball Icons

### Domestic Icons & Trailblazers
1. **Yuta Tabuse (田臥勇太)**: First Japanese player in NBA history (Phoenix Suns, 2004). Returned to Japan to lead Link Tochigi Brex to the 2010 JBL championship and 2017 & 2022 B.League championships. B.League cultural patriarch.
2. **Takehiko Orimo (折茂武彦)**: Legendary shooting guard who played professionally past age 49. Surpassed 10,000 career points in top-tier Japanese basketball. President and founder of Levanga Hokkaido.
3. **Kenichi Sako (佐古賢一)**: "Mr. Basketball" of Japan. Floor general who led Isuzu Motors Giga Cats and Aisin Seahorses to 9 Emperor's Cup titles and multiple Japan League championships. 3-time Japan League MVP.
4. **Michael Takahashi (高橋マイケル)**: Dominant forward who spearheaded the Isuzu Motors dynasty in the 1990s and later starred for Toyota Alvark and Niigata Albirex.
5. **Yuki Togashi (富樫勇樹)**: Dynamic point guard for Chiba Jets. 2018–19 B.League MVP, multiple Finals MVP, 2024 EASL Champion, and captain of the Akatsuki Japan national team at the 2020 & 2024 Olympic Games.
6. **Makoto Hiejima (比江島慎)**: 2023–24 B.League MVP and Utsunomiya Brex scoring virtuoso. Leading offensive weapon for Japan internationally at the FIBA World Cup and Olympics.
7. **Joji Takeuchi (竹内譲次) & Kosuke Takeuchi (竹内公輔)**: The legendary "Takeuchi Twins" who anchored the frontcourt for Japan's national team and top corporate/pro powerhouses (Hitachi, Toyota/Alvark, Ryukyu, Utsunomiya) for over two decades.
8. **Kei Igarashi (五十嵐圭)**: Long-time elite point guard for Hitachi Sunrockers, Toyota Alvark, Mitsubishi Electric, and Niigata Albirex BB.
9. **Takuya Kawamura (川村卓也)**: Elite perimeter scorer; 5-time JBL/Japan League scoring champion and multiple All-Star MVP.

### Naturalized & International Legends
1. **Nick Fazekas (ニック・ファジーカス)**: Former Nevada Wolf Pack and NBA forward who became the face of Toshiba / Kawasaki Brave Thunders from 2012 to 2024. 2016–17 Inaugural B.League MVP, naturalized Japanese citizen who led Japan to historic FIBA World Cup qualification. Retired jersey #22.
2. **J.R. Sakuragi / J.R. Henderson (桜木ジェイアール)**: UCLA NCAA Champion and Vancouver Grizzlies NBA player who joined Aisin Seahorses in 2001. Anchored Aisin/Mikawa's dominance for 19 seasons winning 7 Emperor's Cups and 6 league titles.
3. **Michael Parker (マイケル・パーカー)**: All-time defensive and steal wizard who played for Rizing Fukuoka, Shimane, Wakayama, Toyota/Alvark, and Gunma Crane Thunders. All-time leader in steals and rebounds across bj-league and B.League.
4. **Davante Gardner (ダバンテ・ガードナー)**: 3-time B.League scoring champion with Niigata Albirex BB and SeaHorses Mikawa. Unstoppable low-post force.
5. **Josh Hawkinson (ジョシュ・ホーキンソン)**: Naturalized center for Shinshu Brave Warriors and Sunrockers Shibuya; hero of Japan's 2023 FIBA World Cup campaign clinching 2024 Paris Olympic qualification.
6. **Ryan Rossiter (ライアン・ロシター)**: Versatile triple-double threat for Utsunomiya Brex and Alvark Tokyo. 2019–20 All-B.League First Team and naturalized national team anchor.
7. **Sebastian Saiz (セバスチャン・サイズ)**: Spanish powerhouse center who led Sunrockers Shibuya to the 2020 Emperor's Cup and Alvark Tokyo to multiple deep playoff runs.
