## 3. Infrastructure & Technical Specifications

1. **Court Surface Standards**:
   - High-grade sprung Canadian hard maple court systems approved by FIBA (Level 1 certification).
   - In 2016, the B.League inaugural match at Yoyogi 1st Gymnasium made world history by deploying the world's first full-court interactive LED glass playing surface.
2. **Shot Clock & Officiating Technology**:
   - Four-sided integrated LED 24-second shot clocks mounted atop hydraulic portable backstop units (Schelde / Spalding / Senoh).
   - Integrated Instant Replay System (IRS) linked to Genius Sports and B.League video review center.
3. **B.PREMIER (2026+) Licensing Criteria**:
   - Minimum 5,000 permanent seating capacity.
   - Luxury hospitality suites (minimum 10 VIP suites).
   - Dedicated club revenue exceeding ¥1.2 billion and average home attendance above 4,000 spectators per game.
