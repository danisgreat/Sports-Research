# Japanese Professional Basketball — Venues & Geographical Distribution (1975–2026)

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition / League:** B.League (B1, B2, B3), NBL, bj-league, JBL, & Emperor's Cup
- **Governing Body:** Japan Professional Basketball League (B.LEAGUE) & Japan Basketball Association (JBA)

## 2. Infrastructure Standards & Environmental Dimensions

1. **Court Playing Surfaces**:
   - High-performance spring-cushioned Canadian hard maple floors adhering to FIBA Level 1 certification.
   - Distinctive court styling: Alvark Tokyo (Black & Red perimeter), Ryukyu Golden Kings (Navy & Gold stained court), Chiba Jets (Crimson Red perimeter).
2. **Environmental & Climate Considerations**:
   - Fully climate-controlled enclosed arenas maintaining optimal temperature (20–22°C) and relative humidity (45–55%) to prevent court moisture condensation and athlete slippage.
3. **B.PREMIER Future Arena Standard (2026+)**:
   - The JPBL "B.Revolution" reform established mandatory criteria of 5,000+ seat arenas with luxury hospitality suites to eliminate sporting promotion/relegation and establish a stable franchise financial model.
