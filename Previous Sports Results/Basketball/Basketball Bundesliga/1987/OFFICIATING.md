# Basketball Bundesliga Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1987 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** Basketball Bundesliga
- **Season:** 1987
- **Governing Body:** BBL GmbH / Deutscher Basketball Bund

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** BBL Schiedsrichter Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1987 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by BBL GmbH / Deutscher Basketball Bund.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
