# EuroCup Basketball: Venues & Arenas Directory (1975–2026)

## 1. Overview of Continental Arenas
The EuroCup and its predecessor competitions (FIBA European Cup Winners' Cup, European Cup, EuroCup, Saporta Cup, and ULEB Cup) have been hosted across Europe's most famous and historic indoor sports temples. Spanning from multi-purpose metropolitan super-arenas to historic municipal gymnasiums, these venues represent the heartbeat of European basketball fandom.

## 3. Comprehensive Directory of Regular Season & Playoff Venues

### Spain
- **Palau Municipal d'Esports de Badalona (Palau Olímpic)** (Badalona) — Capacity: 12,760. Home of Club Joventut Badalona; 1992 Olympic basketball stadium.
- **Palacio de Deportes José María Martín Carpena** (Málaga) — Capacity: 11,300. Home of Unicaja Málaga; hosted 2017 Finals.
- **Bilbao Arena (Miribilla)** (Bilbao) — Capacity: 10,014. Home of Bilbao Basket; 2013 Eurocup Finalists.
- **Pabellón Príncipe Felipe** (Zaragoza) — Capacity: 10,744. Home of Casademont Zaragoza; hosted 1999 Saporta Cup Final.
- **Pavelló Nou Congost** (Manresa) — Capacity: 5,000. Home of BAXI Manresa.
- **Pazo dos Deportes** (Lugo) — Capacity: 5,310. Home of Río Breogán.

### Italy
- **PalaDozza (Madison di Piazza Azzarita)** (Bologna) — Capacity: 5,570. Historic temple of Fortitudo and Virtus Bologna.
- **PalaTrento (Il T Quotidiano Arena)** (Trento) — Capacity: 4,360. Home of Aquila Basket Trento.
- **PalaTaliercio** (Mestre / Venice) — Capacity: 3,509. Home of Umana Reyer Venezia.
- **Palasport Pianella / PalaDesio** (Cucciago / Desio) — Capacity: 3,910 / 6,700. Historic home of Pallacanestro Cantù.
- **PalaMaggiò** (Caserta) — Capacity: 6,387. Home of JuveCaserta.

### Germany
- **ratiopharm arena** (Neu-Ulm) — Capacity: 6,000. Home of ratiopharm ulm (2023 German BBL champions).
- **Inselpark Arena (Edel-optics.de Arena)** (Hamburg) — Capacity: 3,400. Home of Veolia Towers Hamburg.
- **Audi Dome (BMW Park)** (Munich) — Capacity: 6,700. Home of FC Bayern Munich during early EuroCup years.
- **Telekom Dome** (Bonn) — Capacity: 6,000. Home of Telekom Baskets Bonn.

### France
- **Astroballe** (Villeurbanne / Lyon) — Capacity: 5,560. Home of LDLC ASVEL.
- **Rhénus Sport** (Strasbourg) — Capacity: 6,166. Home of SIG Strasbourg; 2016 Eurocup Finalists.
- **Palais des Sports de Beaublanc** (Limoges) — Capacity: 5,516. Home of Limoges CSP.
- **Salle Gaston Médecin** (Monaco) — Capacity: 5,000. Home of AS Monaco (2021 7DAYS EuroCup champions).

### Adriatic & Balkan Region
- **Morača Sports Center** (Podgorica, Montenegro) — Capacity: 6,000. Home of Budućnost VOLI.
- **Arena Stožice** (Ljubljana, Slovenia) — Capacity: 12,480. Home of Cedevita Olimpija.
- **Hala Tivoli** (Ljubljana, Slovenia) — Capacity: 5,600. Historic venue of Olimpija Ljubljana.
- **Aleksandar Nikolić Hall (Pionir)** (Belgrade, Serbia) — Capacity: 8,000. Home of Partizan and Crvena zvezda.
- **Krešimir Ćosić Hall (Višnjik)** (Zadar, Croatia) — Capacity: 9,000. Home of KK Zadar.
- **Dražen Petrović Basketball Hall** (Zagreb, Croatia) — Capacity: 5,400. Home of KK Cibona and Cedevita.

### Turkey
- **Tofaş Nilüfer Spor Salonu** (Bursa) — Capacity: 7,500. Home of Tofaş and Frutti Extra Bursaspor.
- **BJK Akatlar Arena** (Istanbul) — Capacity: 3,200. Home of Beşiktaş Fibabanka.
- **Sinan Erdem Dome** (Istanbul) — Capacity: 16,000. Hosted marquee Turkish EuroCup fixtures.

### Eastern & Northern Europe
- **Švyturys Arena** (Klaipėda, Lithuania) — Capacity: 6,200. Home of Neptūnas Klaipėda.
- **Cido Arena (Kalnapilio Arena)** (Panevėžys, Lithuania) — Capacity: 5,950. Home of 7bet-Lietkabelis.
- **Hala Tivoli / Centennial Hall (Hala Stulecia)** (Wrocław, Poland) — Capacity: 6,000. Home of Śląsk Wrocław.
- **Copper Box Arena** (London, United Kingdom) — Capacity: 6,000. Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park venue for London Lions.
- **Arena Riga** (Riga, Latvia) — Capacity: 11,200. Host for Prometey (Ukraine) during displacement.
