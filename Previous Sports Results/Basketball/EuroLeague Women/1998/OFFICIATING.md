# EuroLeague Women Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1998 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** EuroLeague Women
- **Season:** 1998
- **Governing Body:** FIBA Europe

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIBA Europe Women's Officiating Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1998 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIBA Europe.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
