# FIBA AmeriCup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1993 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** FIBA AmeriCup
- **Season:** 1993
- **Governing Body:** FIBA Americas

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIBA Americas Referee Crew.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1993 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIBA Americas.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
