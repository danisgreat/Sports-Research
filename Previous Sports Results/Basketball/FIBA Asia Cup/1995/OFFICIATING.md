# FIBA Asia Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1995 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** FIBA Asia Cup
- **Season:** 1995
- **Governing Body:** FIBA Asia

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIBA Asia Referee Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1995 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIBA Asia.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
