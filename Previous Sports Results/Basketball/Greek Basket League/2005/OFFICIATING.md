# Greek Basket League Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2005 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** Greek Basket League
- **Season:** 2005
- **Governing Body:** Hellenic Basketball Federation (EOK) / HEBA

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Greek Basketball Referee Association.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2005 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Hellenic Basketball Federation (EOK) / HEBA.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
