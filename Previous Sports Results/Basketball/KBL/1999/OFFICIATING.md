# KBL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1999 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** KBL
- **Season:** 1999
- **Governing Body:** Korean Basketball League (KBL)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** KBL Referee Department.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1999 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Korean Basketball League (KBL).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
