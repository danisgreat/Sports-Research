# Korean Basketball League (KBL) — Historical Players & Rosters Register (1975–2026)

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition / League:** Korean Basketball League (KBL / 한국프로농구) & Basketball Festival (농구대잔치)
- **Governing Body:** KBL (한국농구연맹) / Korea Basketball Association (KBA / 대한민국농구협회)
- **Geographic Coverage:** Republic of Korea (South Korea)

---

## 1. All-Time Korean Basketball Legends (Hall of Fame Caliber)

### 1.1 The "Hur-Dong-Taek" Trio & Kia Dynasty
- **Hur Jae (허재):** "The President of Basketball" (농구대통령). 1997–98 Finals MVP (only runner-up Finals MVP in KBL history), 2-time KBL Champion (1997, 2003–04), 7-time Basketball Festival Champion with Kia Motors. Historic 1996 Atlanta Olympics single-game scoring record (62 pts vs Egypt).
- **Kang Dong-hee (강동희):** "The Magic Passer". Inaugural 1997 KBL Regular Season & Finals MVP. Master floor general with pinpoint court vision.
- **Kim Yoo-taek (김유택):** Premier post scorer, rebounder, and shot blocker; anchor of the Kia Motors frontcourt alongside Han Ki-bum.
- **Han Ki-bum (한기범):** 2.07m tower center for Kia Motors, leading rebounder throughout the 1980s and 1990s.

### 1.2 The Scoring Kings of the 1980s
- **Lee Choong-hee (이충희):** Hyundai Electronics legend. Renowned as the greatest pure shooter in Asian basketball history. Averaged over 30 points per game in multiple seasons.
- **Kim Hyun-jun (김현준):** "The Electronic Scorer" (전자슈터). Samsung Electronics sharpshooter, legendary 10-time Basketball Festival All-Star and leading scorer.

### 1.3 The 1990s College Boom Icons
- **Lee Sang-min (이상민):** "Computer Guard". 2-time KBL Regular Season MVP (1997–98, 1998–99), 2003–04 Finals MVP, 9-time consecutive KBL All-Star voting leader. Yonsei University and Daejeon Hyundai/KCC Egis icon.
- **Moon Kyung-eun (문경은):** "Rambo Shooter". Yonsei University and Seoul Samsung/SK Knights sharpshooter.
- **Woo Ji-won (우지원):** "Prince of the Court". Yonsei University star, KBL All-Star.
- **Seo Jang-hoon (서장훈):** "National Center" (국보급 센터). All-time leading scorer in KBL history (13,231 career points) and all-time leading rebounder (5,235 rebounds). 2-time MVP (1999–00, 2005–06).
- **Hyun Joo-yup (현주엽):** "Magic Hippo". Korea University power forward with elite playmaking abilities.
- **Jeon Hee-chul (전희철):** "Air Hee-chul". Korea University and Daegu Tongyang Orions / Seoul SK Knights high-flying forward.

### 1.4 Modern KBL Superstars (2000s–2020s)
- **Kim Joo-sung (김주성):** Wonju TG Sambo / Dongbu Promy franchise pillar. 3-time KBL Champion, 2-time Regular Season MVP, 2-time Finals MVP. The premier defensive rim protector in league history.
- **Yang Dong-geun (양동근):** Ulsan Mobis Phoebus dynasty captain. 6-time KBL Champion, 4-time Regular Season MVP, 3-time Finals MVP.
- **Kim Sun-hyung (김선형):** Seoul SK Knights floor leader, 2012–13 and 2021–22 Regular Season MVP.
- **Oh Se-keun (오세근):** "Lion King". Anyang KGC Ginseng Corporation center, 3-time Finals MVP (2011–12, 2016–17, 2022–23).
- **Heo Ung (허웅) & Heo Hoon (허훈):** Sons of Hur Jae; leading stars of Busan KCC Egis and Suwon KT Sonicboom; perennial All-Star voting leaders.
- **Ha Seung-jin (하승진):** 2.21m center, first South Korean player to play in the NBA (Portland Trail Blazers); 2-time KBL Champion with Jeonju KCC Egis (2008–09, 2010–11 Finals MVP).

## 2. Iconic Foreign Players (Import Legends)
- **Johnny McDowell (조니 맥도웰):** Daejeon Hyundai Dynat/Gullivers powerhouse forward. 3-time Foreign Player MVP (1997–98, 1998–99, 1999–00), leading Hyundai to back-to-back titles.
- **Marcus Hicks (마르커스 힉스):** Daegu Tongyang Orions high-flying forward; 2001–02 Finals MVP; historic shot-blocking and fastbreak maestro.
- **Dante Jones (단테 존스):** Anyang SBS Stars (2004–05). Arrived mid-season and led SBS to an unprecedented 15-game winning streak.
- **Jameel Watkins (자밀 왓킨스):** Wonju TG Sambo defensive anchor (2004–2008).
- **Aaron Haynes (애런 헤인즈):** All-time leading foreign scorer in KBL history (over 10,000 career points). Played across 13 seasons (Samsung, Mobis, LG, SK, KCC).
- **Ricardo Ratliffe / Ra Gun-ah (라건아):** Naturalized South Korean national team center. 4-time KBL Champion with Ulsan Mobis and Busan KCC Egis. 3-time Foreign Player of the Year.
- **Jared Sullinger (제러드 설린저):** Former NBA Boston Celtics forward; joined Anyang KGC late in 2020–21 and swept all 10 playoff games to win the 2021 Championship (Finals MVP).
- **Ethan Alvano (이선 알바노):** First Asian Quarter player to win the KBL Regular Season MVP (2023–24 with Wonju DB Promy).
