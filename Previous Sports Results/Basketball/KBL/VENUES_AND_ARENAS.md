## 3. Pre-KBL Basketball Festival & Tournament Arenas (1975–1996)

| Arena | City | Historical Role & Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Jamsil Gymnasium (잠실실내체육관)** | Seoul | Staged the final championships of the Basketball Festival (농구대잔치) throughout the 1980s and 1990s, including legendary Kia Motors vs Hyundai and Samsung finals. |
| **Jamsil Students' Gymnasium (잠실학생체육관)** | Seoul | Primary hub for University League and Basketball Festival divisional round-robin series (Yonsei vs Korea Univ classics). |
| **Olympic Gymnasium No. 2 (올림픽제2체육관 / 펜싱경기장)** | Seoul | Staged the inaugural KBL opening game and final series in February–May 1997. |
| **Jangchung Gymnasium (장충체육관)** | Seoul | Staged National Business Basketball League (실업연맹전) tournaments throughout the 1970s and early 1980s. |
