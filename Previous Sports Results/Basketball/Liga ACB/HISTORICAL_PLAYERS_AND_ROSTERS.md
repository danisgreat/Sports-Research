## 2. Iconic Player Profiles Across Eras

### 1970s Liga Nacional Pioneers
- **Wayne Brabender:** Lethal perimeter shooter; 13-time Liga Nacional champion with Real Madrid; 1973 EuroBasket MVP.
- **Clifford Luyk:** Dominant baseline hook shot and rim protector; 14-time Liga Nacional champion with Real Madrid.
- **Walter Szczerbiak:** Unstoppable scoring machine; holds the single-game Spanish league scoring record with **65 points** for Real Madrid vs Breogán on February 8, 1976.
- **Carmelo Cabrera:** Wizardly Canary Island playmaker whose behind-the-back passes revolutionized Spanish basketball in the 1970s.
- **Nino Buscató & Josep Maria Margall:** Legendary pillars of Joventut Badalona.

### 1980s ACB Emergence & International Superstars
- **Juan Antonio San Epifanio "Epi":** The defining symbol of 1980s Spanish basketball; 7-time Liga champion with FC Barcelona; silver medalist at 1984 Los Angeles Olympics; final torchbearer at 1992 Barcelona Olympics.
- **Dražen Petrović:** "The Mozart of Basketball"; played the 1988–89 season for Real Madrid; scored 42 points in the Copa del Rey final and 62 points in the European Cup Winners' Cup final against Snaidero Caserta.
- **Audie Norris:** Relentless interior powerhouse for FC Barcelona; epic physical battles against Fernando Martín.
- **Fernando Martín:** First Spanish player to enter the NBA (Portland Trail Blazers, 1986); legendary Real Madrid center whose tragic death in 1989 immortalized his legacy.
- **Nacho Solozábal & Chicho Sibilio:** Barcelona backcourt wizards who formed the backbone of the Aíto era.

### 1990s Domination & Fairytales
- **Arvydas Sabonis:** Widely considered the most dominant center in European history; transformed Fórum Valladolid (1989–1992) and led Real Madrid to the 1993 and 1994 ACB titles and 1995 EuroLeague title.
- **Jordi Villacampa:** Cold-blooded scoring icon for Joventut Badalona; scored 48 points in a single ACB game in 1993; led Penya to 1991 and 1992 ACB crowns and 1994 EuroLeague title.
- **Joan Creus:** Master tactician who orchestrated TDK Manresa's miraculous 1998 ACB championship run at age 41; named Finals MVP.
- **Joe Arlauckas:** Explosive scoring power forward for Baskonia and Real Madrid; scored 63 points in a EuroLeague game in 1996.
- **Alberto Herreros:** Purest jump-shooter of his generation; all-time ACB scoring leader; famous for his buzzer-beating corner three in Vitoria to win the 2005 ACB title for Real Madrid.

### 2000s Golden Generation ("La Generación de Oro")
- **Pau Gasol:** Burst onto the scene in 2000–01 with FC Barcelona, capturing 2001 Copa del Rey MVP and 2001 ACB Finals MVP before being drafted #3 overall in the NBA. Returned in 2021 to win another ACB championship.
- **Juan Carlos Navarro ("La Bomba"):** 8-time ACB champion, 2006 ACB regular-season MVP, 3-time Finals MVP; master of the high-arching one-footed teardrop floater.
- **Felipe Reyes:** Exemplar of relentless rebounding, durability, and leadership; played 824 ACB games over 23 seasons with Estudiantes and Real Madrid.
- **Rudy Fernández:** Electric athlete and clutch shooter; led DKV Joventut to the 2008 Copa del Rey before NBA tenure with Portland and Denver; won 7 ACB titles with Real Madrid.
- **Ricky Rubio:** Made his ACB debut for DKV Joventut on October 15, 2005 at just **14 years, 11 months, and 24 days**; won 2008 Copa del Rey and 2010 EuroLeague before storied NBA career.
- **Luis Scola & Andrés Nocioni:** Argentine warriors who spearheaded Saski Baskonia's rise into a European powerhouse under Dusko Ivanovic.

### 2010s & 2020s Modern Era Icons
- **Sergio Llull:** Ultimate clutch performer; 2017 ACB MVP, 2-time Finals MVP; famous for half-court buzzer-beaters and cold-blooded game-winners.
- **Luka Dončić:** Joined Real Madrid academy at age 13; became youngest player to debut for Real Madrid at 16; won 2018 ACB Regular Season MVP and EuroLeague MVP at age 19 before NBA superstardom.
- **Walter "Edy" Tavares:** Cape Verdean 7-foot-3 center; 3-time ACB Best Defender, 2021 ACB Finals MVP; revolutionized rim protection across Europe.
- **Marcelinho Huertas:** Brazilian floor general who became all-time assist leader; led Lenovo Tenerife to modern heights with pick-and-roll partner Giorgi Shermadini (2-time ACB MVP).
- **Facundo Campazzo:** Tenacious Argentine playmaker; 2-time ACB Finals MVP (2019, 2024); engine of Real Madrid's transition game.
- **Nikola Mirotić:** 2-time ACB Regular Season MVP (2013 with Real Madrid, 2020 with FC Barcelona); 2021 and 2023 Finals MVP with Barcelona.
