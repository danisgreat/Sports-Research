# Spanish Top-Flight Basketball: Historical Teams, Franchises & Rosters (1975–2026)

## 1. Overview of Spanish Basketball Competitions
Spanish men's top-flight basketball is organized across three primary eras and four major domestic competitions:
1. **Liga Nacional (1957–1983):** Operated by the Federación Española de Baloncesto (FEB). Double round-robin league of 12 to 14 teams with two 20-minute halves and draws permitted in regular-season play.
2. **Liga ACB (1983–present):** Independent top-flight professional league founded by the Asociación de Clubes de Baloncesto (ACB). Introduced playoffs in 1983–84, 3-point field goals in 1984–85, four 10-minute quarters in 2000–01, and unified 18-team single-table format in 1994–95.
3. **Copa del Rey de Baloncesto (1933–present):** Premier national knockout cup competition. Since 1987, structured as an elite 8-team mid-season tournament held over four days in February.
4. **Supercopa de España de Baloncesto (Endesa) (1984–1987, 2004–present):** Annual season-opening tournament featuring four clubs: the Liga ACB champion, Copa del Rey champion, host team, and top European/ACB contender.

## 2. Comprehensive Franchise Registry & Lineages

### Real Madrid Baloncesto
- **Established:** 1931
- **Canonical Home Arena:** WiZink Center (Palacio de Deportes de la Comunidad de Madrid), Madrid
- **Historical Arenas:** Pabellón de la Ciudad Deportiva (Pabellón Raimundo Saporta, 1966–2004), Palacio Vistalegre (2004–2010), Caja Mágica (2010–2011)
- **Domestic League Titles (1975–2026):** 1975–76, 1976–77, 1978–79, 1979–80, 1981–82, 1983–84, 1984–85, 1985–86, 1992–93, 1993–94, 1999–2000, 2004–05, 2006–07, 2012–13, 2014–15, 2015–16, 2017–18, 2018–19, 2021–22, 2023–24
- **Copa del Rey Titles (1975–2026):** 1977, 1985, 1986, 1989, 1993, 2012, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2020, 2024
- **Supercopa Titles:** 1984–85, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024
- **Key Historical Eras:**
  - *1970s Golden Era:* Coached by Pedro Ferrándiz and Lolo Sainz; led by Wayne Brabender, Clifford Luyk, Walter Szczerbiak, Carmelo Cabrera, Rafael Rullán.
  - *Dražen Petrović Miracle Season (1988–89):* Dramatic Copa del Rey title and 62-point Cup Winners' Cup final.
  - *Arvydas Sabonis Era (1992–1995):* Dominant interior presence leading to 1993 and 1994 ACB titles and 1995 EuroLeague crown.
  - *Pablo Laso Dynasty (2011–2022):* Fast-paced modern revolution winning 6 Liga ACB titles, 6 Copas del Rey, and 2 EuroLeagues behind Sergio Llull, Felipe Reyes, Rudy Fernández, Sergio Rodríguez, and Luka Dončić.
  - *Chus Mateo Era (2022–present):* 2023 EuroLeague champions, 2023–24 ACB champions, anchored by Walter "Edy" Tavares and Facundo Campazzo.

### FC Barcelona Bàsquet
- **Established:** 1926
- **Canonical Home Arena:** Palau Blaugrana, Barcelona
- **Historical Sponsorship Aliases:** Winterthur FCB, Regal FC Barcelona, FC Barcelona Lassa
- **Domestic League Titles (1975–2026):** 1980–81, 1982–83, 1986–87, 1987–88, 1988–89, 1989–90, 1994–95, 1995–96, 1996–97, 1998–99, 2000–01, 2002–03, 2003–04, 2008–09, 2010–11, 2011–12, 2013–14, 2020–21, 2022–23
- **Copa del Rey Titles (1975–2026):** 1978, 1979, 1980, 1981, 1982, 1983, 1987, 1988, 1991, 1994, 2001, 2003, 2007, 2010, 2011, 2013, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2022
- **Supercopa Titles:** 1987–88, 2004, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2015
- **Key Historical Eras:**
  - *Aíto García Reneses Era (1985–2001):* Modernized Spanish coaching; led by Juan Antonio San Epifanio "Epi", Nacho Solozábal, Chicho Sibilio, Audie Norris, and Andrés Jiménez.
  - *The Pau Gasol Breakthrough (1999–2001):* Explosive sweep of the 2001 Liga ACB and Copa del Rey before NBA departure.
  - *Juan Carlos Navarro "La Bomba" Era (1997–2018):* All-time ACB scoring leader; won 8 ACB championships alongside Dejan Bodiroga, Gregor Fučka, Ricky Rubio, and Ante Tomić.
  - *Šarūnas Jasikevičius Era (2020–2023):* ACB titles in 2021 and 2023 featuring Nikola Mirotić, Nick Calathes, and Jan Veselý.

### Saski Baskonia
- **Established:** 1959
- **Canonical Home Arena:** Fernando Buesa Arena (Pabellón Araba), Vitoria-Gasteiz
- **Historical Sponsorship Aliases:** Vasconia Schweppes, Caja de Álava, Taugrés Baskonia, TAU Cerámica, Caja Laboral, Laboral Kutxa, Kirolbet Baskonia, Bitci Baskonia, Cazoo Baskonia
- **Domestic League Titles:** 2001–02, 2007–08, 2009–10, 2019–20
- **Copa del Rey Titles:** 1995, 1999, 2002, 2004, 2006, 2009
- **Supercopa Titles:** 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008
- **Key Historical Eras:**
  - *Josean Querejeta Presidency & Scouting Miracle:* Unrivaled international scouting pipeline identifying South American and Balkan talent (Luis Scola, Andrés Nocioni, Fabricio Oberto, Pablo Prigioni, Tiago Splitter, Arvydas Macijauskas, Mirza Teletović).
  - *Dusko Ivanovic "Carácter Baskonia" (2000–2005, 2008–2012, 2019–2021, 2023–2024):* Relentless physical defense and transition basketball resulting in four Liga ACB championships, culminating in the heroic 2020 Valencia Bubble title over FC Barcelona (69–67).

  - *Lolo Sainz & Željko Obradović Championships (1990–1994):* Back-to-back ACB champions (1991, 1992) and 1994 EuroLeague champions in Tel Aviv.
  - *DKV Joventut Resurgence (2006–2008):* Coached by Aíto García Reneses; won the 2008 Copa del Rey and ULEB Cup with teenage sensation Ricky Rubio and MVP Rudy Fernández.

## 3. Roster Regulations & Historical Player Eligibility
1. **Pre-Bosman Era (1975–1996):**
   - Maximum 2 non-Spanish (foreign) players per squad.
   - Naturalized Spanish players (e.g. Wayne Brabender, Clifford Luyk, Chicho Sibilio) were critical to domestic dominance.
2. **Bosman Ruling & Cotonou Agreement (1996–present):**
   - Complete freedom of movement for European Union (EU) citizens.
   - The *Cotonou Agreement* (and Kolpak ruling) granted European equivalent status to players from African, Caribbean, and Pacific states.
3. **Cupos de Formación (Homegrown Player Quota):**
   - Introduced by ACB and FEB to preserve Spanish talent development.
   - Every active 12-man game roster must include at least 4 homegrown players ("jugadores formados localmente") who spent a minimum of 3 seasons between ages 14 and 20 in an affiliated Spanish club.
