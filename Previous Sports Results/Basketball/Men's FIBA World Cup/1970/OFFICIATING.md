# Men's FIBA World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1970 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** Men's FIBA World Cup
- **Season:** 1970
- **Governing Body:** FIBA (International Basketball Federation)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIBA World Cup Elite Referee Crew.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1970 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIBA (International Basketball Federation).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
