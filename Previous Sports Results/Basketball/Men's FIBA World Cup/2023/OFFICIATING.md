# Men's FIBA World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2023 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** Men's FIBA World Cup
- **Season:** 2023
- **Governing Body:** FIBA (International Basketball Federation)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIBA World Cup Elite Referee Crew.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2023 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIBA (International Basketball Federation).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
