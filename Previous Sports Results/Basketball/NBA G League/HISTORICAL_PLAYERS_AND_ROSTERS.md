## 3. Notable Player Alumni & Developmental Pathways

1. **Two-Way Contract Pioneers:** Introduced in 2017, the two-way contract fundamentally altered the league's player development ecosystem. Notable standouts including **Alex Caruso** (South Bay Lakers), **Duncan Robinson** (Sioux Falls Skyforce), **Lu Dort** (Oklahoma City Blue), **Chris Boucher** (Raptors 905), and **Austin Reaves** utilized G League developmental reps to establish long-term NBA starter roles.
2. **NBA G League Ignite Pipeline:** From 2020 to 2024, NBA G League Ignite provided an alternative professional pathway for elite high school prospects, producing high NBA Draft selections including **Jalen Green** (2021 No. 2), **Jonathan Kuminga** (2021 No. 7), **Dyson Daniels** (2022 No. 8), **Scoot Henderson** (2023 No. 3), and **Matas Buzelis** (2024 No. 11).
3. **Slam Dunk & Call-Up Phenomena:** **Mac McClung** became the first player to win consecutive NBA Slam Dunk Contests while actively rostered in the G League (2023, 2024), subsequently earning MVP honors and leading Greensboro to the 2026 G League Championship.
