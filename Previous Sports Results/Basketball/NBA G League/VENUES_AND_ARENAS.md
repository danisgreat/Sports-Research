## 2. Historical & Neutral Showcase Venues

### 2.1 Major In-Season Tournament & Showcase Sites
- **Mandalay Bay Resort & Casino / Michelob ULTRA Arena (Las Vegas, NV):** Long-time host of the NBA G League Winter Showcase, Showcase Cup Championship, and D-League All-Star Games (2007–2017).
- **Orange County Convention Center / Hyatt Regency (Orlando, FL):** Host of the 2023 Winter Showcase and Showcase Cup Finals.
- **Santa Cruz Civic Auditorium (Santa Cruz, CA):** Historic interim home of the Santa Cruz Warriors prior to the Kaiser Permanente Arena buildout.
- **Columbus Civic Center (Columbus, GA):** Host of the inaugural 2005 D-League Showcase.

### 2.2 The 2020–21 Single-Site COVID-19 Bubble
- **AdventHealth Arena (ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex, Lake Buena Vista, FL):** Staged all 142 games of the condensed 2020–21 single-site season (135 regular-season games + 7 single-elimination playoff games) under strict closed-door health protocols from February 10 to March 11, 2021. Lakeland Magic won the championship.

### 2.3 Historical Charter & Defunct Grounds (2001–2016)
- **BI-LO Center (Greenville, SC):** Home of the inaugural 2001–02 champion Greenville Groove (Capacity: 15,951).
- **Mobile Civic Center (Mobile, AL):** Home of the 2002–03 champion Mobile Revelers (Capacity: 10,112).
- **North Charleston Coliseum (North Charleston, SC):** Home of the North Charleston Lowgators (Capacity: 10,537).
- **Asheville Civic Center (Asheville, NC):** Home of back-to-back champions Asheville Altitude (2003–04, 2004–05; Capacity: 7,654).
- **Crown Coliseum (Fayetteville, NC):** Home of charter club Fayetteville Patriots (Capacity: 9,564).
- **Von Braun Center (Huntsville, AL):** Home of the Huntsville Flight (Capacity: 7,198).
- **Roanoke Civic Center (Roanoke, VA):** Home of charter club Roanoke Dazzle (Capacity: 9,828).
- **Broomfield Event Center (Broomfield, CO):** Home of the Colorado 14ers (2006–2009; Capacity: 6,000).
- **CenturyLink Arena (Boise, ID):** Home of the Idaho Stampede (2006–2016; Capacity: 5,000).
- **Bismarck Event Center (Bismarck, ND):** Home of champion Dakota Wizards (2006–2012; Capacity: 8,800).
- **DeltaPlex Arena (Walker, MI):** Former home of the Grand Rapids Drive (2014–2021; Capacity: 4,500).
- **Bob Carpenter Center (Newark, DE):** Former home of the Delaware 87ers (2013–2018; Capacity: 5,000).

## 3. Court Specifications & Playing Conditions

- **Court Surface:** High-specification grade-one maple hardwood with high-density resilient sub-floor cushioning conforming to NBA Rulebook standards.
- **Dimensions:** Length: 94 feet (28.65 m); Width: 50 feet (15.24 m).
- **Basket Specifications:** Rim height 10 feet (3.05 m); inner rim diameter 18 inches (45.7 cm) with breakaway spring-flex mechanism.
- **Three-Point Line:** 23 feet 9 inches (7.24 m) at the top of the key; 22 feet (6.71 m) at the corner sidelines.
- **Restricted Area Arc:** 4-foot radius (1.22 m) centered below the basket.
- **Free Throw Line:** 15 feet (4.57 m) from the plane of the backboard face.
- **Shot Clock:** Standard 24-second shot clock (resetting to 14 seconds on offensive rebounds).
- **Ball Pressure & Specification:** Official Wilson NBA G League leather basketball inflated to 7.5 to 8.5 psi.
