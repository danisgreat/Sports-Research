# NBA Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2001 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** NBA
- **Season:** 2001
- **Governing Body:** National Basketball Association Officiating Department

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** NBA Referee Roster & NBA Replay Center (Secaucus NJ).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2001 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by National Basketball Association Officiating Department.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
