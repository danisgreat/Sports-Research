# PBA Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1990 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** PBA
- **Season:** 1990
- **Governing Body:** Philippine Basketball Association (First professional basketball league in Asia)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** PBA Technical and Officiating Committee.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1990 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Philippine Basketball Association (First professional basketball league in Asia).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
