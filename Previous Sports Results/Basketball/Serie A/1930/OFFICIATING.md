# Serie A Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1930 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** Serie A
- **Season:** 1930
- **Governing Body:** Lega Basket Serie A (LBA) / FIP

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** LBA Referee Commission.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1930 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Lega Basket Serie A (LBA) / FIP.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
