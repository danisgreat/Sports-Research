# WNBA Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2013 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** WNBA
- **Season:** 2013
- **Governing Body:** Women's National Basketball Association Officiating Department

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** WNBA Referee Roster and Replay Operations.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2013 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Women's National Basketball Association Officiating Department.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
