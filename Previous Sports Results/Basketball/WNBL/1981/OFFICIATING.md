# WNBL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1981 Season

- **Sport:** Basketball
- **Competition:** WNBL
- **Season:** 1981
- **Governing Body:** Women's National Basketball League (Australia) / Basketball Australia

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** WNBL Referee Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1981 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Women's National Basketball League (Australia) / Basketball Australia.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
