# CFL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1972 Season

- **Sport:** Canadian Football
- **Competition:** CFL
- **Season:** 1972
- **Governing Body:** Canadian Football League Officiating Department

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** CFL Officiating Crew (Referee, Umpire, Head Linesman, Line Judge, Side Judge, Back Judge, Field Judge) & CFL Command Centre.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1972 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Canadian Football League Officiating Department.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
