# Grey Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2019 Season

- **Sport:** Canadian Football
- **Competition:** Grey Cup
- **Season:** 2019
- **Governing Body:** Canadian Rugby Union / Canadian Football League

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Grey Cup Senior Officiating Crew and Command Centre Official.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2019 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Canadian Rugby Union / Canadian Football League.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
