# College Football Playoff Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2014 Season

- **Sport:** College Football
- **Competition:** College Football Playoff
- **Season:** 2014
- **Governing Body:** College Football Playoff Administration / NCAA

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** CFP Championship Neutral-Conference Officiating Crew.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2014 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by College Football Playoff Administration / NCAA.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
