# Cotton Bowl Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1940 Season

- **Sport:** College Football
- **Competition:** Cotton Bowl
- **Season:** 1940
- **Governing Body:** Cotton Bowl Athletic Association

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Cotton Bowl Classic Officiating Crew.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1940 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Cotton Bowl Athletic Association.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
