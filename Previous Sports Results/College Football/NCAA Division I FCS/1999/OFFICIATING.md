# NCAA Division I FCS Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1999 Season

- **Sport:** College Football
- **Competition:** NCAA Division I FCS
- **Season:** 1999
- **Governing Body:** NCAA Division I Football Championship Committee

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FCS Championship Officiating Crew.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1999 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by NCAA Division I Football Championship Committee.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
