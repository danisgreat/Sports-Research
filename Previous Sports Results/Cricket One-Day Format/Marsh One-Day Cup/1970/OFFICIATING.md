# Marsh One-Day Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1970 Season

- **Sport:** Cricket One-Day Format
- **Competition:** Marsh One-Day Cup
- **Season:** 1970
- **Governing Body:** Cricket Australia

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Cricket Australia National Umpire Panel (On-field & Third Umpire).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1970 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Cricket Australia.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
