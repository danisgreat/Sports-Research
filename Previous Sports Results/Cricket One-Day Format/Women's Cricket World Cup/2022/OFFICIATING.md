# Women's Cricket World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2022 Season

- **Sport:** Cricket One-Day Format
- **Competition:** Women's Cricket World Cup
- **Season:** 2022
- **Governing Body:** International Women's Cricket Council (IWCC) / ICC

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** ICC International Women's Umpire Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2022 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by International Women's Cricket Council (IWCC) / ICC.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
