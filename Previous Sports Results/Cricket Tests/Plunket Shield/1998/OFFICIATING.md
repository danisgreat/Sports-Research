# Plunket Shield Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1998 Season

- **Sport:** Cricket Tests
- **Competition:** Plunket Shield
- **Season:** 1998
- **Governing Body:** New Zealand Cricket (NZC)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** NZC National Umpire Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1998 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by New Zealand Cricket (NZC).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
