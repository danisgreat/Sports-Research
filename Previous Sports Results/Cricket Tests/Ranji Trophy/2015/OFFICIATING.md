# Ranji Trophy Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2015 Season

- **Sport:** Cricket Tests
- **Competition:** Ranji Trophy
- **Season:** 2015
- **Governing Body:** Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** BCCI All-India First-Class Umpire Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2015 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
