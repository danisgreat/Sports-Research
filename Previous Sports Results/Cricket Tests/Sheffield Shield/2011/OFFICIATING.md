# Sheffield Shield Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2011 Season

- **Sport:** Cricket Tests
- **Competition:** Sheffield Shield
- **Season:** 2011
- **Governing Body:** Cricket Australia

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Cricket Australia First-Class Umpire Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2011 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Cricket Australia.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
