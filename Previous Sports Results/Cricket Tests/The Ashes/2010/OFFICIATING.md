# The Ashes Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2010 Season

- **Sport:** Cricket Tests
- **Competition:** The Ashes
- **Season:** 2010
- **Governing Body:** Cricket Australia & England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) / ICC

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** ICC Elite Panel Neutral Umpires (modern) / Home Board Umpires (pre-2002).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2010 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Cricket Australia & England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) / ICC.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
