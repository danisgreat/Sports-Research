# Summer Olympics Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1960 Season

- **Sport:** Hockey
- **Competition:** Summer Olympics
- **Season:** 1960
- **Governing Body:** IOC / International Hockey Federation (FIH)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Olympic Games Hockey Umpire Delegation & Video Umpires.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1960 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by IOC / International Hockey Federation (FIH).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
