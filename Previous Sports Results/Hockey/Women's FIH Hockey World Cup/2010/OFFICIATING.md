# Women's FIH Hockey World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2010 Season

- **Sport:** Hockey
- **Competition:** Women's FIH Hockey World Cup
- **Season:** 2010
- **Governing Body:** International Federation of Women's Hockey Associations (IFWHA) / FIH

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIH Women's World Cup Umpiring Delegation.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2010 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by International Federation of Women's Hockey Associations (IFWHA) / FIH.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
