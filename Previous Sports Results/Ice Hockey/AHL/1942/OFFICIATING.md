# AHL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1942 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** AHL
- **Season:** 1942
- **Governing Body:** American Hockey League Officiating Department

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** AHL Referees and Linesmen (Primary training ground for NHL officials).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1942 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by American Hockey League Officiating Department.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
