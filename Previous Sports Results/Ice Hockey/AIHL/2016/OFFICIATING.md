# AIHL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2016 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** AIHL
- **Season:** 2016
- **Governing Body:** Australian Ice Hockey League / Ice Hockey Australia

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** AIHL Referee and Linesman Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2016 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Australian Ice Hockey League / Ice Hockey Australia.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
