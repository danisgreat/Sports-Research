# Czech Extraliga Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1999 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** Czech Extraliga
- **Season:** 1999
- **Governing Body:** Czech Ice Hockey Association (ČSLH) / BPA

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** ČSLH Referee Commission (Hlavní a čároví rozhodčí).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1999 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Czech Ice Hockey Association (ČSLH) / BPA.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
