# DEL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2013 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** DEL
- **Season:** 2013
- **Governing Body:** Deutsche Eishockey Liga (DEL)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** DEL Schiedsrichter und Linienrichter Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2013 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Deutsche Eishockey Liga (DEL).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
