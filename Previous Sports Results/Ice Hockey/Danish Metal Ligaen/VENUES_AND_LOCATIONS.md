# Danish Metal Ligaen — Venues & Locations

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition / League:** Danish Metal Ligaen (Danmarksmesterskabet i ishockey / Superisligaen / Eliteserien / 1. division)
- **Governing Body:** Danmarks Ishockey Union (DIU)

---

## Historical Venues & Stadium Profiles

Danish top-flight ice hockey is contested across nine primary premier arenas located across Jutland, Funen, and Zealand:

1. **Sparekassen Danmark Isarena (Gigantium Isarena) — Aalborg**
   - **Home Club:** Aalborg Pirates (AaB Ishockey / Aalborg IK)
   - **Capacity:** 6,000 (largest dedicated ice arena in Denmark)
   - **Location:** Willy Brandts Vej 31, 9220 Aalborg Øst
   - **Surface:** Olympic size indoor ice sheet (60m × 30m)

2. **Kvik Hockey Arena — Herning**
   - **Home Club:** Herning Blue Fox (Herning IK)
   - **Capacity:** 2,554 (historic capacity up to 4,120 prior to modernization)
   - **Location:** Holingknuden 1, 7400 Herning
   - **Surface:** Indoor ice sheet, historical heartland of Danish hockey dynasties

3. **Sydjysk Sparekasse Arena (Frøs Arena / SE Arena) — Vojens**
   - **Home Club:** SønderjyskE Ishockey (IK Sønderjylland / Vojens IK)
   - **Capacity:** 5,000 (second largest ice arena in Metal Ligaen)
   - **Location:** Stadionvej 8, 6500 Vojens
   - **Surface:** Modernized 2011 ice arena replacing the historic Vojens Skøjtehal (1963)

4. **Granly Hockey Arena — Esbjerg**
   - **Home Club:** Esbjerg Energy (Esbjerg IK / Esbjerg Oilers)
   - **Capacity:** 4,378
   - **Location:** Gl. Vardevej 82, 6700 Esbjerg
   - **Surface:** Indoor ice sheet; major reconstruction completed in 2019

5. **Nordjyske Bank Arena (Scanel Hockey Arena) — Frederikshavn**
   - **Home Club:** Frederikshavn White Hawks (Frederikshavn IK)
   - **Capacity:** 4,000
   - **Location:** Skøjtealléen 4, 9900 Frederikshavn
   - **Surface:** Indoor ice sheet, famed northern hockey bastion

6. **Spar Nord Arena (Bryggeriet Vestfyen Arena / Odense Isstadion) — Odense**
   - **Home Club:** Odense Bulldogs (Odense IK)
   - **Capacity:** 3,280
   - **Location:** Møllemarksvej 75, 5200 Odense V
   - **Surface:** Indoor ice sheet; 2025 Danish Championship winning rink

7. **Holger Danske Arena (Rødovre Skøjte Arena) — Rødovre**
   - **Home Club:** Rødovre Mighty Bulls (Rødovre SIK)
   - **Capacity:** 3,600
   - **Location:** Rødovre Parkvej 425, 2610 Rødovre, Greater Copenhagen
   - **Surface:** Legendary suburban Copenhagen venue; cradle of Danish NHL talent

8. **Concordium Arena (Bitcoin Arena / Saxo Bank Arena / Hørsholm Skøjtehal) — Rungsted**
   - **Home Club:** Rungsted Seier Capital (Rungsted IK / Cobras)
   - **Capacity:** 1,350 (historic capacity 2,460)
   - **Location:** Stadionsvej 11, 2960 Rungsted Kyst
   - **Surface:** North Zealand indoor ice sheet

9. **PM Montage Arena (Herlev Skøjtehal) — Herlev**
   - **Home Club:** Herlev Eagles (Herlev IK / Herlev Hornets)
   - **Capacity:** 1,740
   - **Location:** Tvedvangen 204, 2730 Herlev, Greater Copenhagen
   - **Surface:** Metropolitan Copenhagen ice sheet

## Stadium Infrastructure & Environmental Dynamics

### 1. Ice Sheet & Rink Specifications
- **Playing Surfaces:** High-specification artificial refrigerated ice sheets, maintaining strict ice temperatures between -5°C and -7°C to optimize puck glide, edge grip, and player safety.
- **Dimensional Standards:** Standard IIHF international dimensions (60 meters length × 30 meters width) with rounded corners of 8.5m radius, tempered protective glass, seamless acrylic boards, and protective netting behind goals.

### 2. Environmental & Climate Controls
- **Indoor Climate Management:** Industrial desiccant dehumidification systems prevent fog formation and condensation drips. HVAC climate control ensures comfortable spectator concourses while preserving rigid sub-surface ice integrity.
- **Sub-Floor Refrigeration:** Glycol-chilled tubing networks embedded in reinforced concrete slabs, enabling year-round ice availability for preseason preparation and late postseason play.

### 3. Spectator Capacity & Atmosphere
- **Capacity Range:** Facilities range from boutique, intimate metropolitan venues (1,350 to 1,740 capacity) to premier regional arenas accommodating 4,000 to 6,000 spectators.
- **Matchday Operations:** Integrated video jumbotrons, LED perimeter boards, modern acoustic sound systems, TV broadcast lighting exceeding 1,200 lux, and dedicated press/media booths.
