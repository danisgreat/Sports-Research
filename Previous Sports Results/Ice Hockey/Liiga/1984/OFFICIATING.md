# Liiga Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1984 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** Liiga
- **Season:** 1984
- **Governing Body:** Jääkiekon SM-liiga Oy

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Liiga Erotuomarit (Head Referees and Linesmen).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1984 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Jääkiekon SM-liiga Oy.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
