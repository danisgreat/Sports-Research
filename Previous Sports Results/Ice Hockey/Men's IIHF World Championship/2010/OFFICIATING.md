# Men's IIHF World Championship Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2010 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** Men's IIHF World Championship
- **Season:** 2010
- **Governing Body:** International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** IIHF World Championship Officiating Team.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2010 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
