# NHL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2023 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** NHL
- **Season:** 2023
- **Governing Body:** National Hockey League Officiating Department

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** NHL Referees, Linesmen, and NHL Situation Room (Toronto).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2023 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by National Hockey League Officiating Department.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
