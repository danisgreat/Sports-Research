# SHL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2003 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** SHL
- **Season:** 2003
- **Governing Body:** Swedish Hockey League (SHL)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** SHL Domare och Linjemän.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2003 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Swedish Hockey League (SHL).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
