# Swiss National League Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2021 Season

- **Sport:** Ice Hockey
- **Competition:** Swiss National League
- **Season:** 2021
- **Governing Body:** Swiss Ice Hockey Federation (SIHF) / National League AG

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** SIHF Head Referees and Linesmen.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2021 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Swiss Ice Hockey Federation (SIHF) / National League AG.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
