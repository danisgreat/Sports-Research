# Previous Sports Results (1900–2025)

This archive organizes historical sports results across years, disciplines, and competition tiers.

---

## Directory Structure

Each year from **1900** to **2025** is organized with the following directory structure:

```
Previous Sports Results/
  └── <Year>/                    # 1900 through 2025 (126 years)
        └── <Sport>/             # 16 sport categories
              ├── international/ # International-level competitions (Olympics, World Cups, Continental Championships)
              └── national/      # National & Club-level competitions (Domestic leagues, multi-country club tournaments)
```

## Competition Level Guidelines

### 1. `international`
National team events and global governing body championships:
- **Summer Olympics & Winter Olympics** (Basketball, Ice Hockey, Tennis, Baseball, etc.)
- **World Cups & World Championships**:
  - FIBA Basketball World Cup (Men's & Women's)
  - Men's & Women's Cricket World Cup (One-Day format)
  - Men's & Women's T20 World Cup
  - ICC Champions Trophy
  - Men's & Women's Rugby World Cup (Union)
  - Men's & Women's Rugby League World Cup
  - IIHF World Championship (Ice Hockey)
  - Field Hockey World Cup & FIH Pro League
- **Continental Championships**:
  - EuroBasket, FIBA AmeriCup, FIBA Asia Cup
  - Rugby Europe Championship, The Rugby Championship, Six Nations
  - Davis Cup & Billie Jean King Cup (Tennis national representation)

### 2. `national`
Domestic leagues, club tournaments, and multi-country club leagues:
- **Basketball**: NBA, WNBA, G League, NBL (Australia & NZ), EuroLeague, EuroCup, Liga ACB, BSL, Serie A, LNB Élite, CBA, B.League, KBL, PBA
- **Baseball**: MLB, Minor League Baseball, NPB (Japan), KBO (Korea), CPBL (Taiwan), Mexican League, Australian Baseball League, Caribbean Series & Winter Leagues
- **Cricket**:
  - T20: IPL, WPL, Big Bash League (BBL), WBBL, PSL, SA20, CPL, ILT20, MLC, The Hundred
  - Tests / First Class: Sheffield Shield, County Championship, Ranji Trophy, Plunket Shield
  - One-Day: Marsh One-Day Cup, Royal London One-Day Cup, Vijay Hazare Trophy
- **Rugby League**: NRL, NRLW, State of Origin, Super League, RFL Championship, Women's Super League
- **Rugby Union**: Super Rugby Pacific, URC, European Rugby Champions Cup, Premiership Rugby, Top 14, Japan Rugby League One, Farah Palmer Cup
- **Ice Hockey**: NHL, AHL, ECHL, PWHL, KHL, SHL, Liiga, DEL, Swiss National League, Czech Extraliga, AIHL
- **Australian Rules Football**:
  - `afl`: AFL, AFLW
  - `state level afl`: VFL, SANFL, WAFL, QAFL, TSL, and state women's leagues
- **American Football**: NFL, UFL
- **College Football**: NCAA FBS, FCS, Bowl games, College Football Playoff
- **Canadian Football**: CFL (Grey Cup)
- **Tennis**: Grand Slams (Wimbledon / Lawn Tennis; French Open / Claycourt Tennis; Australian Open; US Open), ATP Tour, WTA Tour, ATP/WTA Finals
