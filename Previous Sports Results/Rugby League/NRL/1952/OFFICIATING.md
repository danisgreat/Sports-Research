# NRL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1952 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby League
- **Competition:** NRL
- **Season:** 1952
- **Governing Body:** Australian Rugby League Commission / National Rugby League

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** NRL Senior Referee Panel & NRL Bunker (Video Review).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1952 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Australian Rugby League Commission / National Rugby League.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
