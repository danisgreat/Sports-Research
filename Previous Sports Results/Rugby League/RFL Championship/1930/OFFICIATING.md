# RFL Championship Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1930 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby League
- **Competition:** RFL Championship
- **Season:** 1930
- **Governing Body:** Rugby Football League (RFL)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** RFL Match Officials Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1930 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Rugby Football League (RFL).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
