# State of Origin Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1998 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby League
- **Competition:** State of Origin
- **Season:** 1998
- **Governing Body:** NRL / QRL / NSWRL

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** State of Origin Match Referee and Bunker Officials.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1998 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by NRL / QRL / NSWRL.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
