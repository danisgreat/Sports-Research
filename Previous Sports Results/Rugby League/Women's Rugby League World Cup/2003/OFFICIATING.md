# Women's Rugby League World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2003 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby League
- **Competition:** Women's Rugby League World Cup
- **Season:** 2003
- **Governing Body:** International Rugby League (IRL)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** IRL Women's World Cup Match Officials Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2003 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by International Rugby League (IRL).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
