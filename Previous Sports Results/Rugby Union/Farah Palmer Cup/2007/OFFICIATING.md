# Farah Palmer Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2007 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby Union
- **Competition:** Farah Palmer Cup
- **Season:** 2007
- **Governing Body:** New Zealand Rugby (NZR)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** NZR Women's Referee Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2007 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by New Zealand Rugby (NZR).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
