# Men's Rugby World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2015 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby Union
- **Competition:** Men's Rugby World Cup
- **Season:** 2015
- **Governing Body:** World Rugby

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** World Rugby Elite Match Officials Panel & TMO (Foul Play Review Bunker).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2015 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by World Rugby.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
