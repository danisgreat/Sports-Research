# Premiership Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2003 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby Union
- **Competition:** Premiership
- **Season:** 2003
- **Governing Body:** Premiership Rugby / Rugby Football Union (RFU)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** RFU Professional Match Officials Team & TMO.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2003 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Premiership Rugby / Rugby Football Union (RFU).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
