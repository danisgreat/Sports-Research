# United Rugby Championship Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2009 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby Union
- **Competition:** United Rugby Championship
- **Season:** 2009
- **Governing Body:** URC / Pro Rugby Championship DAC

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** URC Elite Referee Panel & TMO.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2009 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by URC / Pro Rugby Championship DAC.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
