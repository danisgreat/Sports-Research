# Women's Rugby World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1994 Season

- **Sport:** Rugby Union
- **Competition:** Women's Rugby World Cup
- **Season:** 1994
- **Governing Body:** World Rugby

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** World Rugby Women's Elite Match Officials Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1994 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by World Rugby.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
