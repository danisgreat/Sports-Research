# A-League Men Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2006 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** A-League Men
- **Season:** 2006
- **Governing Body:** Australian Professional Leagues (APL) / Football Australia

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** A-League Elite Referee Panel & Video Assistant Referee (VAR).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2006 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Australian Professional Leagues (APL) / Football Australia.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
