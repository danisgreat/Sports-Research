# Bundesliga Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1973 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Bundesliga
- **Season:** 1973
- **Governing Body:** Deutsche Fußball Liga (DFL) / DFB

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** DFB Elite Schiedsrichter & Video Assist Center (Cologne).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1973 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Deutsche Fußball Liga (DFL) / DFB.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
