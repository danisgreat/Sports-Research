## Historical Timeline & Status (1900–2025)

- **Pre-Founding Era (1900 to 1962):**
  The competition had not yet been formally established. No sanctioned league matches, rosters, or player contracts existed prior to 1963.

- **Active Championship Era (1963 to 2025):**
  Full player registrations, franchise lists, award winners, and championship squads are documented on a season-by-season basis.
  *Note on season overlap:* For competitions operating on a split-calendar schedule (e.g. 2025–26 seasons), active rosters and early 2026 postseason matches are recorded within the 2025 season register.

- **Suspensions, Strikes & Inactive Periods:**
  Any seasons interrupted by geopolitical conflicts (World War I, World War II), labor disputes, structural reorganizations, or public health emergencies (such as the 2020 pandemic) are explicitly detailed in their respective season directories.
