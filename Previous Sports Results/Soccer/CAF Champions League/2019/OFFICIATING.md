# CAF Champions League Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2019 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** CAF Champions League
- **Season:** 2019
- **Governing Body:** Confédération Africaine de Football (CAF)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** CAF Elite Referee Panel & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2019 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Confédération Africaine de Football (CAF).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
