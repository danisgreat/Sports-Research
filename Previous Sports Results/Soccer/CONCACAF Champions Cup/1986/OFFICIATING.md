# CONCACAF Champions Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1986 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** CONCACAF Champions Cup
- **Season:** 1986
- **Governing Body:** CONCACAF

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** CONCACAF Elite Referee Panel & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1986 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by CONCACAF.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
