# FIFA Club World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2017 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** FIFA Club World Cup
- **Season:** 2017
- **Governing Body:** FIFA

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIFA International Match Officials Delegation & Semi-Automated Offside Technology (SAOT).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2017 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIFA.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
