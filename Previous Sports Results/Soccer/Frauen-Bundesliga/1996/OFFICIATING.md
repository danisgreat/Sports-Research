# Frauen-Bundesliga Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1996 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Frauen-Bundesliga
- **Season:** 1996
- **Governing Body:** Deutscher Fußball-Bund (DFB)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** DFB Frauen-Schiedsrichterinnen Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1996 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Deutscher Fußball-Bund (DFB).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
