# La Liga Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1954 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** La Liga
- **Season:** 1954
- **Governing Body:** Liga Nacional de Fútbol Profesional (LALIGA) / RFEF

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Comité Técnico de Árbitros (CTA) & Sala VOR (Las Rozas).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1954 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Liga Nacional de Fútbol Profesional (LALIGA) / RFEF.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
