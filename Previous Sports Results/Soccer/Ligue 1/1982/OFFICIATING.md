# Ligue 1 Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1982 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Ligue 1
- **Season:** 1982
- **Governing Body:** Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP) / FFF

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Commission Fédérale des Arbitres (CFA) & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1982 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP) / FFF.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
