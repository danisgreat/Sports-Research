# Ligue 1 Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2019 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Ligue 1
- **Season:** 2019
- **Governing Body:** Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP) / FFF

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Commission Fédérale des Arbitres (CFA) & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2019 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Ligue de Football Professionnel (LFP) / FFF.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
