# Major League Soccer Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2010 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Major League Soccer
- **Season:** 2010
- **Governing Body:** Major League Soccer Operations / PRO (Professional Referee Organization)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** PRO Match Officials Panel & Video Assistant Referee (VAR).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2010 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Major League Soccer Operations / PRO (Professional Referee Organization).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
