# Men's Africa Cup of Nations Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1959 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Men's Africa Cup of Nations
- **Season:** 1959
- **Governing Body:** Confédération Africaine de Football (CAF)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** CAF Elite Match Officials Panel & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1959 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Confédération Africaine de Football (CAF).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
