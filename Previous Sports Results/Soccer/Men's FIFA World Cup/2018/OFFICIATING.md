# Men's FIFA World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2018 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Men's FIFA World Cup
- **Season:** 2018
- **Governing Body:** FIFA (Fédération Internationale de Football Association)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIFA World Cup Referees, Assistant Referees, Video Match Officials (VMO) & Semi-Automated Offside Technology (SAOT).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2018 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIFA (Fédération Internationale de Football Association).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
