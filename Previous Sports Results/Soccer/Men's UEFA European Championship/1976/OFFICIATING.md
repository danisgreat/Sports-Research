# Men's UEFA European Championship Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1976 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Men's UEFA European Championship
- **Season:** 1976
- **Governing Body:** Union of European Football Associations (UEFA)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** UEFA Elite Match Officials, Video Assistant Referee (VAR) & Connected Ball / SAOT.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1976 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Union of European Football Associations (UEFA).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
