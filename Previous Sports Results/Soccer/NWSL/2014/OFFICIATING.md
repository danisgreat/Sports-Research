# NWSL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2014 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** NWSL
- **Season:** 2014
- **Governing Body:** National Women's Soccer League / PRO

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** PRO Women's Match Officials & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2014 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by National Women's Soccer League / PRO.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
