# Premier League Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2008 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Premier League
- **Season:** 2008
- **Governing Body:** The Football Association (FA) / PGMOL (Professional Game Match Officials Limited)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Select Group 1 Referees, Assistant Referees & Stockley Park VAR Hub.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2008 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by The Football Association (FA) / PGMOL (Professional Game Match Officials Limited).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
