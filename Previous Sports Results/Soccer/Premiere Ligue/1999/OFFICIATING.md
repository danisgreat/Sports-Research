# Premiere Ligue Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1999 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Premiere Ligue
- **Season:** 1999
- **Governing Body:** Fédération Française de Football (FFF)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Arbitres Fédérales Féminines & FFF Officiating Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1999 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Fédération Française de Football (FFF).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
