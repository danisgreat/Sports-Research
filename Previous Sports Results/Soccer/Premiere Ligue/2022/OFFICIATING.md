# Premiere Ligue Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2022 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Premiere Ligue
- **Season:** 2022
- **Governing Body:** Fédération Française de Football (FFF)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Arbitres Fédérales Féminines & FFF Officiating Panel.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2022 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Fédération Française de Football (FFF).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
