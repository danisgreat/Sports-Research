# Serie A Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2002 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Serie A
- **Season:** 2002
- **Governing Body:** Lega Serie A / FIGC / AIA (Associazione Italiana Arbitri)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** Commissione Arbitri Nazionale (CAN A) & Lissone VAR Center.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2002 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Lega Serie A / FIGC / AIA (Associazione Italiana Arbitri).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
