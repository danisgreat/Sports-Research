# WSL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2011 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** WSL
- **Season:** 2011
- **Governing Body:** Women's Professional Leagues Limited (WPLL) / The Football Association

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** PGMOL Women's Select Group Referees.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2011 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by Women's Professional Leagues Limited (WPLL) / The Football Association.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
