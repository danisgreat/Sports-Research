# Women's FIFA World Cup Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1991 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Women's FIFA World Cup
- **Season:** 1991
- **Governing Body:** FIFA (Fédération Internationale de Football Association)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** FIFA Women's World Cup Elite Referees, Assistant Referees & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1991 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by FIFA (Fédération Internationale de Football Association).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
