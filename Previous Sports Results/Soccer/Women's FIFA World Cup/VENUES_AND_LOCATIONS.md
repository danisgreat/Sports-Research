# Women's FIFA World Cup — Venues & Locations

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition / League:** Women's FIFA World Cup
- **Governing Body:** FIFA (Fédération Internationale de Football Association)

---

## Historical Venues & Stadium Profiles

Tianhe Stadium (Guangzhou 1991), Råsunda (Stockholm 1995), Rose Bowl (Pasadena 1999 - 90,185 crowd), Commerzbank-Arena (Frankfurt 2011), BC Place (Vancouver 2015), Parc Olympique Lyonnais (2019), Stadium Australia (Sydney 2023).

---

## Stadium Infrastructure & Environmental Dynamics

### 1. Pitch / Surface & Court Conditions
- **Playing Surfaces:** High-specification natural grass, hybrid turf technology (Desso GrassMaster/SISGrass), premium indoor court hardwoods, artificial turf systems, or world-class prepared ice sheets as dictated by the sport code.
- **Dimensional Standards:** Field, court, and stadium boundary dimensions strictly comply with the governing international federation regulations.

### 2. Environmental & Climate Factors
- **Altitude Variations:** Competitions staged at elevation encounter atmospheric density variations affecting ball aerodynamics, player respiratory recovery, and projectile velocity.
- **Weather Exposure & Microclimates:** Venues feature open-air, covered canopy, or retractable roof facilities to protect playing surfaces against inclement precipitation, excessive heat, and heavy wind.

### 3. Spectator Capacity & Atmosphere
- **Capacity Range:** Historical grounds range from regional boutique stadiums with dedicated community supporters to iconic super-stadiums accommodating 50,000 to 100,000+ spectators.
- **Matchday Operations:** Integrated LED lighting, high-definition broadcast infrastructure, modern acoustic architecture, and state-of-the-art player and official preparation facilities.
