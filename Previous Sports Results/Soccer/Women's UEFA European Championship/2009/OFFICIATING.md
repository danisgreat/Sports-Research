# Women's UEFA European Championship Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2009 Season

- **Sport:** Soccer
- **Competition:** Women's UEFA European Championship
- **Season:** 2009
- **Governing Body:** UEFA

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** UEFA Women's Elite Referee Panel & VAR.
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2009 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by UEFA.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Early television broadcast reviews, fourth-official technology, and initial electronic decision support systems.
