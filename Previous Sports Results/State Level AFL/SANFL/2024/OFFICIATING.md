# SANFL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 2024 Season

- **Sport:** State Level AFL
- **Competition:** SANFL
- **Season:** 2024
- **Governing Body:** South Australian National Football League Commission

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** SANFL Umpiring Department (Field, Boundary, Goal Umpires).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 2024 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by South Australian National Football League Commission.
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Modern video adjudication systems (VAR / TMO / DRS / Video Review / Hawk-Eye / Replay Command Center) fully integrated with on-field communications.
