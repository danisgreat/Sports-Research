# WAFL Officiating & Refereeing Corps — 1992 Season

- **Sport:** State Level AFL
- **Competition:** WAFL
- **Season:** 1992
- **Governing Body:** West Australian Football Commission (WAFC)

## Officiating Structure & Adjudication
- **Appointed Officials:** WAFL Umpiring Panel (Field, Boundary, Goal).
- **Championship Appointments:** Lead match adjudicators and senior officials selected for finals and marquee tournament fixtures based on merit and performance evaluations.
- **Rule Framework & Interpretations:** Strict enforcement of the 1992 rules of the game, procedural updates, and points of emphasis issued by West Australian Football Commission (WAFC).
- **Technology & Review Systems:** Traditional field adjudication relying exclusively on the primary on-field officials.
