# All England Lawn Tennis Club, Wimbledon — Venue Profile

- **Tournament:** The Championships, Wimbledon (Grand Slam)
- **Host City:** Wimbledon, London, United Kingdom (SW19)
- **Primary Courts:**
  - Centre Court (Capacity: 14,979; Retractable roof installed 2009)
  - No. 1 Court (Capacity: 12,345; Retractable roof installed 2019)
  - No. 2 Court & No. 3 Court
- **Surface:** 100% Perennial Ryegrass (cut to exactly 8 mm).
- **Environmental Factors & Court Observations:**
  - **Grass Wear Over 14 Days:** First week: Lush, green, fast ball skid, low bounce, serve-dominated. Second week: Baseline grass completely wears down to dry, compacted soil, creating higher, uneven bounces around the baseline.
  - **Night Curfew & Humidity:** Local Merton borough council imposes a strict 11:00 PM curfew. Evening matches with the roof closed create humid greenhouse conditions, altering ball speed.
  - **Traditions:** Strict all-white clothing rule; royal box attendance; middle Sunday traditionally rested (until 2022).
