# Melbourne Park (Australian Open) — Venue Profile

- **Tournament:** Australian Open (Grand Slam)
- **Host City:** Melbourne, Victoria, Australia
- **Primary Courts:**
  - Rod Laver Arena (Capacity: 14,820; Retractable roof)
  - Margaret Court Arena (Capacity: 7,500; Retractable roof)
  - John Cain Arena / Melbourne Arena (Capacity: 10,300; Retractable roof)
  - Kia Arena & Show Court 3
- **Surface Evolution:**
  - 1905–1987: Natural Lawn Grass at Kooyong Lawn Tennis Club (and inter-state rotations).
  - 1988–2007: Rebound Ace (Cushioned polyurethane/rubber hard court; high bounce, sticky in extreme heat).
  - 2008–2019: Plexicushion Prestige (Acrylic hard court; more uniform, heat resistant).
  - 2020–2025: GreenSet Grand Slam Acrylic (Medium-fast pace; CPI ~38-41).
- **Environmental Factors & Court Observations:**
  - **Extreme Heat Policy (EHP):** January heat waves in Melbourne regularly exceed 40°C (104°F). Wet-bulb globe temperature monitors trigger mandatory 10-minute heat breaks and roof closures.
  - **Roof Closure Dynamics:** When roofs close, humidity increases, wind is eliminated, and ball flight speeds up, dramatically favoring aggressive flat ball-strikers.
