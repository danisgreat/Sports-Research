# USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center (US Open) — Venue Profile

- **Tournament:** US Open (Grand Slam)
- **Host City:** Flushing Meadows–Corona Park, Queens, New York City, USA
- **Primary Courts:**
  - Arthur Ashe Stadium (Capacity: 23,771; Largest tennis stadium in the world; Retractable roof installed 2016)
  - Louis Armstrong Stadium (Capacity: 14,053; Retractable roof installed 2018)
  - Grandstand (Capacity: 8,125) & Court 17
- **Surface Evolution:**
  - 1881–1974: Natural Grass (Newport Casino, then West Side Tennis Club in Forest Hills).
  - 1975–1977: Har-Tru Green Clay (Forest Hills).
  - 1978–2019: DecoTurf II (Fast acrylic hard court).
  - 2020–2025: Laykold Hard Court (Medium-fast acrylic; high durability, CPI ~40-42).
- **Environmental Factors & Court Observations:**
  - **Atmosphere & Acoustics:** Reverberating roar inside Arthur Ashe Stadium; aircraft noise from nearby LaGuardia Airport.
  - **Late Night Sessions:** The US Open is famous for post-midnight matches extending past 2:00 AM under floodlights. High humidity off Flushing Bay.
