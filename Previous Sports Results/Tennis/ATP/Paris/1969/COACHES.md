# ATP Paris Coaches, Hitting Partners & Team Entourage — 1969

- **Tour:** ATP
- **Tournament Location:** Paris
- **Edition:** 1969

## Coaching Structure & Entourage
- **Player Coaching Teams:** Personal traveling coaches, fitness trainers (physios), and sports psychologists accompanying the top seeds and champion.
- **On-Court Coaching Regulations:** Strict ban on in-match coaching; violations penalized under unsportsmanlike conduct.
- **Championship Team:** The coaching staff supporting the 1969 singles champion through the 7-round, 128-player draw.
