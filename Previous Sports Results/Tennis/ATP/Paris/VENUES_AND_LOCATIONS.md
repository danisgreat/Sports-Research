# Stade Roland-Garros (French Open) — Venue Profile

- **Tournament:** Roland-Garros / French Open (Grand Slam)
- **Host City:** Paris, France (Porte d'Auteuil, 16th arrondissement)
- **Primary Courts:**
  - Court Philippe-Chatrier (Capacity: 15,225; Retractable roof installed 2020, floodlights installed 2021)
  - Court Suzanne-Lenglen (Capacity: 10,068; Retractable roof installed 2024)
  - Court Simonne-Mathieu (Capacity: 5,000; Greenhouse glasshouse court in Jardin des Serres d'Auteuil)
- **Surface:** Red Clay (*Terre Battue*)
  - 5-layer composition: Crushed red brick dust (top 2mm), white crushed limestone, clinker/slag, gravel, drainpipe foundation.
- **Environmental Factors & Court Observations:**
  - **Moisture & Weather Sensitivity:** Clay is highly hygroscopic. In sunny, hot weather (>28°C), clay dries, balls bounce high, and heavy topspin (e.g. Rafael Nadal) explodes off the court. In cold, overcast, damp conditions, the clay becomes heavy, balls absorb moisture, sliding becomes sluggish, and aces/winners plummet.
  - **Endurance Requirement:** Slowest surface in professional tennis; longest average rally lengths (6-8 shots).
