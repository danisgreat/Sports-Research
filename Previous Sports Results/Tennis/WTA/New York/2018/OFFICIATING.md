# WTA New York Tournament Officiating & Refereeing — 2018

- **Tour:** WTA
- **Tournament Location:** New York
- **Edition:** 2018

## Officiating Governance
- **Tournament Referee:** Senior ITF/Grand Slam official managing the schedule of play, court allocations, and weather delays.
- **Chair Umpires:** Gold Badge and Silver Badge chair umpires assigned to center courts and marquee matches.
- **Line Calling Protocol:** Hawk-Eye Player Challenge System with 3 challenges per set.
- **Code of Conduct:** Time violation limits between points, racquet abuse, audible obscenity, and coaching regulations.
