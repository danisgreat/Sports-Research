# WTA New York Coaches, Hitting Partners & Team Entourage — 2022

- **Tour:** WTA
- **Tournament Location:** New York
- **Edition:** 2022

## Coaching Structure & Entourage
- **Player Coaching Teams:** Personal traveling coaches, fitness trainers (physios), and sports psychologists accompanying the top seeds and champion.
- **On-Court Coaching Regulations:** Off-court coaching permitted from player boxes via verbal/hand signals.
- **Championship Team:** The coaching staff supporting the 2022 singles champion through the 7-round, 128-player draw.
