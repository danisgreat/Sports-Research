# Starters, bench and availability research

**Online exact-game confirmation:** Open current official selection/lineup evidence for both sides and each applicable role in HISTORICAL_SOURCE_PROTOCOL §2. Record names, starter/bench/reserve/inactive designation, exact event, URL and publication/retrieval times. Compare conflicting reports as needed. Record CONFIRMED, PROJECTED, NOT_YET_PUBLISHED, UNAVAILABLE or CONFLICTING per field; retain the overall card lineup-state vocabulary. Last-game selection, season rosters and postgame participation never prove pregame confirmation. Recheck around actual release and immediately before issue.

**AVAIL-2026.10.16-v3.** Mandatory for every requested game under [CURRENT_RULES §5b](CURRENT_RULES.md#5-sources-event-state-and-time), including obscure, lower-tier, women's, youth, reserve and international competitions. Source routes: [SOURCES §3.11](SOURCES.md#311-field-fallback-and-official-social-use-by-sport). This is a research procedure; it does not establish predictive improvement or guarantee that teams publish all information before a request arrives.

## 1. The requirement

An operator must make a comprehensive, game-specific attempt to establish **both sides' confirmed starters/starting role, selected bench or replacements, and current injury/illness/suspension/absence information**. A first-page search, inaccessible aggregator, old roster or missing English result is never sufficient grounds to stop. A request to start a mini establishes the workflow; the checks occur again for each individual game card.

Record the work in the card's existing **Evidence receipt, Lineup state / issue step, A3, A8 and C20**. The procedure expands those existing checks; it adds no top-level card field, selection rule, probability threshold or mini format. Old issued cards and settled examples are not retrospectively upgraded.

Never manufacture confirmation to avoid `LINEUP_UNCONFIRMED`. Confirmation is a fact about an exact field and event at a particular time. A season roster establishes membership; a game-day roster establishes selection/eligibility; a starting announcement establishes starting role. None guarantees minutes, workload, health or playing time. Absence from an injury article is not evidence of fitness. A bench list does not establish starting order.

## 2. Search every relevant route

For each side and each relevant availability field, apply the following sequence. Once an authoritative exact-game field is confirmed, stop searching for duplicate proof of that field, but complete the other fields and the final late-change check. Log unavailable steps with their reason; do not pretend every step succeeded.

1. **Resolve the competition.** Confirm official competition/season/stage, participant spelling and local aliases, game ID, date and venue timezone. Find the competition's organizer/federation site and the official pages of both teams through that organizer or their own verified domains. Registration lists, competition squads and national-team squads are context, not game selections.
2. **Open the exact match.** Inspect the official match centre's lineup, roster/team sheet, substitutes, scratches/inactives and status tabs. Use the original team-sheet PDF or published image where that is how the competition releases selections. Read names and positions from the actual legible original; a thumbnail or search snippet is discovery only. Check the publication state/time and correct game.
3. **Check both teams directly.** Open each team's latest selection release and medical/availability update, press-conference report and late-change notice. Follow official social accounts only when linked by the team's or organizer's site. A verified badge or matching account name alone does not establish ownership. Read the original post and exact match context; web and social copies of one release share lineage.
4. **Check discipline and registration.** Search the organizer's suspension/disciplinary decisions, injury/availability report, withdrawals, transfers, registration and match eligibility notices. Separate injury, illness, suspension, rest, personal absence, selection omission and unknown reason. Do not diagnose an omitted player. Compare selected roster with recent relevant selections only as a discrepancy check, not as proof of unreported injury.
5. **Use a field-specific structured fallback.** Check the sport routes below and the exact ESPN/FIBA/Genius/official scorer or permitted specialist page when it covers that game. A page containing the players is insufficient: confirm its starter/bench designation, pregame state, update time and original source. Morning skate, depth chart, probable pitcher, predicted XI and estimated minutes remain projections. Postgame starter flags, participation, `didNotPlay` or game-book fields cannot prove pregame availability; require their actual pre-cutoff publication/capture. Exclude betting previews, odds, fantasy picks and tips.
6. **Search in the local language.** If a field is still missing, search the official competition name and both team names in local script, date/round/opponent and the relevant local terms. Search both the web and official-site news/archive navigation. Use translation to understand text while retaining original names, URL and faithful extract. Look for organizer-linked scorer sites, federation circulars, accredited local reports and tournament PDFs. Do not substitute a better-known similarly named league.
7. **Escalate unresolved material fields.** Use a named local reporter or accredited broadcaster with an explicit current report; trace its cited team announcement where possible. A secondary report is labelled with its provenance and confidence. Repeated aggregator copies are not corroboration. Conflicting reports stay conflicting until a newer authoritative correction resolves the field.
8. **Recheck before issue.** Reopen the decisive selection and availability sources around that competition's actual announcement window and immediately before the card is frozen. Check replacements, withdrawals, scratches and last-minute corrections for both teams, then re-price affected scenarios from the same distribution. Complete the search without a countdown deadline, even after start when needed. Apply [TIMING_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL](TIMING_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md): a release first published after start may improve the availability record but cannot establish a pre-start-known field; exclude it from CLEAN_PRE_START_INPUTS or classify the analysis appropriately.

`BLOCKED`, `JS_ONLY`, `STALE`, `WRONG_EVENT`, `RETRIEVAL_MISS` and `NOT_PUBLISHED` have different meanings. A failed text extractor does not mean the information is unpublished: use a permitted browser/rendered official page if available, official PDF/image, original club post or next authorized route. Do not bypass authentication or access controls. Record the failed route and the fallback actually read.

## 4. Obscure-competition escalation

No familiar provider coverage is required to start research. Find the actual federation/organizer and both teams. Check competition-language names, sponsorship aliases, transliterations, women's/men's and reserve/youth suffixes. Use the organizer's current fixtures to identify the exact game; a matching pair of names is not sufficient.

Search templates, adapted to the competition language:

```text
<official competition> <team> <opponent> <local date/round> lineup substitutes injury
site:<organizer domain> <team> <opponent> team sheet
site:<team domain> <opponent> injury squad late changes
<local team name> <date> <local words for starting side / absent / injured / suspended>
```

Examples of discovery terms: Japanese `スタメン`, `先発`, `欠場`, `負傷`; Korean `선발`, `라인업`, `출전선수`, `부상`, `결장`; Spanish `alineación`, `convocatoria`, `suplentes`, `parte médico`, `bajas`; French `composition`, `remplaçants`, `blessés`, `suspendus`; German `Aufstellung`, `Kader`, `Verletzte`, `Ausfälle`; Portuguese `escalação`, `relacionados`, `lesionados`; Turkish `ilk 11`, `kadro`, `sakat`, `cezalı`. These are search aids, not automatic status classifiers. Retain context and translation uncertainty.

Open federation documents, team-sheet PDFs, organizer-linked scorer services and authenticated official-team posts discovered this way. If a page is inaccessible, check organizer or team mirrors of the same release and permitted current reporting, keeping lineage explicit. Do not use guessed API endpoints, unofficial scraped dumps, fan predictions or another league's injury list to fill gaps.

The existing attempt ledger must identify both sides, fields sought, exact query/route, event/season checked, source publication/update time if available, retrieval time with offset, field/extract obtained, failure or projection label, fallback and remaining gap. For missing fields, show every relevant ladder level attempted or an exact reason it does not apply. “No results”, “obscure league”, “ESPN does not cover it” and one blocked site are not completed research.

Finding a new useful source on a game-card task does not authorize editing canonical rules. Record its identity and field evidence in the existing appendix and queue the source-register addition for a framework/source task. This preserves prompt 1's named-mini write scope.

## 5. Timing and honest disposition

The request time and lineup release time are different. Begin sporting research immediately. Use an early named side as a provisional selection until the actual competition's final team sheet or starting-role confirmation is published. Do not impose a universal 60-, 90- or 30-minute publication rule across sports. Look up the actual event's release convention; if unknown, say so and recheck the sources.

At the final recheck, complete **both sides** and **all applicable fields**. Distinguish: verified starters; verified selected bench/replacements; reported injuries/absences and unresolved participation; late-change check performed. Tennis has no bench in an ordinary singles match; mark it N/A with that basis. A team's full training squad is not a confirmed game-day bench. Complete XI/five does not make an incomplete medical report complete, and “available” does not confirm expected minutes or workload.

Use the existing lineup metadata and evidence statuses to explain the outcome:

- **CONFIRMED:** exact relevant selection/start-role evidence read for both sides, with sources/times; identify any separate unverified medical/role/workload field.
- **LINEUP_UNCONFIRMED / NOT_PUBLISHED:** after the full applicable ladder, the original selections are not yet published or essential starting roles remain projected; record which side/field and next release check.
- **NOT_VERIFIED / CONFLICTING:** access, stale/wrong-event data or conflicting evidence prevented confirmation; show failures, alternatives and material implications. Do not label an access failure NOT_PUBLISHED.
- **N/A:** the field is structurally inapplicable to the exact sport/format, with reason; no generic exemption for an obscure competition.

If a user explicitly requires an only-confirmed numeric card, prepare the research draft and wait/recheck within the active task while the event remains pregame; do not issue a numeric card whose required confirmation is absent. Otherwise current rules allow a documented, supported uncertainty mixture or qualitative/NO_FORECAST card after comprehensive research. A mixture does not turn a projection into a fact. If material availability states or weights cannot be supported, use the existing qualitative/NO_FORECAST route. Retain the requested event and missingness.

Never wait past actual start and then call a revealed lineup pregame evidence. Later information is a dated addendum/reforecast in its proper timing cohort. A retry time is a plan, not a scheduled job; disclose whether a retry actually ran and whether automation exists. A not-yet-published lineup can remain unconfirmed despite excellent research; the enforceable guarantee is completion of the search and honest evidence status.

## 6. Forecast implications and verification

Apply actual selection changes once: affected role, available rotation, batting/pitching/goalkeeping resources or substitutions. Re-price from the common distribution, show source-linked assumptions and any changed top two, and preserve baseline and candidate-line definitions. Bench membership and injury labels alone do not quantify a probability effect. Unknown minutes, pitch counts, batting order and tactical roles stay assumptions even when participants are selected.

C20 fails when the operator skips relevant searches, claims a projected/roster field is confirmed, conflates starters and bench/injury coverage, or lacks the final state/late-change recheck. An honestly documented publication/access gap passes the **research procedure** while remaining unconfirmed; it does not pass a confirmation requirement. See [verification fixtures](research/verification/framework_change_2026-10-14-4/FIXTURES.md).

This protocol should reduce avoidable missing information and unsupported availability assumptions. Its effect on predictive scores remains untested and must be measured on frozen same-event comparisons with coverage and timing reported.
