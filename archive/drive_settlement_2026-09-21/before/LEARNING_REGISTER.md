## 1. Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CANDIDATE | Observed in one or more cases; no prospective evidence yet |
| TESTING | A prospective process check is active; forecast-weight promotion additionally requires the complete v2 test manifest |
| PROMOTED_PROCESS | Required because it protects identity, data quality, coherence, or leakage; not a claim of predictive lift |
| PROMOTED_FORECAST | Prospective chronological evidence supports a forecast-weight change |
| RETIRED | Tested and removed or narrowed |
| SUPERSEDED | Replaced because it was incorrect, contradictory, or redundant |
