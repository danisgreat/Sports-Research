 Abhishek&#x27;s record century seals series sweep for India
 Abhishek Sharma led the way for India with a record ton in the third T20I against Afghanistan.
 17 September, 2026 
 View this post on Instagram
 Having shown great form with his 82 and 39 in the first two matches of the series, Abhishek Sharma carved a milestone hundred in the third game against Afghanistan, as India broke several batting records.
 View this post on Instagram
 The batter hit the fastest-ever T20I century for India, and third-fastest overall, racing to his hundred in the ninth over of the innings off merely 30 balls.
 View this post on Instagram
 His first 50 runs took merely 16 balls, making it the joint fourth-fastest fifty for India. Abhishek already has the record for the second-fastest fifty for India, a half-century against New Zealand in January this year, which came off just 14 balls.
 Abhishek Sharma’s breathtaking knock was brought to an end at 108 by Rashid Khan, just one ball before the halfway mark of the innings.
 Abhishek hit 11 sixes during his innings, the second-most by an India men&#x27;s batter in a T20I.
 View this post on Instagram
 Sanju Samson was watchful yet again at the start, just as he was in the second T20I, but eventually opened arms and took every opportunity to increase India&#x27;s scoring rate, with the duo adding 100 runs off the powerplay, a team-best for India.
 Samson eventually fell for 50 of his own, his second-consecutive fifty in the format, but Afghanistan managed to contain India&#x27;s batting after the duo had departed. From 164/2 at the 13 over mark, the team managed to add only 57 runs over the next seven overs.
 Azmatullah Omarzai was the standout bowler with his 2/27, while India were also dented by three run-outs.
 Rahmanullah Gurbaz and Ibrahim Zadran showed initiative in the chase, adding 31 runs in three overs, but Axar Patel&#x27;s double-strike in the fourth over gave the control back to India.
 Bumrah struck in the fifth over, while Yash Thakur struck in the sixth to further trouble Afghanistan.
 As the asking rate mounted, Afghanistan batters lost their way. Nitish Kumar Reddy ran through the lower-order to all but end Afghanistan&#x27;s hope, picking three wickets in a space of five balls in the 12th over. 
 Afghanistan were eventually bowled out in the 15th over.
 Ravi Bishnoi finished at 3/26, whereas Nitish Kumar had picked 3/25. 
 The two sides will now travel to Japan to feature in the men&#x27;s cricket tournament of the Asian Games . Prior to the tournament, India will feature in a historic one-off T20I against Japan on September 22.
