# 2026-06-12 System Audit — Where to Improve, What Was Added, What Remains

Scope: full read of combined_sports_doc.txt (incl. Gates 0–10 and Sections 1–12), PREDICTION_RESULTS_LOG.md (all lessons, results, template), SPORTS_CALIBRATION_LEDGER.csv, SPORTS_SOURCE_REGISTRY.md, plus the sports-research-analyst skill. All new source/access claims below were verified by live tests on 2026-06-12; anything not retested is explicitly labelled.

## A. What is already strong (do not dilute these)
1. The Gate 0–10 sequence with mandatory written outputs is the single best feature of the system — "a rule that produces no written output did not happen" is the right enforcement model, and every new rule added in this upgrade follows it.
2. The honesty layer is unusually good: raw slot rates vs penalty-ledger rates tracked separately, approximate counts labelled, OPENED/SNIPPET/ASSUMED tags, blocked sources declared rather than faked.
3. The per-sport leak gates (Section 11: MLB/AFL bottom-slot leak, cricket T20 under-bias, KBO inversion, NBA phase coinflips) are the most valuable calibration findings in the file. They directly target the adjusted-record deficit.

## B. The biggest performance lever is unchanged
The bottom slot is still the entire deficit: raw #4 wins ~49% (a coinflip) while #1 wins ~65% raw / ~82.7% on the penalty ledger. Nothing in this upgrade replaces that priority. Sections 13–15 feed Gate 9 with better inputs (counted form deltas, classified press signals, officials context), but the Section 6 slot tests, swap test, separation test, and forbidden-#4 list remain the levers that decide the record. Rolling last-20 top/bottom KPIs are still listed "Unknown" in the calibration tracker — compute them at the next sweep; that was already flagged and is still the most overdue action item.

## C. Gaps found and closed in this upgrade (v2 documents)
1. ROLLING WINDOWS WERE COLLECTED, NOT COMPARED. L5/L10/L15/L20 existed in every annex but with "(if available, move on)" opt-outs, no mandated comparison to the season average, no historical baseline definition, no divergence thresholds, no verdict, and no rule for L5-vs-L20 conflicts. → Section 13: mandatory FORM DELTA TABLE (L5/L10/L15/L20 vs SEASON vs HISTORICAL with a defined baseline hierarchy), sport-scaled thresholds (labelled as starting calibration values, recalibrated every 20 cards), verdict vocabulary, written FORM ANCHOR line, regime flag, 5-game sample-size guard, and StatMuse query templates incl. two newly observed live misparse examples.
2. PRESS-CONFERENCE COMMENTS HAD NO TAXONOMY AND NO FEEDBACK LOOP. Nine annexes said "use coach comments, cross-check, don't use alone" — but nothing classified signals, capped their weight by evidence, or measured whether they predict anything. Your exact question (defensive-identity coach saying "they need to make more shots" → does the next game go over?) has NO trustworthy public base rate in any league; anyone quoting one is guessing. → Section 14 builds the answer instead of assuming it: S1–S6 classification, direction hypothesis logged at card time, outcome graded at settlement, promotion only at ≥20 samples and ≥60% accuracy, inversion/retirement below 45%. Until then S3 colours writeups and breaks adjacent ties only — it can never flip #1/#4.
3. OFFICIALS HAD REQUIREMENTS BUT NO SOURCE MAP. → Section 15 + the registry's new officials table: verified release timings and open, no-key sources per league (NBA Today's Officials ~9am ET; MLB crew rotation + Gameday/Stats API officials block; AFLUA round PDFs; NRL Tuesday appointments via Zero Tackle/League Unlimited; EPL matchweek announcements; ICC/match-page officials for cricket). NHL (Scouting the Refs) and NFL (Football Zebras) are standard trackers NOT retested this pass — probe on first use. Hard cap: officials are environment modifiers for totals/props, never side anchors.
4. NO-API ACCESS KNOWLEDGE WAS SCATTERED ACROSS SECTIONS 3/4/8/9/10. → Section 16 consolidates per-fact-type chains and reconciles your directive: ESPN is a working rung (re-confirmed); X/Twitter and Reddit are REQUIRED CHECK TARGETS executed through the only routes that exist — official mirrors, dated snippets, r.jina.ai wording reads (SNIPPET grade), Bluesky — with the mandatory SOCIAL CHECK line. Direct logged-out X is still hard-blocked (HTTP 402) and Reddit is blocked on every automated route per your own 2026-06-10 retests; the documents now mandate the check without ever permitting a fabricated claim of coverage.
5. CROSS-SPORT STAT BLINDSPOTS. → Section 17 adds, each tied to the annex it extends: NBA schedule density (3-in-4/5-in-7), opponent-3P% luck regression, FT-rate environment, altitude, late-season minutes management; MLB times-through-order, getaway-day lineups, series-game context; NHL PDO regression; AFL venue dimensions, sub usage, codified 6-day-break decline; NRL set-restart/referee tempo; cricket dew module, ball type, standing boundary-dimension field, over-rate penalty state; soccer rotation-risk calendar, manager-change bounce, referee card/penalty rates; universal coach-tenure regime check.
6. LEDGER COULD NOT CARRY THE NEW EVIDENCE. → Ledger v2 appends nine columns (form delta, form anchor, press signal class/quote/hypothesis/outcome, officials fields); log v2 template adds the FORM DELTA TABLE, FORM ANCHOR, PRESS SIGNAL, and OFFICIALS lines; Section 18 adds the matching KPIs and threshold-recalibration loop.

## D. Residual risks (honest list — not fixable by writing more rules)
1. Process compliance, not process design, caused the June failures (the 06-10 upgrade says this itself). The new sections add four written lines per card; if time pressure causes skipping, skip Section 17 colour first, never Gates 1/2/7/9 or the Form Delta table on the decision-driving stat.
2. The press-signal module starts at zero evidence by design. Expect ~10–20 cards before any class produces a usable per-sport read; resist promoting a class off one memorable hit (Section 14.5).
3. The 13.4 divergence thresholds are operational defaults, not measured constants. They are explicitly labelled as such and self-correct via 18.4 — but treat early verdicts as provisional.
4. Source access remains volatile day-to-day (Cricbuzz/NDTV class fluctuations). The chains reduce, not eliminate, UNKNOWNs; the access-probe discipline in Section 12 still applies every session.
5. StatMuse freshness/misparse risk is real and now has two more documented live examples; the table-date validation step is not optional.
