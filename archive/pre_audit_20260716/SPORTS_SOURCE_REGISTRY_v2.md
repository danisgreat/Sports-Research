Use this registry for recurring source dependencies. Official league/team/competition sources control volatile facts when they are fresh and opened. Aggregators are discovery or cross-check sources unless the sport-specific annex says otherwise.

Retest rule: any access claim older than 30 days is stale for live/current use. Retest immediately when a source blocks, returns stale data, omits the target fixture, or conflicts with another opened source.

## Rules Provenance Upgrade

Every serious card must identify the rules authority before ranking. Operational data sources tell us what happened; rulebook and competition-regulation sources tell us what legally counts, how the market settles, and whether a player/team was eligible.

Status tags:
- `OFFICIAL`: governing body, league, club, federation, official gamebook, official scorecard, or official rulebook.
- `PROJECTED`: lineup, goalie, XI, starter, or team-list projection that is not final.
- `EXPECTED`: strong reporting or official pre-final indication, but not final.
- `UNOFFICIAL`: aggregator/stat site/beat source used for discovery or cross-check.
- `DELAYED`: source updates late or may lag the event.
- `BLOCKED`: authentication, CAPTCHA, dynamic rendering, or automation barrier.
- `ARCHIVAL`: historical source only; not enough for live/current status.
- `SNIPPET`: search/result snippet only; cannot control a #1 or #4 pick.

Rules-source rows should be retested at the start of each season, after any published rule change, and before any card where competition administration could change the answer.

### Canonical Hard Data Gate — consolidated 2026-07-11

This block supersedes the overlapping 2026-06-23 and 2026-07-07 gate restatements previously located here.

- **American-league StatMuse-first rule:** for every U.S./North American league that StatMuse covers, including NBA, WNBA, MLB, NFL, NHL, MLS, CFB, CBB, PGA and comparable leagues, query StatMuse before any fallback statistical source. Use it for every decision-driving team, player, unit, phase, opponent/H2H and home/away statistic it supports. Validate the answer sentence, competition/season, qualifier and newest table date. If a result is blocked, stale, unsupported, incomplete or misparsed, write `STATMUSE MISSING/STALE/MISPARSED`, identify the rejected query, then disclose the fallback used. Official league/team sources still control live state, availability, lineups, starters, conditions, rules and settlement.
- **All-sport four-window rule:** for every decision-driving statistic with reliable observations, document L5, L10, L15 and L20 for both teams and every available player or unit that can materially affect the listed market. This includes scoring and allowed scoring, phase splits, pace/opportunity, shooting or conversion, defence, venue/home-away, opponent tier, H2H and prop-specific production as applicable. Also show the individual results behind L5, season/year baseline, historical/career or same-level baseline, and the selected form anchor.
- If fewer than 20 observations exist, use every available observation and label the actual sample `N`. If a window or aspect cannot be built, mark it `MISSING` with the reason; do not estimate it from memory. `PARTIAL` or `MISSING` coverage caps confidence and forbids the label `comprehensive` unless the missing data is demonstrably irrelevant to every ranked market.
- Required compact output: `STAT | L5 | L10 | L15 | L20 | SEASON/YEAR | HIST/CAREER | HOME/AWAY/H2H | VERDICT`. Non-American sports use the relevant official/statistical source chain; StatMuse is optional there unless it directly covers the competition.
