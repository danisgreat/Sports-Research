# Sports source registry v4

Status: **ACTIVE DIRECTORY, NOT A GUARANTEE OF ACCESS**  
Effective date: **2026-07-17**

## 1. Rules for using this registry

This file provides source starting points and a source hierarchy. A listing does not mean a page is current, complete, accessible, or sufficient for a particular forecast. Re-test every source at the evidence cutoff.

For each prediction, record:

- source name and direct page where practical;
- fact supported;
- access time and timezone;
- page/event update time if shown;
- whether the fact is official, reported, inferred or unknown;
- conflicts with other sources.

Do not cite a home page as if it proves a specific lineup or score. Do not turn a preview into a confirmed pitch report. Search snippets and social posts are discovery tools unless independently verified.

## 2. Source hierarchy

1. Official competition, governing body, team, venue or government weather source.
2. Official event data partner or official match centre.
3. Reputable specialist statistical provider.
4. Reputable news/reporting source with named information.
5. Timestamped market page for line/price and, cautiously, live state.
6. Aggregators, snippets, anonymous social claims, and forums only as leads.

Market prices can be informative but are not proof of a lineup, injury, or final settlement. Where feasible, corroborate live state with an official feed.

## 3. Cross-sport core sources

| Need | Preferred source |
|---|---|
| Schedule/result/rules | official league, federation or competition |
| Confirmed lineup/team sheet | official competition or team channel |
| Injury/availability | official report first; reputable beat reporting second |
| Venue | official venue/competition |
| Weather | government meteorological service; venue observation |
| Settlement | exact bookmaker or platform rules plus official result |
| Line/price | exact timestamped market page |
| Statistics | official data first; documented specialist provider second |

## 4. Baseball

Official starting points:

- MLB: [MLB schedules and scores](https://www.mlb.com/scores), [MLB transactions](https://www.mlb.com/transactions)
- Minor League Baseball: [MiLB](https://www.milb.com/)
- Nippon Professional Baseball: [NPB](https://npb.jp/eng/)
- Korea Baseball Organization: [KBO](https://www.koreabaseball.com/)
- Mexican Baseball League: [official LMB site](https://lmb.com.mx/); use [MiLB Mexican League pages](https://www.milb.com/mexican) only as a secondary data representation when relevant
- World Baseball Softball Confederation: [WBSC](https://www.wbsc.org/)

Required event facts: probable/confirmed starters, lineups, bullpen workload, park/roof, weather, rules and live inning/base-out state. League identity matters: do not transfer MLB priors or calibration to LMB, NPB, KBO, college, winter or international baseball.

KBO source note: use the official KBO game centre as the final-result authority. If an official detailed box score is unavailable, a clearly labelled secondary scorecard may supply descriptive inning, player or pitch-count context only; record that limitation and never let it override the official final or substitute for a sportsbook settlement contract.

## 8. Rugby league

- Australia/New Zealand: [NRL](https://www.nrl.com/)
- England/Europe: [Super League](https://www.superleague.co.uk/), [Rugby Football League](https://www.rugby-league.com/)
- International: [International Rugby League](https://www.intrl.sport/)

Resolve the exact competition. NRL, Super League, Challenge Cup, internationals and lower divisions have different player pools, schedules and data environments. Required facts include squads, late changes, venue/weather, rules and live clock/sin-bin/interchange state.

## 10. Ice hockey and American football

- Hockey: [NHL](https://www.nhl.com/), [IIHF](https://www.iihf.com/)

Hockey requires confirmed goalie and regulation/overtime contract. American football requires quarterback and major availability, weather, drive/clock state and overtime contract.

## 11. Rugby union, tennis and volleyball

- Rugby union: [World Rugby](https://www.world.rugby/), plus the exact competition and national union
- Tennis: [ATP Tour](https://www.atptour.com/), [WTA](https://www.wtatennis.com/), [ITF](https://www.itftennis.com/)
- Volleyball: [FIVB](https://www.fivb.com/), [Volleyball World](https://en.volleyballworld.com/)

For tennis, confirm surface, format, retirement rules and serve state. For volleyball, confirm set format and lineup. For rugby, distinguish league from union and resolve card, substitution and competition rules.

## 12. Golf, motorsport and combat

- Golf: [PGA Tour](https://www.pgatour.com/), [LPGA](https://www.lpga.com/), [DP World Tour](https://www.europeantour.com/dpworld-tour/), [The R&A](https://www.randa.org/)
- Motorsport: [FIA](https://www.fia.com/) and the exact series organizer
- Combat: [UFC](https://www.ufc.com/), relevant promotion, athletic commission and official result source

Required facts vary by contract: field/cut/dead heat in golf; grid/session/classification in motorsport; weight, rounds, method and no-contest rules in combat.

## 13. Source failure protocol

When a preferred source is blocked, stale or contradictory:

1. record the failure;
2. try an official alternate or official data partner;
3. use a reputable secondary source with a limitation label;
4. reduce confidence or pass if the missing fact is material;
5. never fill the gap from memory.

Access claims expire. Do not hard-code “works” or “blocked” forever; re-test at the next request.
