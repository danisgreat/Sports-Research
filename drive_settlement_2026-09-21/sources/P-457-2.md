 Australia's first bilateral ODI series win away from home since September 2024 and it comes after back-to-back series losses to Pakistan and Bangladesh.
Australia bowled 29.1 overs of spin today - their most in a match in Africa, past the 29 at Cape Town in 1994. Their seven spin wickets are also a continental best.
Zimbabwe's 272 is their highest ODI total against Australia at home, past the 246/9 at Bulawayo in 2003.
This is only the third time nine or more Zimbabwe batters have reached double figures in an ODI, after 10 against India at Rajkot in 2000 and nine against West Indies at Harare in 2001.
