 Dockers storm home to seal Grand Final berth in epic finish over Swans
 Fremantle is into the 2026 Toyota AFL Grand Final after beating Sydney
 By Emily Patterson 
 -->
 Shai Bolton celebrates a goal during the Preliminary Final between Sydney and Fremantle at the Sydney Cricket Ground on September 18, 2026. Picture: AFL Photos 
 THERE was no Wharfie Time at the SCG on Friday night, but Fremantle still produced an act of late-game magic, storming home from 28 points down to claim a thrilling 12-point win and secure just the second Grand Final appearance in the club's history.
 With the Dockers surging home on the back of five unanswered final-quarter goals, Mason Cox, reviving memories of his famous preliminary final heroics in Collingwood colours, clunked a towering mark and coolly slotted the set shot to edge Fremantle in front.
 SWANS V DOCKERS Full match details and stats 
 It completed a remarkable comeback, given the Swans had led by a game-high 28 points midway through the third term.
 When these teams met in round 18, Sydney led at half-time by 22 points before eventually losing by 38 - and history repeated itself as the hosts ran out of steam late, falling 10.11 (71) to 12.11 (83) in a severe test of their depth against the minor premiers.

 Continuing his outstanding run of form, Logan McDonald starred with a game-high three goals from his 14 disposals. Errol Gulden's trademark run and speed were also on full display, collecting 36 disposals and 766 metres gained.

 However, early in the fourth, consecutive goals from Bolton and Voss, the latter coming after a powerful fend-off, cut the deficit to just nine points as the Swans began to look tired.
 As the intensity escalated, Bolton snagged his second of the term to make it a one-point game, before Cox drilled a trademark preliminary final goal to snatch the lead for the first time since the five-minute mark of the opening term.
 Punching their ticket to the last Saturday in September, Fremantle advanced to a Grand Final clash with either Hawthorn or Brisbane.
 Dockers' key forwards go missing again as premiership hopes slip
 Fremantle's three key forwards have combined for 162 goals this season, but much like their qualifying final loss to Hawthorn a fortnight ago, the trio struggled to make an impact when it mattered most. The Dockers' talls were held in check in the first half, with Patrick Voss tallying just one disposal, Josh Treacy three and Jye Amiss four, while none registered a score. By the final siren, the trio had managed just two goals between them.
 McDonald's goal-spree delivers career milestone
 Logan McDonald's rich vein of form continued as he slotted another three majors against Fremantle, bringing his tally to 24 goals across his past seven outings. The key forward has now booted multiple goals in every match during that stretch. It marks a significant milestone for McDonald, who had never previously managed more than 16 goals across any six-game period in his career, underlining his emergence as a reliable focal point in Sydney's attack.
