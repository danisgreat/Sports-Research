 Dayne Zorko was important to the Lions getting the ball forward for what turned into the match-defining play. The veteran tries to make sense of what he just witnessed and how he felt when McCluggage lined up for the shot.
 double quotation mark 
 He had multiple opportunities throughout the game. So he lines up that. Always confident. He works extremely hard on his set shot goals. He probably rushed a little bit. I didn’t like that he rushed, could’ve take a little off the clock, but oh my god, can’t believe he did it again.
 [When the Lions lead was 21 points … ] We thought, oh, the game’s done. And then the Hawks kick the next four. Certainly didn’t think it was done, but they did get some more looks, that was concerning. So had to cut off a few leads and tighten a few things up in there. And we did. They didn’t get away from us. It kept it a margin that we could come back.
 The 30 second sign went up. So I thought he’d actually take his 30 and maybe be the hero after the siren. But he rushed through it. He’s a beautiful set shot. He’s a beautiful kick.
 That’s the footy you want to see. Oh, it’s the entertainment, right? We would love to be a little bit tighter in defence no doubt about it. But the game doesn’t allow that all the time. So look, we knew it was going to be a moment by moment thing tonight.

 Hugh McCluggage just kicked the match-winner to launch the Lions into a fourth consecutive grand final. The Lions midfielder is speaking to Fox Sports.
 double quotation mark 
 Unbelievable. I’m absolutely speechless. It was just such an arm wrestle there, back and forth, and the crowd was amazing. We had momentum in the third. They played really well in the fourth and we just came out on top.
 They got that run going and centre bounce as well. They really got some territory, they’re an amazing team. Like, the Wiz and and Gunners as well. They’re hard to stop if you give them good looks. I’m just super super proud of the boys for fighting back at the end.
 I was thinking I’ve kicked these. I warmed up and kicked a lot of them and I had a couple in the game as well. So just run straight, kick it and take the moment. Probably could have taken a bit more time off the clock now that I think about it
