 Toronto Blue Jays vs Texas Rangers &mdash; September 18, 2026
 Box Score for Blue Jays (1) vs Rangers (7) on September 18, 2026 | Baseball Almanac 
 ARLINGTON � Corey Seager capped a four-run second inning with an RBI double, and the Texas Rangers capitalized on wild pitching and defensive miscues to roll past the Toronto Blue Jays, 7-1, on Friday night at Globe Life Field.
 Texas broke the game open in the bottom of the second against Blue Jays starter Dylan Cease, who struggled with his command throughout a laborious outing. Cease issued back-to-back walks to Wyatt Langford and Jake Burger before Nicky Lopez singled to load the bases. With one out, Evan Carter hit a bouncer up the middle; shortstop Ernie Clement fielded the ball and attempted to force Burger at third, but his throw sailed wide into foul territory, allowing both Langford and Burger to score. Ezequiel Duran followed with a sacrifice fly to right field, and Seager laced an RBI double to deep right-center to push the Rangers ahead, 4-0.
 Cease issued a season-high six walks among the first 15 batters he faced, throwing 38 balls and 36 strikes before being lifted with two outs in the third inning. Five of the seven runs surrendered by Toronto were unearned, aided by 10 walks and three throwing errors, including two by third baseman Kazuma Okamoto.
 Texas starter Kumar Rocker protected the lead into the fifth inning, scattering three hits and two walks across 4&frac13; innings. Toronto generated its lone run in the fifth when Myles Straw lined an RBI double to left-center to score Josh Smith. Rocker exited after 71 pitches, yielding to Tyler Alexander, who escaped the threat after an overturned review confirmed a strikeout of Brett Bateman and pinch-hitter Davis Schneider flied out. The move to Schneider officially snapped Nathan Lukes�s 13-game hitting streak, the longest active streak in the majors, after Lukes went 0-for-2.
 The Rangers expanded their cushion in the bottom of the fifth on Burger�s sacrifice fly, tacked on another run in the sixth when Langford drew a bases-loaded walk, and finalized the scoring in the eighth on Burger's RBI double off Joe Mantiply.
 Trevor Williams dominated in relief to secure the victory, tossing four scoreless frames while striking out four to close out the contest.
