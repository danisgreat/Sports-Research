 Delaware defense does a number on Coastal Carolina in the second half to preserve a 22-14 win over the Chanticleers (AUDIO)
 By Chris Burgin In Coastal Carolina 
 Colton Hinton had three receptions for 42 yards with a touchdown on Saturday (Coastal Carolina Athletics) 
 Game Recap Courtesy of CCU Media Relations: 
 NEWARK, Del. –  Coastal Carolina held a seven-point lead during the second quarter, but Delaware scored the final 15 points to hand the Chanticleers a 22-14 setback Saturday afternoon at Delaware Stadium.
 Coastal struck first with 7:51 remaining in the opening quarter. Starting at its own 5-yard line, the offense needed just five plays to travel 95 yards. Deuce Bailey capped the drive with a 62-yard touchdown pass to Goldie Lawrence, giving the Chanticleers a 7-0 lead.
 After Delaware evened the score early in the second quarter, Coastal answered with a nine-play, 72-yard scoring drive. Bailey connected with Colton Hinton for a 27-yard touchdown to put the Chanticleers back in front, 14-7, with 8:19 remaining in the half.
 The Blue Hens responded in the closing seconds of the second quarter, scoring on a 2-yard run before converting a two-point attempt to take a 15-14 advantage into halftime.
 Neither team scored during the third quarter as Coastal&#8217;s defense kept the Chanticleers within one point. The Chants forced punts on Delaware&#8217;s first two possessions of the second half and held the Blue Hens to 93 rushing yards for the game.
 Delaware extended its lead following a turnover on a punt return midway through the fourth quarter. On the next play, Nick Minicucci connected with Sean Wilson for a 36-yard touchdown, pushing the margin to 22-14 with 8:19 remaining.
 Coastal responded by driving 62 yards to the Delaware 13-yard line, but the Blue Hens stopped the Chanticleers on fourth down with 4:07 remaining and ran out the clock.
 The Chanticleers (1-2) finished with 385 yards of total offense, including 250 through the air and 135 on the ground. Bailey completed 16-of-26 passes for 250 yards and two touchdowns without an interception while adding 42 rushing yards.
 Jevon Edwards led Coastal&#8217;s rushing attack with 49 yards, while Dominic Knicely added 44. Lawrence&#8217;s 62-yard touchdown reception led the receiving corps, while Clayton Coppock Jr. recorded 52 yards on two catches. Hinton finished with three receptions for 42 yards and a touchdown. 
 The Chanticleers return to Brooks Stadium on Thursday, Sept. 24, to host Liberty. Kickoff is scheduled for 7:30 p.m. ET on ESPN.
