**Analyst primary probability (ANALYST_PRIMARY_V1, no code).** Build one coherent event distribution by hand and take every p_card, rank and expected value from it. 1. **League anchor:** for the exact competition and season, record mean total, home edge and margin/total SDs from LEAGUE_PROFILES, BASE_RATES_REGISTER §7 or verified recent seasons, with source and n. 2. **Team strength:** from the verified season-to-date and L20 rows, compute each side's points (goals, runs) for and against per game relative to the league, shrink toward the league mean by sample size (PROBABILITY_TOOLKIT §4 constants where the league is listed; otherwise state the shrinkage weight) and opponent-adjust only where schedule strength is sourced. 3. **Centre:** expected margin = strength difference + home edge; expected total = league mean scaled by both sides' scoring and conceding ratios. 4. **Adjustments:** list each confirmed-availability, rest/travel, venue or weather effect with size, basis and source in the adjustment table; never count an effect already inside the form rates. 5. **Width:** start from the league benchmark SD; widen, never narrow without evidence, for early season, stale profiles or unconfirmed lineups, or price the low/base/high mixture (pricing basis below). 6. **Convert:** use the sport file's step-4 distribution family with the toolkit (§1 normal Φ table with half-point and key-number handling; §2 Poisson goals; §3 negative-binomial runs; §6 tennis Elo and serve chain; §7 cricket innings), writing each formula and intermediate value. 7. **Check** (toolkit §11): complementary rows sum to one; winner, handicap and total rows come from the same distribution; print TEAM_BASELINE_P (§4) and the same-line BASELINE_P, and explain the gap with the departure ledger (§8); a p_card above 0.85 needs a named, sourced mechanism and is never clipped. Label every p_card ANALYST_PRIMARY_V1 / UNCALIBRATED_ANALYST_PRIMARY. A row that cannot be supported gets a bounded range or UNCALIBRATED_QUALITATIVE / NO_FORECAST with the reason; never an invented number.

**Pricing basis (PR-03).** When an input is labelled unfitted, stale, early-season or unconfirmed, the primary distribution is the declared mixture of its low/base/high cases (default weights 0.25/0.50/0.25, frozen before pricing). Print each row's `p_base` beside p_card. Compute at the precision actually used and print ε. A gate within ε of 0.62, or within 2ε of the 0.04 lead, is `GATE_UNRESOLVED_PRECISION`; rows within 2ε are a `PRECISION_TIE` (SELECTION_RULES §2). Apply any key-number, push or tie adjustment to the normalised common distribution, never to one row. Print each ranked row's line distance from centre (units and z, or N/A) and the card's baseline coverage, with an enumerated reason for each missing BASELINE_P (SELECTION_RULES §5, §9).
