## Promotion gate

The 100 decisions / 30 cards, 25 RM-1 cards and 150 shadow games are review checkpoints, not proof thresholds. Inconclusive or unavailable evidence leaves the scope experimental. The executable gate is `tools/evidence_status.py`; the policy remains in `PERFORMANCE_ELIGIBILITY_POLICY.md`, `NUMERICAL_PROGRAM.md` and `H0_DATASET_CARD.md`.
