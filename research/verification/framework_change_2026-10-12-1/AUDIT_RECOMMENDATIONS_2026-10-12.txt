EXECUTIVE FINDING

The published prompt framework has useful safeguards, but further correction is
needed before its instructions can be followed consistently. The most urgent
published issues are wrong sport-source references, contradictory operating
instructions, an invalid tennis probability constraint, and underspecified
settlement revision handling.

There is also a separate, more urgent LOCAL-ONLY data problem. Unpublished Czech
hockey and English football archive builders invent missing measurements and
write them as ordinary historical data. One inspected football result is also
wrong against an official club record. These working files should not be admitted
to forecasting, profiles or settlement evidence until repaired. This finding does
not mean those new files have already been published to main.

This report contains 38 recommendations. It distinguishes confirmed defects,
preventive improvements and existing pending work. It does not claim that every
historical record in this large repository has been independently verified.

Acceptance: the counterexample grades match winner LOSS and -1.5 games WIN;
