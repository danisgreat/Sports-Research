# Source-route review

**Review date:** 14 October 2026, Australia/Sydney. **Method:** web search of primary/official publishers and targeted opens; no current game was requested. URLs below are routes or examples, not issued availability evidence. Player details from returned pages were not copied into cards or treated as current inputs. All source independence remains unknown unless an exact release's collection is established.

The protocol covers other competitions with organizer → both teams → medical/discipline → scorer/provider → local-language escalation. Those discovery paths are deliberately labelled D until exact game fields are read. This review does not certify universal league coverage, full medical disclosure, pregame publication timestamps or independent collection.
