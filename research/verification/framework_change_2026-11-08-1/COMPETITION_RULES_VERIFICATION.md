# Competition rules re-verification — 8 November 2026 (CR-2026.11.08-R1)

**Owner instruction, 8 November 2026:** "Re-verify the sports rules for each competition in each sport that has been covered through the game logs, and make the necessary changes to the files."

## 1. Scope and method

- **Inventory.** The competitions come from every canonical record in [GAME_LOG_STATUS_CURRENT](../../../GAME_LOG_STATUS_CURRENT.md) (P-001 to P-622), the card headings of the prediction logs and the active mini `20261022F6K3OJNO`. That is about 130 competitions in 11 sports.
- **What was checked.** Only the rules that change a card or its settlement:
  - game length;
  - whether a game can end level, and what happens then (overtime, extra time, penalties, shoot-outs, extra innings, super overs);
  - two-leg and series formats;
  - points systems and play-offs;
  - any rule or format change for the 2026 or 2026-27 season.
- **Sources.** Each fact was searched on the open web, preferring the field owner (league, federation, governing body) and dated reports, between 18:00 and 21:00 AEDT on 8 November 2026. No odds, previews or betting content were used; where a page also carried prices (an aggregator's standings page), only its format statement was read.
- **Limits.** The search tool returned a limited number of searches in this session, so the minor competitions were grouped and some are marked `NOT_RE_VERIFIED` with the reason.

Statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `VERIFIED` | Confirmed for the current or latest season |
| `CHANGED` | Differs from the file or the previous season; the file is corrected |
| `CONFLICT` | Sources disagree; confirm from the field owner on the card |
| `NOT_RE_VERIFIED` | No current statement found in this pass; rule G2 applies before the next card |
