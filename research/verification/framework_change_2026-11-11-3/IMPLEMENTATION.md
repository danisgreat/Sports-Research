## Additional data defect found and corrected

The original cricket importer included all_matches.csv alongside individual match files. Five aggregate files passed its old date filter, creating ten nonsensical innings and contaminating phase aggregates. Original files are preserved unchanged. The corrected importer accepts numeric per-match members only and checks that every row has that member's match ID. It replays the actual retained raw archives, verifying their hashes against original capture receipts, and writes a new cricket/v24.15 normalization (19,889 innings, unique competition/match/innings keys), excluded-member list and actual normalization receipt. It does not claim a fresh network capture. Source-recorded occasional extra legal balls remain unchanged rather than being guessed away.
