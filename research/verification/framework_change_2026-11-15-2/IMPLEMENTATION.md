2026-11-15T00:00:00+11:00. New event initialization from that time requires the audit
protocol at its pinned commit. The clock override in internal test functions is for
synthetic fixtures; live CLI commands use their actual coordinator clock. Do not
backdate an issue/package to evade a gate. Existing issued cards are not rewritten.

and byte identity; the settlement reviewer verifies the official sporting fact and
source access. Corrections are dated append-only records; an unbound RESULTS.csv
timing column cannot override immutable issue records.
