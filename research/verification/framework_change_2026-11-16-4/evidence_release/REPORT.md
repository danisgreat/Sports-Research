# Historical evidence and prospective admission

All **685 historical cards** are accounted for: 3150 contract rows, 3082 admitted, 1291 with stated probabilities and 1791 outcome-only. 68 are excluded with reasons in EXCLUSIONS.csv.

These records are used in CARD-TRAIN-v1. Their original probabilities, cohorts and source references remain intact. The copied historical study scores are outcome-exposed evidence, not a prospective verdict. Applying a new validator after a result does not establish that its rules or probabilities were frozen before that result. A missing probability, original request, native identity or publication timestamp is never manufactured.

Prospective advantage is decided separately from the complete registered request/seal sequence. No current-method eligibility is inferred from a historical card count. Recoverable historical custody can be audited as an explicitly labelled historical cohort; a later rule is never backdated.

FEATURES.csv preserves sport-specific definitions, raw values, sources, known-at times and missingness. It is a research-feature candidate store; fitted models remain shadow studies. The old cards' unsaved research is recorded as an explicit gap in HISTORICAL_CARD_AUDIT.csv, not filled from post-result pages.
