# PROSPECTIVE LINEUP, BENCH AND ACTIVE-ROSTER CONFIRMATION GATE — Rule `PRESTART-XI-BENCH-2026-10-11`

**Effective only for NEW research after this rule was requested; never apply retrospectively to overwrite a frozen prediction.** Source authority: the user's explicit instruction, stored locally pending approval for canonical repository rules. SPORT/SPORTS_ONLY and MARKET_BLIND remain mandatory.

1. **Basketball — hard two-team pre-tip gate:** Obtain and timestamp both OFFICIALLY CONFIRMED starting fives, all named active bench/reserve players, players marked inactive or unavailable, injuries and game-time decisions, suspensions and expected rotation/minutes from the league's exact-game box, approved official teams' team sheet or authenticated dated first-party posts. A projected depth chart, 'probable' lineup, roster web page or five-player graphic without available bench status does NOT pass. For other sports apply the equivalent: baseball starting nine/pitcher and active reserves/bullpen; soccer XI + bench; cricket confirmed XI; tennis both participants' actual ready-to-play status (no bench); etc.
2. **Before the actual start:** Verify the source timestamp and exact fixture key/venue, first-choice official match center and team source, ideally again in the 60–15 minute pre-tip window and at the last opportunity before start; record `CONFIRMED_STARTERS`, `CONFIRMED_ACTIVE_BENCH`, `CONFIRMED_INACTIVES`, `LAST_RECHECK`, `ACTUAL_TIP_STATUS` and URLs verbatim in A3/A8. Do NOT backfill the evidence using post-tip boxscore identification while describing the call as prospective.
3. **Gate:** If any starter or bench member's game availability is unresolved before tip, set `PRESTART_LINEUP_BENCH_GATE: FAIL — UNVERIFIED` and `FORECAST_STATUS: HOLD_UNISSUED`; no final ranked/probability card, no qualified Rank-1, no purported confirmed starters, no performance eligibility. Continue only evidence collection until confirmed. If no confirmation materialises before tip, document `NO_QUALIFIED_PREGAME_CARD`; research afterwards is separate `LATE_START_UNVERIFIED`, not a substitute.
4. **Post-confirmation:** Fit opponent-adjusted expected minutes, usage and rotation; rerun the entire joint score distribution (pace × PPP, score covariance, OT, margins, handicap, totals) and recompute every line and winner from that ONE object. Name all changes, +/- point adjustment magnitudes, supporting data and uncertainty sensitivity. Never infer minutes from 'starting' alone; preserve role and bench-depth tail scenarios.
5. **Lineup/bench changes before start:** Re-freeze and revise under a dated A8 addendum; recheck all ranked rows and dependencies. If a change is discovered after the actual start, keep original frozen card, add A8 with `LATE_INFORMATION`, never retroactively certify or back-date, and grade the original card using original issued propositions only.
6. **Proof/fail-close:** Independent timestamped owner-sourced roster/box evidence is required; if official lists conflict, leave `DISPUTED` and HOLD; after a no-game or abandonment check the rules apply as written. A final box score can audit which players appeared, but does not prove lineup confirmation was available before tip-off.

**Archive/process distinction:** The existing P-556–P-579 cards were issued under their then-active procedures; they remain immutable and are assessed for missing bench verification as a process deficiency without relabelling their original timing.
