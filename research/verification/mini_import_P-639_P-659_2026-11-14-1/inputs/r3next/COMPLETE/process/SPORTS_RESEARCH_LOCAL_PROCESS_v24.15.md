## 3 — Evidence and probability release

1. First verify both teams' confirmed availability: expected/confirmed starters, playing bench, absences, injury mechanisms, suspensions and late roster changes. **Projected is not confirmed.** For hockey, actual starting goalies; for cricket, toss and innings; for tennis, court and best-of; and indoor/outdoor match conditions as relevant.
2. Reconstruct exact-competition team/player history as of the evidence cutoff: season, L5/L10/L15/L20 with actual sample size `n`, opponents, scores, dates, home/away, exclusions, scheduling/rest and same-season matchup. Preserve source URL/class, publication/retrieval times, direct field values and original collector identity.
3. Treat uncorroborated, conflicting, late or unreachable facts as named gaps.
