Young Suns turn up the heat against winless Saints despite gun's return to form
St Kilda held a half-time lead on the back of four goals from Jesse Tawhaio-Wardlaw, but then Gold Coast lifted three levels
By
Howard Kimber
Lily Quigley and Havana Harris during round seven between Gold Coast and St Kilda at People First Stadium, September 27, 2026. Picture: AFL Photos
GOLD Coast has charged into the top four with a 10.9 (69) to 6.3 (39) win against St Kilda at People First Stadium on Sunday.
The young Suns struggled early as Jesse Tawhiao-Wardlaw single-handedly kept the Saints in the contest early, but eight second-half goals to one saw Gold Coast steam home to the 30-point victory.
SUNS v SAINTS
Full match coverage and stats
Tawhiao-Wardlaw's return to form may give St Kilda fans something to smile about if only for a while, however they will soon be sobered by the fact their side sits winless on the bottom of the ladder ofter seven rounds.
A close first quarter turned the Saints' way in the second as they held a 15-point lead at the long break, but everything reversed when play resumed, as momentum, and the football, started heading towards the Suns' end of the ground.
Charlie Rowbottom's sensational season continued at full stride as she racked up 38 disposals and nine clearances.
