## Source roles and scope

ESPN monthly NBA scoreboards at `site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/nba/scoreboard?dates=YYYYMM&limit=1000` provide the primary population. The yearly query with limit 5000 silently returned only 25 rows and is not an input. Date ranges returned HTTP 400. Month rows are checked against Basketball-Reference ending-year season/month schedules, team game logs and the additionally accessible NBA league archive. The 86 month URLs cover September 2019 through October 2026; rows outside the declared season/window are explicitly excluded. Daily queries investigate missing matches. Four apparent missing regular-season fixtures were source-clock/date disagreements, not recovered new ESPN games. Unique adjacent-date matches require exact season, both teams, orientation and final scores; `IDENTITY_CLOCK_REVIEWS.csv` records their evidence without editing source clocks.

ESPN summaries supply 2,271 retained full team-box projections, including 602 initial special-stage requests and a targeted 1,677-request follow-up for missing component corroboration (eight of the latter returned 404). A later actual summary may update a scoreboard observation; `OBSERVATION_REVISIONS.csv` records observed status changes and SOURCE_VALUE_REVISIONS.csv records later same-provider count observations against the earlier captured scoreboard. Prior projection conflicts remain separately retained; current conflicts are not overwritten by a different publisher. No retrieval is backdated. The ESPN scrimmage-results article (story 29524639) provides 33 dated matchup/final pairs. Its winner-first ordering is oriented using the official NBA schedule; its rows do not claim the article published UTC tipoff or arena fields, which remain blank. Article prose, roster projections and market content are not included.

Basketball-Reference schedules are season-ending years 2020–2027; overlapping index/month copies are collapsed by exact native box-score key after final-score equality checks. The 210 team-season game-log pages cover 30 teams and seven completed seasons, including their available postseason tables. Their own team boxes supply source counts; opponent mirrors are not counted again. It does not cover the whole preseason or special-event population. Source-reported final scores and published listed Eastern start times are preserved; Eastern-to-UTC conversion uses America/New_York historical daylight saving. Adjacent provider dates do not manufacture another game.

The normal public NBA `data.nba.com/data/10s/v2015/json/mobile_teams/nba/<starting-year>/league/00_full_schedule.json` route supplies actual published 10-digit league IDs for seven accessible seasons. The same archive's `scores/gamedetail/<id>_gamedetail.json` supplies 9,329 detailed boxes from 9,332 requested IDs; three 2022 Rising Stars detail objects returned 403. The 2026–27 archive, CDN and other failed probes have actual HTTP/error receipts; there is no proxy/header/captcha evasion. Schedule entries may be stale or provisional. Detail observations supersede schedule status only where a real captured detail exists, retaining their actual source values/time. Archive-only or conflicting fixtures remain unverified, not an independently established fixture denominator. Some current-season/postseason native-ID mappings remain unavailable. A named NBA phase cannot cure an uncorroborated source fixture.

## Identity, stages and clocks

`event_key` is an evidence join key, normally ESPN:NBA:<actual ESPN ID>, with NBA:<actual league ID> for article/archive cases and BREF:NBA:<actual box key> where unmatched. It is not a P-ID. `source_event_id` retains the source-native identity; ARTICLE:29524639:NBA:<id> labels a per-game observation in one article and is never represented as an ESPN game ID. One row per game per source is checked. Missing official IDs stay blank and open, never generated from dates or row numbers.

TEAM_ALIASES.csv lists only explicit reviewed abbreviation mappings, including BRK/BKN, CHO/CHA, PHO/PHX, international preseason clubs and All-Star teams. ESPN team IDs 130136 (Ulm) and 126120 (Melbourne Pnx) lack abbreviations and are explicitly mapped to the observed official ULM and SEM codes. The dates, matchup, orientation and scores support those joins. Provider source values remain in the raw captures.

ESPN event type/notes distinguish REGULAR, PLAY_IN, PLAYOFFS (NBA Finals flag), NBA_CUP_CHAMPIONSHIP, PRESEASON, RISING_STARS and ALL_STAR. Cup quarterfinal/semifinal/group notes remain REGULAR. Do not trust season-type 2 alone: ESPN puts special All-Star events there. Matched secondary rows reference this classification and do not pretend to independently confirm it. Unmatched NBA rows retain their native phase classification; ALL_STAR_RELATED does not claim an independently confirmed exact Rising Stars subtype. Unknown subtype/identity remains unsuitable for first fit. All nonregular stages are excluded from the first NBA candidate mask.

Source scheduled UTC clocks remain unaltered. CANONICAL_EVENTS chooses an exactly matching two-source schedule when available, otherwise labels its primary/single-source schedule as uncorroborated. A date-matched final does not establish actual first play. Clock differences remain open requirements, even when identity/final scores agree. Actual start is always NOT_VERIFIED. The Orlando venue rule requested by the owner normalizes 2019–20 bubble venues to neutral; the contradictory original ESPN flag remains in neutral_flag_reported. Other unknown neutral flags are not filled from zero attendance or home designation.

## Scores, periods and statistic definitions

Non-final rows retain their status and blank outcome scores; displayed live/scheduled zeros are not final labels. Regulation score is derived only from actually observed first-four period points in a standard-scoring game whose complete reported periods sum to the source final. Overtime is the observed period count beyond four, not inferred from a tie. Missing periods stay absent. Target-score/All-Star/Rising Stars rows have no 48-minute regulation score. Scrimmage rule lengths differ and remain PRESEASON with a separate endpoint. No shootout/extra-time-like branch is invented for basketball.

Long tables use the existing 14-column TEAM_GAME_STATS export schema, but review_status is SOURCE_FACT_NOT_TRAINING_ADMITTED. No ingestion is performed. The native_event_id column holds the evidence join key, with actual provider aliases in EVENT_ALIASES; a later authorised export must apply its declared single-event identity mapping and population filter.

Core mappings: fg/fgm and fieldGoalsMade → FGM; fga/fieldGoalsAttempted → FGA; fg3/tpm/threePointFieldGoalsMade → 3PM; fg3a/tpa/threePointFieldGoalsAttempted → 3PA; ft/ftm and freeThrowsMade → FTM; fta/freeThrowsAttempted → FTA; orb/oreb/offensiveRebounds → ORB. REB, DRB, AST, STL, BLK, PF and remaining numeric team-box components are retained. BRef's TOV is total team turnovers; NBA archive tov is PLAYER_TOV and tmtov is TEAM_TOV, and TOV explicitly equals their observed sum. ESPN turnovers is PLAYER_TOV, teamTurnovers is TEAM_TOV and totalTurnovers is TOV. No missing team turnover component is assumed zero. Negative count sentinels (including ESPN TEAM_TOV=-1) are quarantined and omitted from usable count tables, never coerced to zero. NBA/BRef/ESPN provider rates and other unmapped keys keep provider prefixes; BRef published percentages are fractions, ESPN's percentages are provider scale. No pace, offensive rating, defensive rating or fitted effect is created. STAT_DICTIONARY records units/scope; no attempts-free provider rate is promoted to a model feature.

Every pair of available core count observations is compared; FIELD_COMPARISONS retains agreeing and disagreeing values. Conflicting counts and intrasource inconsistent points remain in QUARANTINE. Score disagreement quarantines the event, never overwrites another source. The evidence-side future candidate mask excludes nonregular, nonfinal, uncorroborated and conflicting facts, and requires both sides' possession components; it is not a guarantee of completed corroboration for every other field. BOX_ARITHMETIC_ISSUES, PERIOD_COMPARISONS, FIELD_CORROBORATION and REQUIREMENT_CLOSURE preserve remaining limitations. The verifier applies explicit box/period quality exclusions to the derived candidate mask only and records QUALITY_MASK_EXCLUSIONS; it never changes source observations. A future regulation-target study must additionally require corroborated regulation labels. No result disagreement is resolved by majority voting.

## Timing and model boundary

known_at equals retrieved_at on every source fact/stat. published_at remains blank where an exact value was not collected; a game date, article byline or final box is not backfilled pregame availability. These results can support future forecasts once separately reviewed/admitted. The current strict historical feature builder cannot reconstruct earlier-season context from captures first known today. No historical feature admission, pregame lineup, prediction bundle, expert loss, model fit, calibration, prospective improvement or RL control policy is claimed. The reusable framework describes the required next-stage timing choice and later owner-authorised training gates.
